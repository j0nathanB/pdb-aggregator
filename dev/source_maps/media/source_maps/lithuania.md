# Source Intelligence Map: Lithuania

## Media Landscape Summary

Lithuania's media has undergone rapid digital transformation, with several print dailies closing or converting to weeklies. The landscape is now dominated by online portals — Delfi.lt (owned by Estonian Ekspress Grupp, which also acquired wire service ELTA in 2022) and 15min.lt — alongside the public broadcaster LRT, which has emerged as a respected source for defense and security reporting. The remaining major daily, Lietuvos rytas, publishes both in print and online. Lithuania has a small but impactful investigative sector led by Siena (the country's only dedicated investigative journalism center) and Laisves TV, which together triggered a major political crisis in 2025-2026 with their investigations into Prime Minister Paluckas. The business press centers on Verslo zinios (80% owned by Swedish Bonnier). There is no significant domestic Russian-language press.

## Notable Exclusions

- **Alfa.lt**: News portal; lower readership and largely duplicates Delfi/15min coverage.
- **Vakaro zinios**: Conservative tabloid; limited foreign/security-policy content.
- **Kauno diena**: Regional daily (Kaunas); rarely breaks national-level stories ahead of LRT or Delfi.
- **Lietuvos zinios**: Centrist daily; ceased print publication and has limited digital footprint.

## Coverage Gap Assessment

The set provides strong coverage with particular depth in security/defense (LRT, VSD annual reports, Siena investigations). The main gap is **defense-industrial and procurement reporting** — Lithuania is rapidly scaling military purchases (tanks, IFVs) but lacks a specialized defense-trade publication; this is partially covered by LRT and kam.lt press releases. A secondary gap is **systematic monitoring of Lithuanian diaspora media**, which can signal domestic-constraint dynamics through emigrant political engagement but falls outside the curated set.
