# Source Intelligence Map: Finland

## Media Landscape Summary

Finland's media ecosystem is anchored by the public broadcaster Yle (the most trusted news source in Finland), the dominant national daily Helsingin Sanomat, and two mass-market evening tabloids (Ilta-Sanomat and Iltalehti) that together reach over half the adult population. The business press is concentrated in Kauppalehti and Talouselama, both owned by Alma Media. Swedish-language media serve the constitutionally protected 5.2% Fenno-Swedish minority through Hufvudstadsbladet and Svenska Yle. Finland has the world's highest rate of paying news subscribers (70% of adults in 2025), and most outlets operate paywalled digital editions alongside declining print runs. Investigative and slow-journalism outlets like Long Play and the new subscriber-funded startup Uusi Juttu represent a growing niche.

## Notable Exclusions

- **MTV Uutiset** (mtv.fi): Commercial TV news; duplicates Yle's broadcast coverage without adding analytical depth for OSINT purposes.
- **Keskisuomalainen** and regional dailies: Important for domestic politics but rarely break national-level foreign/security stories ahead of HS or Yle.
- **Uusi Juttu** (uusijuttu.fi): Promising new subscriber-funded startup (launched January 2025, ~16,000 subscribers) but too young to assess reliability for an operational pipeline.
- **Nykypäivä**: Kokoomus party paper; redundant with Verkkouutiset for party-line monitoring.

## Coverage Gap Assessment

The recommended set provides strong coverage of all five domains. The main gap is **real-time military-operational reporting** — Finland has no dedicated open-source defense-intelligence publication comparable to Jane's or Defense News; Maanpuolustus-lehti is quarterly and establishment-oriented. A secondary gap exists in **Russian-language monitoring of Finnish policy debates**, as Finland lacks a significant domestic Russian-language press. Analysts should supplement with FIIA's English-language output for international-comparative framing.
