## Key Gaps — Cross-Pass

### Systemic Failures (Both Passes)

1. **Citation system entirely absent** — The single most consequential gap. Zero Markdown footnotes ([^N]) appear in either pass. No Sources section exists in either pass. The rubric requires 15–25 citations per pass for substantive claims, statistics, dates, and institutional details. Every section in both passes fails asks 17–20 of the cross-cutting rubric on this basis alone. This affects credibility of all other content.

2. **STRUCTURAL CLAIMS blocks entirely absent** — Cross-cutting ask 9 requires each section to conclude with a numbered, discrete, falsifiable structural claims block. Not one section in either pass complies. This is a complete systemic miss across all 13 evaluated sections.

3. **Confidence markers entirely absent** — Cross-cutting ask 5 requires [HIGH CONFIDENCE], [MODERATE CONFIDENCE], or [ASSESSED] where evidence is thin or contested. Neither pass uses these markers anywhere. Behavioral inferences about Erdoğan (term extension motivations), contested historical death tolls, and uncertain institutional assessments are asserted without epistemic qualification.

### Content Gaps (Pass 1)

4. **Fisheries completely absent** (Section 2, ask 9) — No analysis of Aegean or Black Sea fisheries as political friction points despite clear relevance to Greece-Turkey tensions.

5. **Informal economy absent** (Section 6, ask 7) — Turkey's informal sector (estimated 26–32% of GDP by various sources) is not estimated, characterized, or analyzed for its political implications (clientelism, diminished tax base, labor market distortions).

6. **Land registration and titling absent** (Section 3, ask 10) — Property systems as institutional inheritance not addressed; agrarian politics and property disputes absent.

7. **Renewable energy supply chain dependencies absent** (Section 7, ask 12) — Turkey's dependence on Chinese solar manufacturing and critical mineral supply chains for its rapid solar/wind buildout not addressed.

8. **Electricity grid interconnections absent** (Section 7, ask 11) — Turkey's grid dependencies on neighboring states not analyzed.

9. **Pension and aging fiscal sustainability absent** (Section 5, asks 3–4) — Despite tracking demographic transition thoroughly, the fiscal consequences (pension sustainability, healthcare costs, intergenerational transfers) are not developed.

10. **IMF relationship absent** (Section 6, ask 13) — Turkey's post-2008 non-IMF status and the implications of operating without a program are not addressed.

### Content Gaps (Pass 3 — Present Sections)

11. **Pipeline operational instructions absent** (Section 17, ask 6) — The "When the pipeline encounters..." analytical instruction format required by the rubric is completely absent from Section 17.

12. **US posture classification framework absent** (Section 14, ask 7) — The rubric's specific categorization of each US dependency dimension as withdrawal/hostility/indifference is not applied.

13. **Alignment falsification threshold absent** (Section 14, ask 14) — No observable threshold is provided for falsifying the "strategic promiscuity" multi-vector characterization.

14. **Behavioral inference labeling absent** (Section 15, ask 10) — Inferences about Erdoğan's motivations (term extension, risk appetite) are stated as conclusions rather than labeled [ASSESSED] with supporting episodes.

15. **Grievance constraint graduation absent** (Section 16, asks 3–4) — Narratives described but not formally graded on regime-threatening vs. manageable scale; no instances of leaders being punished for defying narratives.

16. **Section 13 forward-looking crisis infrastructure absent** (ask 6) — The operationally significant assessment of who controls dissent channels and mobilization speed for a 6–12 month crisis window is missing.
