## Overall Key Findings

### Strengths

1. **Factual density is exceptional.** All three passes demonstrate superior command of Italian political, economic, demographic, and security data — specific figures, dates, legislation, and actors are consistently cited throughout.

2. **Causal reasoning is present and often strong.** The vincolo esterno mechanism (Pass 1), the mafia COVID welfare offensive (Pass 2), the coalition reform-engineering structure (Pass 3), and the demographics-immigration contradiction (Pass 3) are all explained with causal specificity.

3. **Section 0 (Key Actors)** is the highest-performing section (90%), functioning well as a glossary. Sections 1, 6, 8, and 17 also perform above average.

4. **Pass 1 is the strongest pass** at 63.8% addressed, with Pass 2 solid on content depth (53.9%) and Pass 3 weakest structurally despite strong analytical content.

### Systemic Failures

1. **Complete absence of citations** across all three passes (CC-17, CC-18, CC-19, CC-20). This is the single most consequential structural failure — the rubric requires 15-25 Markdown footnotes per pass with a Sources section. Zero compliance across 286 applicable asks represents a fundamental departure from the specification.

2. **Complete absence of STRUCTURAL CLAIMS blocks** (CC-9). The rubric requires every section to conclude with numbered, falsifiable structural claims. This discipline is entirely absent — 21 sections, 0 STRUCTURAL CLAIMS blocks.

3. **Complete absence of confidence markers** (CC-5). [HIGH CONFIDENCE], [MODERATE CONFIDENCE], and [ASSESSED] are specified as required tools for epistemic transparency. Zero usage in any pass.

4. **Analytical frame not applied** (CC-10, CC-11). The four analytical questions (Exposure, Adaptation, Constraint, Russia axis) and the baseline framing of US retrenchment as the structural operating environment are absent from all passes.

5. **Pass 3 format compliance deteriorates sharply.** Sections 18, 19, and 20 — which have the most precisely specified formats in the rubric — have the lowest compliance scores (28.6%, 25.0%, 20.0% respectively). Content quality is high but structural formatting requirements are almost entirely unmet.
