## Content Quality Assessment

Despite poor rubric compliance, the substantive content quality is high. The dossier's strength lies in:

- **Exceptional specificity**: dates, figures, vote counts, monetary values, percentages consistently present across all passes
- **Strong causal reasoning**: the mechanism-chains (occupation → threat perception → 4.91% GDP spending; ABLV collapse → MONEYVAL compliance → sanctions enforcement capacity) are analytically coherent
- **Current information**: data current to early 2026; Rail Baltica cost overruns, Istanbul Convention crisis, BRELL desynchronization all current
- **Self-containment**: all abbreviations and actors introduced; would serve a consuming LLM without prior Latvia knowledge

The rubric compliance failures are primarily **formal** (missing citation apparatus, structural claims blocks, confidence markers, and four entire sections in Pass 3) rather than **substantive** (the factual claims and analytical frameworks are generally sound).
