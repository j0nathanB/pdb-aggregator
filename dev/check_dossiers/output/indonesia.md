## Systemic Issues (Appear Across Multiple Passes)

### 1. Citation failure in Passes 2 and 3 — CRITICAL

Pass 1 has 27 well-sourced footnotes. Passes 2 and 3 have zero. This represents a structural failure in the production pipeline for the later passes — not a content quality issue (the analytical quality of Passes 2 and 3 is high) but a complete omission of sourcing infrastructure. Every statistic, treaty date, survey result, and institutional claim in Passes 2 and 3 is unsourced. This is the single highest-priority gap across the entire dossier.

### 2. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS blocks absent from Passes 2 and 3 — CRITICAL

Pass 1 correctly concludes every section (0–7) with a numbered STRUCTURAL CLAIMS block containing falsifiable assertions. Passes 2 and 3 omit this structure entirely. Pass 2 substitutes a five-point narrative conclusion; Pass 3 has no equivalent. This means 13 of 21 sections (Sections 8–20) lack the discrete, numbered structural claims that allow the pipeline to track, test, and update beliefs against incoming events.

### 3. Confidence markers absent from Passes 2 and 3 — HIGH

Pass 1 uses [HIGH CONFIDENCE], [MODERATE CONFIDENCE], and [ASSESSED] throughout. Passes 2 and 3 contain no confidence markers in sections 8–17. Section 18 of Pass 3 uses percentage confidence levels instead of the specified bracket notation. This prevents the pipeline from calibrating analytical reliability — particularly problematic in contested areas like TNI military loyalty, Prabowo's inner-circle decision-making, and state-criminal integration evidence.

### 4. Section 19 (Watch Indicators) format non-compliance — MODERATE

The rubric specifies a five-field format: Indicator, Relates to, Observation channel, Trigger threshold, Structural implication. The delivered format omits "Relates to" (section cross-reference) and "Structural implication" as distinct labeled fields, reducing the pipeline's ability to connect incoming observations to specific structural claims.

### 5. Section 17 (Cross-Facet Intersections) missing operational instruction format — MODERATE

All six intersections are analytically strong but substitute bolded synthesis sentences for the rubric-required "When the pipeline encounters [X], it should [Y] because [Z]" format. This reduces the pipeline operationalizability of the intersection analysis.

### 6. Section 20 source guidance not in conditional rule format — MODERATE

The rubric specifies a conditional format (IF outlet / topic domain → THEN interpretation adjustment / BECAUSE basis). The delivered format provides media tier rankings with calibration notes, which is analytically equivalent but not machine-processable in the same structured way.
