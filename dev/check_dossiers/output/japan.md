## Ranked Gap List (By Impact)

### Tier 1 — Systemic Failures (All Three Passes)

1. **STRUCTURAL CLAIMS blocks absent from all 21 sections.** This is the primary deliverable format for pipeline consumption. Every section should end with numbered, discrete, falsifiable assertions. Currently none do.

2. **Zero citations across all three passes.** No Markdown footnotes ([^N]), no ## Sources section, no minimum 15-25 citations per pass. The dossier makes extensive quantitative and institutional claims (¥600 million slush funds, 357/365 CCG incursions, 99.8% conviction rate) without any source attribution. This is the most significant quality gap.

3. **Confidence markers ([HIGH CONFIDENCE] / [MODERATE CONFIDENCE] / [ASSESSED]) absent.** Pass 3 Section 18 partially compensates with percentage confidence figures, but the required format with evidential basis statements is never used. Thin or contested evidence claims are not distinguished from well-supported ones.

### Tier 2 — Section-Level Structural Failures

4. **Section 19 format non-compliance.** The Watch Indicators section fails 5 of 8 asks. Required: Leading/Confirming categorisation, section cross-references, five-field format (Indicator / Relates to / Observation channel / Trigger threshold / Structural implication). Current: unnumbered prose paragraphs with embedded trigger thresholds only.

5. **Section 17 missing pipeline instruction format.** Five strong intersections are identified but analytical implications are stated as observations rather than operational instructions using the required "When the pipeline encounters..." format.

6. **Section 20 source guidance not in conditional rule format.** Tier ratings and orientations are useful, but not equivalent to conditional interpretation rules (outlet × topic domain → specific adjustment × basis).

### Tier 3 — Section Content Gaps

7. **Section 0 incomplete:** No judiciary entries (Supreme Court, Cabinet Legislation Bureau); no institutionally-stable vs. regime-specific flags; no explicit external actors category entry.

8. **Section 14 missing pivot analysis:** No structured feasibility tests for pivot options; no diaspora/ethnic alignment pressure analysis; no explicit falsification threshold for current alignment characterization.

9. **Section 15 behavioral inference calibration:** No [ASSESSED] markers distinguishing institutional observables from behavioral inferences; succession scenarios not developed; personal factors (health, financial exposure) not addressed.

10. **Section 10 stress episode analysis:** Neither the Fukushima 2011 nor Hyogo 2024 election episodes are developed with the required full timeline + platforms targeted + alternative channels + ultimate effect analysis.

11. **Section 4 cleavage analysis:** Five of ten asks partially addressed — specifically institutional channels of cleavage activation, cross-cleavage mapping, and systematic assessment of whether management mechanisms are functioning vs. under strain.

12. **Section 20 false equivalence guidance missing:** No domains identified where "both sides" framing obscures power asymmetry in Japan coverage.
