## Key Gaps by Priority

### CRITICAL (applies to entire dossier)

1. **No confidence markers anywhere** — [HIGH CONFIDENCE], [MODERATE CONFIDENCE], [ASSESSED] are entirely absent across all three pass evaluations. This is the most pervasive cross-cutting failure.

2. **No STRUCTURAL CLAIMS blocks in Pass 2 embedded material or Pass 3** — Sections 0–7 have correctly formatted STRUCTURAL CLAIMS; the embedded Sections 8–12 and all of Sections 13–20 do not. STRUCTURAL CLAIMS blocks are a core structural requirement of the rubric.

3. **Pass 3 has zero citations** — No footnotes, no [^N] references, and no ## Sources section. Every substantive factual claim in Sections 13–20 is unsourced. This is a structural failure of all four citation-related cross-cutting asks (17–19–20).

4. **No dedicated ## Sources section in any pass** — Even Pass 1 (which does have footnotes) places footnote definitions inline at the document end rather than under a "## Sources" header as required.

### MAJOR (structural non-compliance in specific sections)

5. **Section 17 (Cross-Facet Intersection Analysis) is entirely absent** — The merged 17–20 section contains no labeled cross-facet intersections, no mechanism analysis, no "When the pipeline encounters..." operational instructions. All 8 asks for Section 17 are NOT ADDRESSED.

6. **Section 18 (Key Analytical Judgments) effectively absent** — Three "structural tensions" in the conclusion do not meet any of the 7 asks: not 5–7 numbered judgments, not formatted with structural basis / falsification conditions / confidence levels.

7. **Section 19 (Watch Indicators) minimally addressed** — Eight indicators listed but lacking observation channels, Leading/Confirming organization, signal-vs-noise guidance, section cross-references, or the specified format.

8. **Section 20 (Pipeline Integration Notes) substantially absent** — No conditional source interpretation rules, no recency bias warnings, no false equivalence guidance; bilateral analysis from Section 14 is not formatted as pipeline notes.

### MODERATE (section-specific gaps)

9. **Section 2**: EEZ claims not explicitly mapped; fisheries politics absent; no cross-reference to Section 7.

10. **Section 3**: Land registration/titling system analysis entirely absent.

11. **Section 5**: Pension sustainability and intergenerational conflict mechanisms not modeled.

12. **Section 6**: Informal economy analysis absent; dollar-denominated debt vulnerability not specifically analyzed.

13. **Section 8**: Historical climate-governance cycle analysis absent; transboundary environmental framework underdeveloped; climate-economic conflict not analyzed.

14. **Section 9**: Structural relevance test (five criteria) not explicitly applied at the top of the section.

15. **Section 10**: Non-Telegram digital platforms not analyzed; open-source monitorability not assessed.

- **Exceptional factual density and specificity** — Specific vote percentages, casualty estimates, dollar figures, dates, and treaty names throughout; exemplary for asks 14 and 1.
- **Sections 1, 4, 11** achieve full marks — Formation Trauma, Cleavage Structure, and International Commitments are the strongest individual sections.

- **Self-contained references** — No prior expertise assumed; all institutions, events, and actors are explained in-line.
- **Wartime specificity** — The Cardboard Revolution, Yermak-Budanov transition, Trump-Zelensky Oval Office confrontation, and Energoatom scandal are covered with enough detail to be analytically useful.
