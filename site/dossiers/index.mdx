---

Country dossiers are the analytical foundation of the Middle Powers Monitor. Each dossier maps the structural landscape of a country — its key actors and institutions, power dynamics, economic dependencies, information environment, and known blind spots. They are the context layer that informs how weekly signals are interpreted.

Dossiers are updated periodically as structural conditions shift. They are not reactive to weekly events but rather provide the stable backdrop against which events are assessed.
