### Canada scraps EV mandate, introduces new incentives program

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney announced Thursday that Canada is scrapping its electric vehicle sales mandate and replacing it with stricter emissions standards and a new $2.3 billion incentive program. The government said it will eliminate the requirement that 20% of vehicles sold in 2026 be emissions-free, instead introducing tailpipe regulations aimed at achieving 75% EV sales by 2035 and 90% by 2040. The new program offers up to $5,000 incentives for EVs under $50,000 made in countries with Canadian free trade agreements, with no price cap for Canadian-made vehicles. Carney's office said the changes will "rationalize emission reduction policies" while avoiding "undue burden on the Canadian industry."
