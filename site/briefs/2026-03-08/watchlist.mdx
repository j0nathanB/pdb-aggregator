## Items on Watch

Iran's crisis threatens to jump its current borders. Egypt, Jordan, Pakistan, and Kazakhstan have stayed out so far, but military incidents, refugee flows, or economic shocks hitting any of these countries could turn a regional crisis into Middle East-wide chaos.

In East Asia, alliance coordination is creating new tensions. As Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan deepen security ties and share technology, China may accelerate its own military preparations in response. Washington is simultaneously pushing Japan, South Korea, and Australia to take on defense costs and capabilities beyond what NATO members currently bear. Either Chinese pushback or American pressure could fracture alliance unity or drive partners toward greater defense independence.
