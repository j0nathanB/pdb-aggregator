### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/gb.png" alt="United Kingdom" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />United Kingdom

The United Kingdom actively implements its bilateral web strategy through the first China leadership visit in 8 years and renewed EU defense engagement, confirming diversification away from US alliance dependence amid continued Trump friction. Domestic regime fragmentation accelerates with high-profile Conservative defections to Reform UK (now 8 MPs), explicit Conservative abandonment of centrism, and escalating Labour factional warfare including blocking potential leadership rivals. King Charles's unprecedented political documentary breaks constitutional neutrality convention, adding monarchical intervention to regime stability pressures.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** Prime Minister Starmer visited China January 27-30, the first UK leader to do so since 2018, announcing visa-free travel agreements and pushing for stronger trade ties. Trump criticized the engagement, calling UK-China business 'very dangerous', demonstrating continued US-UK friction over third-country relationships. *([BBC](https://bbc.com); [Reuters](https://reuters.com); [The Guardian](https://theguardian.com))*
- **Diplomatic:** Speaking from China, Starmer expressed desire to join the EU's €150 billion SAFE defense fund after previous negotiations failed, seeking to 'get closer' to the EU on defense, security, energy and trade cooperation. *([Bloomberg](https://bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-01/starmer-renews-uk-bid-to-join-eu-defense-fund-after-rejection); [POLITICO](https://politico.eu/article/uk-keir-starmer-aims-to-revive-defense-talks-with-eu))*
- **Domestic:** Labour's National Executive Committee voted to block Greater Manchester Mayor Andy Burnham from standing in the Gorton and Denton by-election. Critics accused Starmer of preventing a potential leadership challenger, triggering internal party turmoil with union criticism and widespread MP anger. *([The Guardian](https://theguardian.com/politics/2026/jan/25/andy-burnham-blocked-from-byelection-race-by-labour-ruling-committee); [Reuters](https://reuters.com); [Bloomberg](https://bloomberg.com))*
- **Domestic:** Former Home Secretary Suella Braverman joined Reform UK, becoming the latest Conservative MP to defect. Braverman accused her former party of lying to voters over immigration and delivered scathing critique at Reform rally, bringing Reform to 8 MPs. *([Reuters](https://reuters.com); [The Guardian](https://theguardian.com/politics/2026/jan/26/bravermans-predictable-defection-is-farages-biggest-political-gamble-yet); [Sky News](https://news.sky.com))*
- **Domestic:** Conservative leader declared centrist ideas are 'no longer wanted' in the party and told one-nation Tories who disagree with her rightward direction to 'get out of the way.' Explicitly rejected approach of Andy Street and Ruth Davidson's new centrist Conservative movement. *([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/jan/28/centrist-ideas-no-longer-wanted-in-conservative-party-says-kemi-badenoch); [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c62vrjyzyxlo))*
