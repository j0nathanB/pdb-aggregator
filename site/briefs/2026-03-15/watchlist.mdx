## Items on Watch

China's potential military response to closer US alliance ties in Asia tops the watch list. If the United States, Japan, and South Korea speed up joint military projects or share more technology, Beijing could respond with military measures that escalate regional tensions. Taiwan sits at the center of this dynamic, as any Chinese military response would likely affect the island's security.

Related to these closer ties is the question of who pays for them. Washington may soon push Japan, South Korea, and Australia to meet specific spending targets or take on specific military capabilities that go beyond current NATO guidelines. Such moves could fracture existing alliances if partners resist or if the costs prove too high politically.

Beyond Asia, Western democracies are being watched for signs they might coordinate responses to stress on democratic institutions. France, Britain, Italy, Canada, and Australia could develop shared mechanisms for dealing with political pressures, which would show institutions adapting to new challenges.
