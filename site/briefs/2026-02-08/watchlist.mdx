*Items worth monitoring that didn't make the executive briefing.*

France, Germany, Taiwan and South Korea are each finding different ways to institutionalise economic sovereignty — moving beyond rhetoric to policy frameworks for reducing dependence. Watch whether the EU begins coordinating these efforts systematically or if Asia-Pacific states turn their technological advantages into sustained hedging patterns.

Japan, South Korea and Australia are moving from strategy documents to actual implementation on alliance burden-shifting. Whether this translates into spending targets, capability requirements and cost-sharing arrangements or remains at the level of strategic planning is the question.

China faces a regional surge in economic sovereignty initiatives from Taiwan, Japan and South Korea. Whether Beijing responds with trade restrictions, investment limitations or diplomatic pressure, or takes a more measured approach to these hedging strategies, bears watching.
