### Americas

Across the hemisphere, middle powers are drawing lines in the sand — not by breaking with the United States, but by carefully marking the boundaries of what they will and will not tolerate.

Three major countries pushed back against US pressure this week, each through distinct mechanisms but with similar strategic logic. Canada challenged Western institutional effectiveness at Davos while simultaneously pursuing deals with China and Qatar. Mexico set explicit red lines on US military aircraft operations while maintaining FBI cooperation in other areas. Brazil condemned US intervention in Venezuela through a high-profile New York Times op-ed while coordinating with China on separate initiatives. None chose accommodation. None chose defection. All chose calibrated resistance.

The timing suggests something structural rather than coincidental. These sovereignty assertions emerged simultaneously across different issue domains — trade, military access, intervention — with no evidence of coordination between the countries involved. Instead, they reflect independent responses to shared external pressure from President Donald Trump's administration's return to assertive foreign policy. Middle powers appear to be responding not through accommodation but through strategic boundary-setting, maintaining operational cooperation while establishing clear limits on what they will accept.

What did not happen may be equally revealing. Regional institutional frameworks — the Organisation of American States, hemispheric summits, multilateral mechanisms — remained dormant. No country sought collective responses to US pressure. The absence suggests these institutions either lack the capacity for coordinated pushback or that countries prefer managing asymmetric relationships bilaterally, preserving individual flexibility over regional solidarity.

This represents a marked departure from traditional hemispheric dynamics, where US pressure typically produced either accommodation or isolated defections. Instead, a pattern of parallel sovereignty assertion has emerged — countries maintaining cooperation while drawing operational boundaries. Whether this proves sustainable as US pressure intensifies remains the critical question for regional stability.

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/ca.svg" width="32" alt="Canada" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', position: 'relative', top: '-2px', marginRight: '8px', border: '1px solid #d1d5db', borderRadius: '2px'}} />Canada

Prime Minister Mark Carney declared the "old order is dead" in a landmark speech that earned him a standing ovation at the World Economic Forum and marked Canada's most explicit break from Western institutional deference in decades.

In Davos, Mr Carney called for middle powers to band together and urged countries to condemn economic coercion "even by allies" — a reference to American trade threats. ([The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/article-eu-trump-greenland-tariff-threat/), 2026-01-20) He backed the rhetoric with concrete action, announcing trade agreements with China and investment deals with Qatar during the same trip. President Donald Trump responded by threatening 100% tariffs on Canadian goods if Canada pursues a trade deal with China, prompting Mr Carney to clarify that while limited agreements were made on electric vehicles and agriculture — including a 49,000 Chinese EV quota at a reduced 6.1% tariff rate — no comprehensive free trade agreement is planned. (CBC News, 2026-01-24) The prime minister is expected to spend much of 2026 travelling internationally to secure new partnerships, with a goal of doubling non-US exports within a decade. (CTV News, 2026-01-25)

The external pressure is producing contradictory domestic responses. Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre offered to help fast-track Liberal legislation on trade and affordability measures when Parliament resumes, calling Mr Carney's Davos speech "well-crafted" — an unusual display of bipartisan cooperation in the face of US threats. (CBC News, 2026-01-22) Yet Quebec sovereigntists mobilised fiercely against the prime minister this week, with Parti Québécois leader Paul St-Pierre Plamondon accusing him of "rewriting history" after Mr Carney described the Battle of the Plains of Abraham as the beginning of Canadian "partnership" rather than conquest. ([Radio-Canada](https://ici.radio-canada.ca/nouvelle/2222775/quebec-abraham-pspp-discours-roberge), 2026-01-22)

The backlash forced Mr Carney to cancel a planned news conference and prompted federal-provincial sovereigntist coordination not seen in years. Bloc Québécois leader Yves-François Blanchet appeared at the PQ convention declaring "Paul is my chief" and calling for unity between sovereigntist parties — the first time a Bloc leader was invited to speak at a PQ event since 2023. Both demanded an apology from the prime minister. The episode shows how even amid external threats that historically unified Canada, the country's linguistic and regional divisions remain as sharp as ever.

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/br.svg" width="32" alt="Brazil" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', position: 'relative', top: '-2px', marginRight: '8px', border: '1px solid #d1d5db', borderRadius: '2px'}} />Brazil

President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva published a combative op-ed in the New York Times condemning America's capture of President Nicolás Maduro as "another lamentable chapter in the continuous erosion of international law," declaring it the first direct American military attack on South America in over 200 years.

The timing was deliberate. On the same day President Donald Trump launched his Gaza Peace Council, Mr Lula held a 45-minute call with President Xi Jinping to coordinate Global South resistance, with Mr Xi emphasising the need to protect developing nations' interests and strengthen the UN's role. ([G1](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/01/23/lula-e-xi-conversa-telefone.ghtml), 2026-01-23) Mr Lula also announced visa exemptions for Chinese citizens, reciprocating China's 2025 measure. The diplomatic offensive reflects Brazil's increasingly assertive sovereignty positioning, even as it creates pressure on the country's traditional multi-alignment strategy. Mr Trump's invitation for Mr Lula to join his Gaza council has been analysed as a "diplomatic trap" — accept and subordinate to a US-controlled framework, or decline and assume public misalignment. ([Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/trump-pressiona-lula-com-armadilha-diplomatica-em-convite-para-conselho-de-paz-para-gaza/), 2026-01-19) Brazil, Mr Lula declared, "will not submit to hegemonic projects" while maintaining dialogue with Washington. ([G1](https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/2026/01/18/lula-artigo-eua-venezuela-nyt.ghtml), 2026-01-18)

In a private meeting, Mr Lula signalled plans to run for re-election with Vice-President Geraldo Alckmin, saying "in a winning team, you don't change players." ### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/mx.svg" width="32" alt="Mexico" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', position: 'relative', top: '-2px', marginRight: '8px', border: '1px solid #d1d5db', borderRadius: '2px'}} />Mexico

Mexico is drawing explicit operational boundaries with the United States while simultaneously deepening tactical cooperation — a careful balance that defines how a junior partner manages an asymmetric relationship.

President Claudia Sheinbaum announced this week that US military aircraft will no longer be permitted to land at civilian airports, responding to controversy over a US Hercules aircraft landing at Toluca. Instead, she said, Mexican aircraft will travel to pick up officials for training. ([El Financiero](https://elfinanciero.com.mx/nacional/2026/01/21/claudia-sheinbaum-mananera-temas-hoy-21-de-enero-de-2026-en-vivo), 2026-01-21) Even as Ms Sheinbaum drew that red line, her Security Secretary Omar García Harfuch was meeting with new FBI Director Kash Patel to discuss increased operational coordination. The meeting covered joint operations and extraditions, and two high-value targets were transferred to US custody immediately afterward. ([Tiempo.com.mx](https://www.tiempo.com.mx/nacional/reunion-omar-garcia-harfuch-kash-patel-director-fbi/), 2026-01-22) Days later, Mr García Harfuch delivered 37 more criminals to the US in a third major handover, bringing the total transferred to 92. He emphasised that none would face the death penalty and all represented genuine security threats to Mexico. ([El Financiero](https://elfinanciero.com.mx/nacional/2026/01/20/tercera-entrega-de-narcos-a-eu-harfuch-confirma-traslado-de-37-criminales-no-tendran-pena-de-muerte), 2026-01-20)

Ms Sheinbaum's Fourth Transformation (4T) government maintains strong popular support — 69.5% approval according to Mitofsky polling — but faces emerging coalition management challenges. Electoral reform, a key institutional priority, has stalled amid resistance from allied parties. The Labour Party (PT) and Green Ecologist Party of Mexico (Verde) expressed concerns about the lack of dialogue, with Ricardo Monreal, the National Regeneration Movement's (Morena) Senate leader, acknowledging that the party lacks sufficient votes for constitutional reform without allied support. Opposition parties are demanding meaningful negotiations. ([Política Expansión](https://politica.expansion.mx/presidencia/2026/01/21/sheinbaum-reforma-electoral-se-construye-con-opinion-ciudadana-no-a-partir-del-pt-y-verde), 2026-01-21)

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/cl.svg" width="32" alt="Chile" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', position: 'relative', top: '-2px', marginRight: '8px', border: '1px solid #d1d5db', borderRadius: '2px'}} />Chile

President-elect José Antonio Kast unveiled a cabinet with two-thirds independents, the most technocratic government in Chilean democratic history.

Mr Kast presented his 24-minister cabinet with 16 independents and only eight party members, describing it as a "government of emergency." The technocratic composition drew sharp criticism from coalition parties over minimal representation, with Mr Kast stating the cabinet "does not arise from quotas or calculations." The appointment process was notably closed, with parties having little input on final selections. ([La Tercera](https://latercera.com/politica/noticia/kast-anuncia-su-gabinete-con-mas-de-la-mitad-de-independientes-y-en-medio-de-incomodidad-de-partidos), 2026-01-20) The unprecedented composition reflects both Mr Kast's campaign promise of emergency governance and his weak position in Congress, where institutional constraints force adaptation from traditional party-based cabinet formation.

As Mr Kast prepares to take power, the outgoing administration faces twin crises. Major forest fires in Ñuble and Biobío regions killed over 20 people and destroyed hundreds of homes, prompting President Gabriel Boric to declare a state of catastrophe. The emergency triggered a massive response including military deployment and international firefighting assistance, testing state capacity during the transition. ([BBC](https://bbc.com/mundo/articles/ceqz0dj829vo), 2026-01-18) Meanwhile, the Santiago Court of Appeals revoked the dismissal of Mr Boric in a case investigating alleged misappropriation of public funds for hiring a private lawyer. The court questioned the "public necessity" of the contract and ordered the case reopened, keeping Mr Boric as an accused party during his final weeks in office. ([Nuevo Poder](https://nuevopoder.cl/corte-revoca-sobreseimiento-de-boric-en-causa-por-malversacion-de-caudales-publicos), 2026-01-23)

The president-elect also began building his foreign policy agenda around border security. Mr Kast met with Dominican President Luis Abinader to study migration control policies and border security measures, specifically the border fence model. Mr Kast indicated the Dominican experience would inform Chile's "Plan Escudo Fronterizo" starting in March, emphasising biometric controls and systematic identification of border crossers. ([La Tercera](https://latercera.com/politica/noticia/kast-profundiza-agenda-de-seguridad-y-control-migratorio-en-republica-dominicana-tras-reunion-con-abinader), 2026-01-25) Chilean Armed Forces meanwhile conducted Operation Sovereignty with deployment of the multipurpose vessel Sargento Aldea to Antarctica, reinforcing Chile's territorial claims and logistical presence in the region. ([Zona Militar](https://zona-militar.com/2026/01/18/operacion-soberania-chile-refuerza-su-proyeccion-estrategica-en-la-antartica-con-el-despliegue-del-buque-multiproposito-sargento-aldea), 2026-01-18)
