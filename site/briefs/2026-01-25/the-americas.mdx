### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/ca.png" alt="Canada" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Canada

Canada is undergoing its most significant strategic reorientation since the 1940s establishment of the postwar liberal order. The Carney government has explicitly challenged Western institutional effectiveness while maintaining membership, pursuing middle power coalitions and trade diversification through China engagement and international partnerships. Domestically, external US pressure is producing contradictory responses - both unusual bipartisan cooperation on foreign policy and continued Quebec sovereigntist mobilization. The country's institutional density remains high but fragmented along persistent cleavage lines, while democratic stability continues through established constitutional processes despite recurring stress tests along federal-provincial and linguistic divisions.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** PM Carney delivered a widely acclaimed speech at WEF declaring the rules-based international order dead and calling for middle powers to band together. He urged countries to condemn economic coercion even by allies, received standing ovation. Also announced trade agreements with China and investment deals with Qatar during the trip. *([The Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/article-eu-trump-greenland-tariff-threat/), 2026-01-20)*
- **Diplomatic:** Trump threatened 100% tariffs on Canadian goods if Canada pursues trade deal with China. Carney clarified that while limited agreements were made on EVs and agriculture, no comprehensive free trade agreement is planned. Includes 49,000 Chinese EV quota at reduced 6.1% tariff rate. *([CBC News](https://cbc.ca/news/politics/carney-canada-trump-tariff-threat-china-trade-9.7060150), 2026-01-24)*
- **Diplomatic:** PM Carney expected to spend much of 2026 traveling internationally to secure new trade links and investment, with goal of doubling non-US exports within decade. Potential destinations include Australia, India, and various multilateral summits. *([CTV News](https://ctvnews.ca/politics/article/carney-set-to-spend-much-of-2026-travelling-the-world-in-search-of-trade), 2026-01-25)*
- **Domestic:** PM Carney sparked controversy by describing Battle of Plains of Abraham as beginning of Canadian 'partnership' rather than conquest. PQ leader Paul St-Pierre Plamondon accused him of 'rewriting history' and demanded apologies. Carney cancelled planned news conference. *([Radio-Canada](https://ici.radio-canada.ca/nouvelle/2222775/quebec-abraham-pspp-discours-roberge), 2026-01-22)*
- **Domestic:** Conservative Leader Poilievre offered to help fast-track Liberal legislation on trade and affordability measures when Parliament resumes. Called Carney's Davos speech 'well-crafted' but criticized lack of action to match rhetoric. Represents unusual bipartisan cooperation offer. *([CBC News](https://cbc.ca/news/politics/poilievre-carney-davos-speech-9.7057086), 2026-01-22)*
- **Unexpected:** Conservative leader offers bipartisan cooperation while praising Carney's anti-Western order speech.

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/br.png" alt="Brazil" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Brazil

Brazil operates as a continental-scale middle power practicing strategic multi-alignment between Western institutions and Global South platforms, now with more assertive sovereignty positioning against US interventionism. Under Lula III, the country confronts persistent fiscal rigidity and deepening China dependency while the political system remains structured around Lula-Bolsonaro polarization despite the former president's imprisonment. Both sides demonstrate continued mobilization capacity heading into a highly competitive 2026 election. Institutional legitimacy faces pressure through STF internal crisis over Master Bank case handling while coalition presidentialism requires constant Centrão bargaining.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** Lula called the US capture of Maduro 'another lamentable chapter in the continuous erosion of international law,' stating this is the first time in 200+ years that South America suffered direct US military attack. Declared Brazil will not submit to hegemonic projects while maintaining dialogue with Washington. *([G1](https://g1.globo.com/economia/noticia/2026/01/18/lula-artigo-eua-venezuela-nyt.ghtml), 2026-01-18)*
- **Diplomatic:** 45-minute call where Xi emphasized protecting Global South interests and strengthening UN role. Occurred same day as Trump's Gaza Peace Council launch. Lula announced visa exemptions for Chinese citizens in reciprocity for China's 2025 measure. *([G1](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/01/23/lula-e-xi-conversa-telefone.ghtml), 2026-01-23)*
- **Diplomatic:** Invitation described as 'diplomatic trap' forcing choice between accepting and subordinating to US-controlled framework or declining and assuming public misalignment. Analysis notes this exposes Lula to 'difficult choice' that constrains multi-alignment strategy. *([Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/republica/trump-pressiona-lula-com-armadilha-diplomatica-em-convite-para-conselho-de-paz-para-gaza/), 2026-01-19)*
- **Domestic:** In private meeting, Lula indicated plans to repeat winning ticket with VP Alckmin, saying 'in a winning team, you don't change players.' Also discussed São Paulo electoral scenarios with different roles for Haddad depending on Tarcísio's presidential intentions. *([Veja](https://veja.abril.com.br/politica/a-reuniao-em-que-lula-sinalizou-que-repetira-chapa-com-alckmin-em-2026/), 2026-01-18)*
- **Domestic:** Rally organized by Deputy Nikolas Ferreira drew ~18,000 people calling for Bolsonaro's release and other January 8 convictions. Demonstrates continued mobilization capacity despite former president's imprisonment and ineligibility. *([G1](https://g1.globo.com/df/distrito-federal/noticia/2026/01/25/manifestacao-pela-liberdade-de-bolsonaro-reune-18-mil-pessoas-em-brasilia-diz-ong.ghtml), 2026-01-25)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/mx.png" alt="Mexico" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Mexico

Mexico operates as a periphery middle power locked into asymmetric structural integration with the United States through overwhelming economic dependency (83% export concentration) while pursuing calibrated sovereignty boundary-setting. Sheinbaum is actively managing the bilateral relationship through explicit operational red lines (restricting US military aircraft access) while deepening tactical cooperation (record extraditions, FBI director meetings). The Fourth Transformation government maintains genuine popular support (69.5% approval) but faces emerging coalition management challenges as allied parties resist key institutional reforms. The military has consolidated unprecedented commercial and civilian governance roles that transcend electoral cycles.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** Following controversy over US Hercules aircraft landing at Toluca airport, Sheinbaum announced that US military aircraft will no longer be permitted to land at civilian airports. She stated Mexican aircraft will travel to pick up officials for training rather than allowing US military aircraft to enter. *([El Financiero](https://elfinanciero.com.mx/nacional/2026/01/21/claudia-sheinbaum-mananera-temas-hoy-21-de-enero-de-2026-en-vivo), 2026-01-21)*
- **Diplomatic:** Security Secretary García Harfuch met with new FBI Director Kash Patel to discuss increased operational coordination. Meeting covered joint operations, extraditions, and two high-value targets were transferred to US custody following the meeting. *([Tiempo.com.mx](https://www.tiempo.com.mx/nacional/reunion-omar-garcia-harfuch-kash-patel-director-fbi/), 2026-01-22)*
- **Domestic:** PT and Verde parties express concerns about lack of dialogue on electoral reform. Ricardo Monreal acknowledged Morena lacks sufficient votes for constitutional reform without allied support. Opposition parties demand meaningful negotiations. *([Política Expansión](https://politica.expansion.mx/presidencia/2026/01/21/sheinbaum-reforma-electoral-se-construye-con-opinion-ciudadana-no-a-partir-del-pt-y-verde), 2026-01-21)*
- **Security:** Third major extradition bringing total transferred to US to 92 criminals. García Harfuch emphasized none would face death penalty and all represented genuine security threats to Mexico. Included high-value targets like Pedro Inzunza Noriega and Ricardo González Sauceda. *([El Financiero](https://elfinanciero.com.mx/nacional/2026/01/20/tercera-entrega-de-narcos-a-eu-harfuch-confirma-traslado-de-37-criminales-no-tendran-pena-de-muerte), 2026-01-20)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/cl.png" alt="Chile" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Chile

Chile enters the final weeks of transition under an institutional framework that continues to constrain executive power despite Kast's strong electoral mandate. The incoming administration's technocratic cabinet structure (67% independents) represents adaptation to congressional weakness rather than choice, while early foreign policy signaling suggests alignment-building with restrictive immigration models. State capacity remains functional as demonstrated by forest fire emergency response, but judicial proceedings against the outgoing president and ongoing estallido accountability issues highlight persistent institutional tensions. The multi-vector trade architecture remains unchanged, preserving Chile's structural economic dependencies while new diplomatic alignment patterns emerge around security and migration issues.

**Key developments:**

- **Domestic:** President-elect Kast presented his 24-minister cabinet featuring 16 independents and only 8 party militants, described as a 'government of emergency.' The technocratic composition drew criticism from coalition parties over minimal representation, with Kast stating the cabinet 'does not arise from quotas or calculations.' The appointment process was notably closed, with parties having little input on final selections. *([La Tercera](https://latercera.com/politica/noticia/kast-anuncia-su-gabinete-con-mas-de-la-mitad-de-independientes-y-en-medio-de-incomodidad-de-partidos), 2026-01-20)*
- **Domestic:** Major forest fires in Ñuble and Biobío regions caused over 20 deaths and destroyed hundreds of homes, prompting President Boric to declare a state of catastrophe. The emergency triggered massive response including military deployment and international firefighting assistance, testing state capacity and civil-military coordination during the transition period. *([BBC](https://bbc.com/mundo/articles/ceqz0dj829vo), 2026-01-18)*
- **Domestic:** Santiago Court of Appeals revoked the definitive dismissal of President Gabriel Boric in a case investigating alleged misappropriation of public funds for hiring a private lawyer. The court questioned the 'public necessity' of the contract and ordered the case reopened, maintaining Boric as an accused party during his final weeks in office. *([Nuevo Poder](https://nuevopoder.cl/corte-revoca-sobreseimiento-de-boric-en-causa-por-malversacion-de-caudales-publicos), 2026-01-23)*
- **Diplomatic:** President-elect Kast met with Dominican President Abinader to study migration control policies and border security measures, specifically the border fence model. Kast indicated the Dominican experience would inform Chile's 'Plan Escudo Fronterizo' starting in March, emphasizing biometric controls and systematic identification of border crossers. *([La Tercera](https://latercera.com/politica/noticia/kast-profundiza-agenda-de-seguridad-y-control-migratorio-en-republica-dominicana-tras-reunion-con-abinader), 2026-01-25)*
- **Security:** Chilean Armed Forces conducted Operation Sovereignty with deployment of multipurpose vessel Sargento Aldea to Antarctica, alongside completion of paratrooper training and various international military exchanges. The Antarctic operation reinforces Chile's territorial claims and logistical presence in the region. *([Zona Militar](https://zona-militar.com/2026/01/18/operacion-soberania-chile-refuerza-su-proyeccion-estrategica-en-la-antartica-con-el-despliegue-del-buque-multiproposito-sargento-aldea), 2026-01-18)*
