*Items worth monitoring that didn't make the executive briefing.*

The Pentagon's signals to Japan, South Korea and Australia on burden-sharing may amount to more than the usual pressure. If Washington is moving from strategic documents to concrete spending targets and capability requirements, that represents a shift in how the alliance operates, not just what it costs. Worth watching whether implementation follows the rhetoric.

Separately, the surge in economic sovereignty spending across Asia-Pacific — Taiwan, Japan, South Korea and Indonesia are all making large investments — has yet to draw a serious Chinese response. That silence probably won't last. Any move by Beijing to restrict trade, limit investment or pressure individual states would test whether these hedging strategies hold up under direct retaliation.
