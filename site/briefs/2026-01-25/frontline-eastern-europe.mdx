### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/ua.png" alt="Ukraine" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Ukraine

Ukraine operates as a wartime state undergoing tactical recalibration of its Western alignment strategy while transitioning to more aggressive military posture and potentially preparing for post-war leadership transitions. The country maintains unprecedented Western integration through EU accession negotiations and bilateral security agreements, but is openly criticizing European strategic paralysis while pursuing transactional commitments with the Trump administration through new trilateral negotiation formats. Military doctrine has shifted toward offensive operations with escalated casualty objectives and AI-integrated drone warfare capabilities. Major domestic leadership changes bring intelligence professionals into civilian roles, potentially signaling succession planning. Despite severe demographic decline and infrastructure destruction, the state demonstrates continued capacity for strategic adaptation while fighting an existential conflict.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** Zelensky gave an unusually harsh speech at WEF criticizing Europe as 'fragmented' and 'lost,' comparing the situation to 'Groundhog Day.' He stated Europe looks 'lost trying to convince the US president to change' and that Trump 'will not listen to this kind of Europe.' Following his meeting with Trump, Zelensky announced they reached agreement on post-war US security guarantees for Ukraine, though territorial issues remain unresolved. *([Multiple outlets](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/cjrzjqg8dlwt), 2026-01-21)*
- **Diplomatic:** Following Zelensky's Davos meetings, trilateral negotiations between Ukrainian, Russian, and American delegations began in Abu Dhabi. Ukraine sent high-level delegation including Budanov and Umerov. Trump's envoy Steve Witkoff and son-in-law Jared Kushner held three-hour talks with Putin. Kremlin confirmed the three-way format while stating Russia would continue battlefield operations until diplomatic settlement. *([ABC Australia](https://abc.net.au/news/2026-01-23/zelenskyy-announces-ukraine-talks-with-russia-us/106260134), 2026-01-22)*
- **Security:** Syrskyi announced Ukraine will conduct offensive operations in 2026, stating 'victory cannot be achieved through defense alone.' He emphasized Ukraine will maintain strategic defense while conducting offensive operations to keep operational initiative. He reported Russian drone production at 404 Shaheds daily with plans to reach 1,000 per day, while Ukrainian forces disrupted Russian formations and liberated 430 sq km near Pokrovsk in recent counteroffensive. *([RBC Ukraine](https://newsukraine.rbc.ua/news/ukraine-to-shift-to-offensive-operations-1768757707.html), 2026-01-18)*
- **Security:** Defense Minister Fedorov outlined goal of inflicting 50,000 Russian battlefield deaths monthly, up from current 35,000. He announced plans for drone-assault units with new staffing structures, AI integration of combat data through Mission Control project, and 40,000 interceptor drones expected this month. Stated Ukraine would target Russian drone operators directly and scale up specialized hunter units. *([Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/68426), 2026-01-19)*
- **Domestic:** Former military intelligence chief Kyrylo Budanov was appointed head of the President's Office, replacing Andriy Yermak. Analysis describes this as a 'tectonic shift' in Ukrainian politics. Budanov is highly trusted (some polls show more than Zelensky), known for surviving 10 assassination attempts and directing major operations against Russia. Move seen as potentially launching 'Operation Successor' though Zelensky has cut Budanov off from his intelligence base by choosing a Yermak ally as HUR successor. *([The Week](https://theweek.com/world-news/the-rise-of-the-spymaster-a-tectonic-shift-in-ukraines-politics), 2026-01-18)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/fi.png" alt="Finland" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Finland

Finland has undergone the most dramatic strategic realignment in its modern history since 2022, pivoting from decades of military non-alignment to NATO's most heavily militarized frontline member with the Alliance's longest Russian border. The country now fields one of Europe's largest reserve armies (280,000 wartime strength expanding toward 1 million) and spends 2.5% of GDP on defense with a target of 3% by 2029. This week saw the materialization of the predicted defense-fiscal tension as Finland was placed under EU Excessive Deficit Procedure, requiring corrective measures by April 2026 partly due to F-35 costs. Diplomatically, Finland demonstrated mature engagement with China while firmly holding Western positions, and coordinated EU responses to Trump's tariff threats. Domestically, the right-wing Orpo government faces a severe legitimacy crisis with coalition polling at just 36-39% while managing unprecedented demographic decline (fertility rate 1.25) and mounting fiscal pressures. Finland's institutional framework remains among the world's strongest, but internal coalition tensions and political harassment of ministers reflect growing polarization.

**Key developments:**

- **Economic:** EU Council formally placed Finland under excessive deficit procedure, requiring fiscal corrective measures by April 30, 2026. Finance Minister Purra said Finland prepared to implement additional measures if Commission demands them. Deficit partly due to F-35 costs hitting earlier than expected. May require 0-€1.4 billion additional austerity by 2027. *([YLE](https://yle.fi/a/74-20205403), 2026-01-21)*
- **Economic:** Orpo visited Valio Seinäjoki factory, praised dairy export growth including 'truckloads of Finnish butter going to France for croissant baking.' Valio's exports to 50 countries grew to €513 million in 2024, with Sweden trade doubling since 2017 to over €100 million. *([Maaseudun Tulevaisuus](https://maaseuduntulevaisuus.fi/uutiset/7a9e05b7-a490-4121-bb99-b55584178c17), 2026-01-25)*
- **Diplomatic:** Stubb attended WEF January 19-22, delivering speeches on the new world order and European security. He participated in panels on Ukraine's future and Europe's security situation, held bilateral meetings with presidents of Azerbaijan, Armenia, Israel, and Switzerland. Finland joined 7 other countries in joint statement supporting Denmark and Greenland sovereignty while criticizing US tariff threats. *([Presidential Office](https://www.presidentti.fi/en/president-stubb-in-davos-we-are-witnessing-the-emergence-of-a-new-world-order/), 2026-01-19)*
- **Diplomatic:** Orpo visited Beijing January 25-28, meeting President Xi, Premier Li Qiang, and other officials. Ukraine was his 'absolute top priority' in discussions. He raised human rights concerns and defended multilateral institutions. Meeting with Xi extended from planned 30 minutes to 50 minutes due to Orpo's historical knowledge impressing the Chinese leader. *([Government Communications Department](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/prime-minister-orpo-to-meet-with-chinese-leadership-in-beijing), 2026-01-25)*

He called threats 'based on incorrect conclusions' and said EU must prepare countermeasures if negotiations fail, warning of trade war risk. *([Government Communications Department](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/prime-minister-orpo-to-attend-informal-european-council-transatlantic-relations-on-the-agenda), 2026-01-22)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/ee.png" alt="Estonia" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Estonia

Estonia is a digitally advanced Baltic microstate whose entire strategic orientation constitutes a continuous response to the existential problem of surviving beside Russia without sufficient mass to defend itself alone. This week demonstrated the stress-testing of Estonia's alliance architecture: when faced with US pressure over Greenland, Estonia chose EU solidarity and alliance principles over avoiding displeasure from its most critical security guarantor. The country's response—participating in EU emergency meetings while maintaining military cooperation commitments despite economic threats—reveals both the depth of institutional embedding and the priority placed on alliance cohesion over bilateral accommodation. Estonia continues spending 5% of GDP on defense while achieving the world's highest density of unicorn companies and ranking 2nd globally in press freedom, but its willingness to risk US displeasure for alliance solidarity represents a notable evolution in how it manages competing alliance pressures.

**Key developments:**

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/cz.png" alt="Czech Republic" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Czech Republic

The Czech Republic operates under increasing institutional tension as cohabitation between pro-Western President Pavel and the Babiš coalition creates policy friction, particularly on Ukraine support. While structural Western integration (F-35, KHNP nuclear, EU economic dependence) remains irreversible, the government is constraining concrete aid delivery and tolerating anti-Ukraine rhetoric from coalition partners. The country maintains Western orientation but with reduced solidarity and more transactional approach to alliance commitments.
