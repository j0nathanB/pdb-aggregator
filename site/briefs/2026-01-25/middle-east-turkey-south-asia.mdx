### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/tr.png" alt="Turkey" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Turkey

Turkey occupies the single most consequential geopolitical position of any middle power, straddling Europe and Asia while controlling the only naval chokepoint between the Mediterranean and Black Seas. Under Erdoğan's hyper-presidential system, Turkey has exploited the vacuum left by US retrenchment to pursue strategic autonomy, but is now selectively deepening alliance partnerships—particularly with Washington on Middle East conflicts through Gaza Peace Council founding membership and intensive Syria coordination. The Kurdish peace process displays both extraordinary symbolic progress (Öcalan-Bahçeli carpet gift) and dangerous fragility (border flag incident), with the transactional linkage to constitutional amendments creating delicate coalition management requirements. With 86 million people, NATO's second-largest military, and a $1.4 trillion economy, Turkey remains too large to ignore, too strategically located to abandon, and too institutionally compromised to fully trust, but is increasingly prioritizing its U.S. partnership as the key to consolidating regional gains.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** President Erdoğan accepted Trump's invitation to join the Gaza Peace Council, with Foreign Minister Fidan designated as Turkey's representative. Multiple high-level phone calls discussed Syria and Gaza coordination between the two leaders. *([BBC News Türkçe](https://bbc.com/turkce/articles/cwynp991zx7o), 2026-01-22)*
- **Diplomatic:** President Erdoğan declared a 'new era' in regional politics, claiming Turkey's Syria strategy has been vindicated. Multiple reports highlighted Turkey's growing Middle East influence and strategic gains from the Syrian conflict resolution. *([Anadolu Agency](https://aa.com.tr/en/turkiye/president-erdogan-highlights-turkiye-s-role-as-emerging-pole-in-reshaped-world-order/3807845), 2026-01-18)*
- **Diplomatic:** Hakan Fidan held multiple meetings with regional counterparts and US officials on Syria ceasefire and Gaza peace plan. He warned about potential military operations in Syria if negotiations fail and discussed extending the current ceasefire. *([Anadolu Agency](https://aa.com.tr/tr/gundem/disisleri-bakani-fidan-ypg-sdg-suriye-kurtlerini-temsil-eden-aktor-degil/3809221), 2026-01-25)*
- **Diplomatic:** Israel banned Bilal Erdoğan and 28 other senior Turkish officials from entering the country, citing 'systematic boycott calls, glorification of violence and antisemitic content.' The move reflects deteriorating Turkey-Israel relations. *([Israel Hayom](https://israelhayom.com/2026/01/22/israel-bans-erdogan-son-turkish-officials-entry), 2026-01-22)*
- **Domestic:** Devlet Bahçeli issued an 8-point roadmap for Syria calling for SDG dissolution and revealed that PKK leader Öcalan had gifted him a specially-woven carpet, which he named the 'February 27, 2025 Peace and Democracy Carpet.' He also made statements supporting territorial integrity while calling for negotiated solutions. *([Türkiye Gazetesi](https://turkiyegazetesi.com.tr/gundem/devlet-bahceli-ocalanin-hediyesini-ilk-kez-acikladi-baris-ve-demokrasi-kilimi-1765920), 2026-01-25)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/sa.png" alt="Saudi Arabia" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia operates as a hyper-centralized petrostate under Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, who has consolidated unprecedented personal authority while attempting ambitious socioeconomic transformation. The kingdom continues multi-vector hedging but demonstrated stronger US alignment this week through Major Non-NATO Ally designation and diplomatic coordination. Realistic adjustment of Vision 2030 ambitions through NEOM scaling reflects fiscal constraints from oil dependency, while technological modernization efforts continue. Critical vulnerabilities include geographic concentration of oil infrastructure, dependence on desalinated water, and social contract renegotiation dependent on continuous job creation for massive youth demographic.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** President Trump signed presidential determination formally designating Saudi Arabia as Major Non-NATO Ally under Foreign Assistance Act. Only 20 countries globally hold this status. *([Federal Register](https://federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/23/2026-01421/presidential-determination-on-designation-of-the-kingdom-of-saudi-arabia-as-a-major-non-nato-ally), 2026-01-23)*
- **Diplomatic:** Foreign Minister held multiple diplomatic meetings at WEF including signing founding charter of Trump's Board of Peace initiative for Gaza, conducted calls with US Secretary Rubio on Iran and regional security *([US State Department](https://www.state.gov/releases/office-of-the-spokesperson/2026/01/secretary-rubios-call-with-saudi-foreign-minister-prince-faisal-bin-farhan-al-saud-9), 2026-01-25)*
- **Economic:** Kingdom conducting major review of NEOM megaproject with plans to significantly scale back and redesign development. The Line component may be reduced from original 110-mile scope, 2029 Asian Winter Games postponed *([Financial Times](https://ft.com/content/036b158f-ea34-46d1-ad0a-093eba031501), 2026-01-25)*
- **Economic:** Saudi Aramco CEO Amin Nasser spoke at World Economic Forum dismissing forecasts of oil oversupply as 'seriously exaggerated' and announcing company expects $3-5 billion in AI-driven savings for 2025 *([Reuters](https://reuters.com), 2026-01-23)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/ae.png" alt="United Arab Emirates" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />United Arab Emirates

The UAE continues executing its calculated multi-alignment strategy with notable deepening of strategic relationships. MBZ's India visit produced defense partnership frameworks and $200B trade targets, positioning India as a key partner alongside existing US, French, and Korean relationships. The $3B ADNOC-India LNG deal establishes India as the UAE's largest LNG customer, demonstrating energy statecraft diversification. UAE's hosting of trilateral Russia-Ukraine-US peace talks reinforces its trusted mediator positioning across major power divides. Sovereign wealth deployment accelerates into AI and longevity sectors while G42's digital sovereignty framework addresses previous China partnership constraints. The regime maintains performance-based legitimacy through continued security provision, with Abu Dhabi retaining its decade-long 'world's safest city' ranking.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** Three-hour official visit produced Strategic Defense Partnership Letter of Intent covering defense industrial collaboration, counter-terrorism, and special operations, plus commitment to double bilateral trade from current $100B to $200B by 2032. Fifth MBZ visit to India in decade, third as president. *([UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://mofa.gov.ae/en/MediaHub/News/2026/1/19/uae-india), 2026-01-19)*
- **Diplomatic:** Two-day trilateral negotiations in Abu Dhabi, building on UAE's role as trusted mediator having facilitated 17 prisoner exchange operations releasing 4,641 detainees. Foreign Minister Abdullah bin Zayed emphasized UAE's balanced partnerships with all parties enable trusted mediation role. *([UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://mofa.gov.ae/en/MediaHub/News/2026/1/23/UAE-Russia-Ukraine-United-States), 2026-01-23)*
- **Diplomatic:** MBZ accepted US invitation to Board of Peace, with Foreign Minister emphasizing importance of fully implementing Trump's 20-point peace plan for Gaza and UAE's confidence in Trump's leadership exemplified by Abraham Accords. *([UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://mofa.gov.ae/en/MediaHub/News/2026/1/20/UAE-US), 2026-01-20)*
- **Economic:** Ten-year agreement valued $2.5-3 billion for 0.5 million tonnes annually from Das Island facility, making India the UAE's largest LNG customer. ### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/in.png" alt="India" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />India

India demonstrates continued multi-alignment strategy with assertive diplomatic pushback against partner pressure while actively developing alternative economic architecture. The UAE President's visit with concrete trade targets and India's blunt warnings to Poland about Pakistan engagement show sustained Gulf ties and active resistance to European approaches that undermine Indian security interests. The BRICS CBDC proposal represents significant institutional positioning for reduced dollar dependency, while domestic organizational changes maintain regime continuity through younger leadership appointments and routine political mobilization.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan's fifth visit to India in ten years resulted in joint commitment to double bilateral trade to $200 billion by 2032, up from current $100 billion. Leaders issued joint condemnation of cross-border terrorism and reviewed comprehensive strategic partnership. *([Prime Minister's Office](https://pmindia.gov.in), 2026-01-19)*
- **Diplomatic:** External Affairs Minister criticized selective US tariffs and warned Poland against 'fueling terrorism in our neighbourhood' during Polish Deputy PM's visit. Remarks were described as unusually blunt diplomatic signaling regarding Pakistan engagement. *([Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/selective-targeting-on-tariffs-says-jaishankar-flags-poland-ministers-outreach-to-pakistan-10483095), 2026-01-19)*
- **Economic:** India's central bank recommended including proposal to connect BRICS countries' CBDCs on 2026 summit agenda to facilitate cross-border trade and tourism payments while potentially reducing US dollar reliance. India's e-rupee has 7 million retail users since December 2022 launch. *([Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/rbi-proposes-linking-brics-digital-currencies-says-report-heres-why-it-would-be-an-important-move/articleshow/126673026.cms), 2026-01-19)*
