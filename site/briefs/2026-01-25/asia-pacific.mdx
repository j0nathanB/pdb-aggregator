### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/jp.png" alt="Japan" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Japan

Japan is navigating unprecedented alliance uncertainty while maintaining domestic political strength. PM Takaichi's snap election call demonstrates confidence in her ~70% approval ratings and represents strategic political positioning to strengthen governing authority. The Trump administration's 'America First' National Defense Strategy creates the most significant alliance stress since 1951 by explicitly reducing China focus and demanding allied self-reliance, forcing Japan to accelerate autonomous capabilities while managing alliance assurance through early summit diplomacy. Japan is deploying active economic statecraft through ¥39 billion rare earths diversification to counter Chinese economic coercion while coordinating fiscal expansion with monetary normalization to sustain escape from deflation. The opposition's CDP-Komeito merger into the Centrist Reform Alliance represents the largest realignment attempt in years but reflects weakness rather than strength.

**Key developments:**

- **Economic:** The LDP manifesto pledges to secure stable rare earths supply through ¥39 billion in funding for mine development and refining projects, explicitly countering China's ban on dual-use exports including critical minerals. The government will fund the Japan Organization for Metals and Energy Security to jointly invest in overseas rare earth projects. *([Japan Times](https://japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/01/21/japan/politics/ldp-election-manifesto), 2026-01-21)*
- **Economic:** BOJ Governor Ueda indicated the central bank would continue raising interest rates, emphasizing timely decisions as wage hikes drive inflation. Comments came as Japanese bond markets experienced volatility due to concerns over PM Takaichi's expansionary fiscal policies and consumption tax cut proposals. *([Reuters](https://reuters.com), 2026-01-25)*
- **Economic:** Japan Business Federation announced it will lead efforts to deepen wage increase momentum, proposing larger hikes for younger workers and performance-based allocation. PM Takaichi emphasized business community's role in achieving public-private investment goals of ¥200 trillion by FY2040. *([Prime Minister's Office](https://japan.kantei.go.jp/104/actions/202601/19kondankai.html), 2026-01-19)*
- **Domestic:** PM Takaichi dissolved the Lower House on January 23 for a February 8 election, seeking to capitalize on high approval ratings to strengthen her coalition government. This represents Japan's second election in less than 16 months and Takaichi's first as PM. *([NHK](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/en/news/20260123_500/), 2026-01-23)*
- **Domestic:** The Constitutional Democratic Party and Komeito officially launched the Centrist Reform Alliance with 172 Lower House members, led by joint leadership of Yoshihiko Noda and Tetsuo Saito. The merger represents the largest opposition consolidation attempt in years ahead of the snap election. *([Yomiuri Shimbun](https://japannews.yomiuri.co.jp/politics/election/20260123-306091), 2026-01-23)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/id.png" alt="Indonesia" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Indonesia

Indonesia under Prabowo is actively implementing multi-alignment foreign policy through intensive presidential diplomacy while deepening state control over the economy through expanded Danantara deployment and potential central bank independence erosion. The £4 billion UK partnership and Davos 'Prabowonomics' promotion demonstrate continued Western engagement alongside existing Chinese dependencies, while the \$14 billion sovereign wealth fund expansion and nepotistic BI appointment signal intensifying political control over economic institutions. This week confirms the dossier's assessment of strategic hedging combined with domestic institutional capture, but shows the regime is operating with confidence and international acceptance rather than facing immediate constraints.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** President Prabowo concluded UK working visit with £4 billion investment commitments covering maritime sector, including 1,582 fishing vessels to be produced in Indonesia employing ~600,000 workers, plus education cooperation establishing 10 new campuses in medicine and STEM fields *([Detik.com](https://news.detik.com/berita/d-8318978/prabowo-bawa-pulang-investasi-rp-90-t-proyek-ribuan-kapal-nelayan-dari-inggris), 2026-01-22)*
- **Diplomatic:** President Prabowo addressed WEF 2026 promoting his economic philosophy 'Prabowonomics', emphasizing Indonesia's economic resilience (5%+ growth, 2% inflation), Danantara sovereign wealth fund as enabling equal partnership globally, and plans to rationalize 1,044 SOEs to ~300 while recruiting foreign executives for better management *([Kompas](https://nasional.kompas.com/read/2026/01/23/12310741/isi-prabowonomics-yang-dibicarakan-prabowo-di-wef-2026-apa-saja?page=all), 2026-01-23)*
- **Economic:** Indonesia's sovereign wealth fund Danantara announced plans to deploy up to \$14 billion in 2026 funded by state-owned enterprise dividends, while launching waste-to-energy projects, new Patriot Bond issuances, and making debut at World Economic Forum in Davos *([Reuters](https://reuters.com), 2026-01-19)*
- **Economic:** President Prabowo nominated Thomas Djiwandono (currently deputy finance minister and his nephew) as one of three candidates for BI deputy governor, sparking investor concerns about central bank independence and contributing to rupiah hitting record lows *([Reuters](https://reuters.com), 2026-01-19)*
- **Economic:** Major Indonesian bank stocks including BCA fell over 5% in one week amid foreign selling pressure totaling Rp 3.27 trillion, with BCA alone seeing Rp 3.97 trillion in net foreign sales, reflecting broader market weakness and rupiah concerns *([CNBC Indonesia](https://cnbcindonesia.com/market/20260123180238-17-704888/sepekan-bbca-dihajar-asing-saham-turun-526), 2026-01-23)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/tw.png" alt="Taiwan" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Taiwan

Taiwan achieved a significant economic statecraft breakthrough this week through the US trade agreement, securing 15% tariff treatment (matching key allies) in exchange for unprecedented \$250-500 billion semiconductor investment commitments. This represents successful navigation of Trump's transactional diplomacy while accelerating US semiconductor onshoring strategy. TSMC-driven market records and analyst projections suggest Taiwan retains technological advantages despite capacity diversification. However, domestic institutional gridlock persists with the defense budget blocked for the eighth consecutive time, creating implementation gaps between defense commitments and legislative approval. The information warfare response to the trade deal demonstrates ongoing Chinese influence operations targeting even Taiwan's policy successes.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** Premier Cho Jung-tai welcomed returning negotiators after securing agreement reducing US tariffs on Taiwanese goods from 20% to 15%, matching Japan/South Korea/EU rates. Taiwan commits \$250B in semiconductor/tech investments plus \$250B in credit guarantees. Deal includes TSMC's accelerated Arizona expansion beyond existing \$165B commitment. *([Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/politics/202601190006), 2026-01-22)*
- **Diplomatic:** Presidential Office reports Lai met with German Marshall Fund transatlantic delegation, announced Taiwan's defense spending will reach 5% of GDP by 2030 and confirmed \$40 billion special defense budget for T-Dome air defense system development. *([Presidential Office](Government source findings), 2026-01-20)*
- **Economic:** Trade agreement includes Taiwan commitment of up to \$500B for US-based chip manufacturing (\$250B private investment, \$250B government credit). Reduces US tariffs from 20% to 15%. President Lai defended deal against opposition claims it could 'hollow out' Taiwan's chip industry. *([TaiwanPlus](https://www.taiwanplus.com/news/taiwan-news/taiwan-us-trade-and-tariffs/260122002/taiwan-president-defends-new-us-trade-agreement), 2026-01-22)*
- **Economic:** TAIEX reached record closing high of 31,639.29 points driven by TSMC performance. TSMC shares hit record NT\$1,780 intraday. Market reflects strong AI chip demand and trade agreement optimism, with analysts noting less than 15% of TSMC advanced processes expected in US by 2029 despite expansion. *([Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202601190014), 2026-01-19)*
- **Security:** KMT and TPP used Legislative Yuan majority to block Executive Yuan's NT\$1.25 trillion (\$39.55 billion) special defense budget for eighth time. Opposition demands military pay raises be incorporated into general budget and insists president report to legislature on defense matters. *([Taipei Times](https://taipeitimes.com/News/taiwan/archives/2026/01/23/2003851109), 2026-01-22)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/kr.png" alt="South Korea" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />South Korea

South Korea maintains its multi-vector hedging strategy while facing new external pressures. Lee Jae-myung continues balancing US alliance commitments with pragmatic engagement with China, now confronting Pentagon signals of reduced US commitment to North Korea deterrence that Seoul is resisting. Domestically, Lee governs from a position of strength with sustained approval ratings above 50% while institutional accountability from the December 2024 martial law crisis continues through high-level convictions. The economy shows mixed signals with GDP contraction offset by equity market milestones, while new technological sovereignty capabilities emerge through the BOK's pioneering sovereign AI system.

**Key developments:**

- **Diplomatic:** Pentagon's 2026 National Defense Strategy states South Korea should take 'primary responsibility' for deterring North Korea with 'critical but more limited US support.' South Korea's defense ministry responded that US Forces in Korea 'remained central to the alliance and would continue to deter North Korean aggression.' *([The Guardian](https://theguardian.com/world/2026/jan/24/north-korea-pentagon-to-reduce-role-deterrence), 2026-01-24)*
- **Security:** Pentagon document describes shift toward South Korea taking 'primary responsibility' for North Korea deterrence with 'more limited US support,' consistent with 'updating US force posture on the Korean Peninsula.' ROK defense ministry pushes back, stating USFK remains central to deterrence. *([The Guardian](https://theguardian.com/world/2026/jan/24/north-korea-pentagon-to-reduce-role-deterrence), 2026-01-24)*
- **Economic:** BOK and Naver launched BOKI (Bank of Korea Intelligence), described as world's first finance-focused sovereign AI system by any central bank. System operates entirely within BOK's internal network using Naver's HyperCLOVA X model and 1.4 million standardized documents. Represents exception to Korea's network separation rules. *([Korea Times](https://koreatimes.co.kr/business/banking-finance/20260121/bok-teams-up-with-naver-to-launch-sovereign-ai-service), 2026-01-21)*
- **Economic:** Economy contracted 0.3% in Q4, growing only 1% for full year 2025, missing 0.2% expansion estimate. Simultaneously, Kospi surged past 5,000 for first time, reaching President Lee's campaign pledge milestone. Reflects divergence between export-oriented sectors and broader economic performance. *([Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/01/22/economy/south-korea-contraction-fragility/), 2026-01-22)*
- **Domestic:** Seoul Central District Court sentenced Han Duck-soo to 23 years in prison for his role in December 2024 martial law declaration, marking first cabinet-level conviction from the crisis. Court ruled martial law constituted insurrection aimed at subverting constitutional order. *([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/01/21/asia/south-korea-han-yoon-jailed-intl-hnk), 2026-01-21)*

### <img src="https://flagcdn.com/h24/au.png" alt="Australia" style={{display: 'inline', verticalAlign: 'middle', marginRight: '8px'}} />Australia

Australia occupies a structurally contradictory position as a wealthy, stable democracy of 27.6 million positioned at the hinge of the Indian and Pacific Oceans, economically dependent on China (29% of exports) while security-dependent on the United States through the deepening AUKUS alliance. The country possesses robust Westminster institutions, extraordinary natural resource endowments, and a \$4+ trillion superannuation system, yet faces permanent geographic constraints from its island-continent isolation. Australia is undergoing its most significant strategic transformation since WWII through the \$268-368 billion AUKUS submarine program while maintaining the world's second-worst media concentration. The May 2025 landslide victory is now under severe political stress with government approval collapsed and One Nation at record highs following the Bondi terror attack response, while the opposition Coalition faces existential fragmentation over counter-terrorism legislation.

**Key developments:**

- **Domestic:** Parliament passed new hate speech laws targeting extremist groups and gun buyback scheme in response to Bondi Beach massacre. Coalition split with Liberals supporting weakened version after Labor dropped racial vilification provisions, while Nationals opposed entire package. Laws passed 38-22 in late-night Tuesday vote. *([The Guardian](https://theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/jan/20/labor-albanese-hate-speech-laws-expected-pass-parliament), 2026-01-20)*
- **Domestic:** Nationals leader David Littleproud and all frontbench colleagues agreed to resign shadow cabinet positions after Opposition Leader Sussan Ley accepted resignations from three Nationals senators who crossed floor on hate speech laws. Threatens future of Coalition with mass exodus over cabinet solidarity breach. *([The Guardian](https://theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/jan/21/nationals-coalition-frontbench-sussan-ley-hate-speech-bill), 2026-01-21)*
- **Domestic:** PM's net performance rating fell from plus 6 in December to minus 22, while One Nation vote share hit record high following weeks of political turmoil over government's Bondi terror attack response. Labor primary support slumped 5 points to 30%, lowest since February last year. *([The Age](https://theage.com.au/politics/federal/support-for-albanese-plummets-after-muddled-response-to-bondi-attack-20260118-p5nuv8.html), 2026-01-18)*
- **Domestic:** PM delivered emotional apology to families of 15 victims killed in December 14 Bondi Beach terror attack during memorial ceremony at Sydney Opera House, saying he was 'profoundly sorry' for being unable to protect victims and acknowledging particular impact on Jewish community. *([SBS](https://sbs.com.au/news/article/anthony-albanese-apology-bondi-attack-day-of-mourning/5di2rwpf0), 2026-01-21)*
- **Diplomatic:** PM declined to specify Australia's response to Trump's invitation to join proposed 60-nation Board of Peace for Gaza reconstruction, saying government would 'give it further consideration.' Major allies France, Sweden, Norway, Canada and UK have raised concerns about the initiative. *([Guardian Australia](https://theguardian.com/australia-news/2026/jan/20/labor-albanese-hate-speech-laws-expected-pass-parliament), 2026-01-19)*
