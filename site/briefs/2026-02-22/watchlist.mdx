Middle powers across several regions — India, Turkey, Brazil, Indonesia — are all hedging independently, but parallel strategies have a way of becoming coordinated ones. Any sign of joint policies or shared institutions among these states would mark a shift from individual manoeuvring to collective leverage.

The tightening defence cooperation among allies in Asia-Pacific has yet to draw a serious Chinese response. Whether Beijing reacts with military pressure, restrictions on technology sharing, or something quieter will shape how fast Japan, South Korea, Australia and Taiwan move.

And Washington's approach to burden-sharing bears watching for what it reveals about differentiation. If the Pentagon sets specific spending targets or capability requirements for Asian allies beyond existing NATO benchmarks, it confirms that the US is managing its alliances region by region rather than through a single framework.
