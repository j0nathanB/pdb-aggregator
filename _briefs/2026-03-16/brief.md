# Week of March 9, 2026

> European leaders this week confronted escalating tensions over Ukraine policy and Middle East conflicts, with French President Emmanuel Macron and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy meeting to discuss maintaining Russian sanctions while criticizing reported U.S. plans to pause oil restrictions. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney announced a $35 billion Arctic defense plan and said Canada would not join U.S. military operations in Iran, signaling independent foreign policy positioning amid regional conflicts. In Latin America, Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum proposed alternative electoral reforms after congressional rejection while Brazilian President Lula da Silva revoked a U.S. diplomat's visa following disputes over access to imprisoned former President Jair Bolsonaro. The diplomatic developments occurred as military casualties mounted, with French forces losing personnel in Iraq and Congo drone attacks.

### Brazil announces diesel subsidies amid Middle East war
{: #brazil-announces-diesel-subsidies-amid-middle-east-war}

BRASILIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva signed decrees Thursday creating a package of measures to contain the impact of Middle East conflict on diesel prices, including eliminating federal taxes and providing subsidies totaling 0.64 reais per liter. "We are making a sacrifice to prevent the effects of the irresponsibility of war from reaching the Brazilian people," Lula said during a press conference. Finance Minister Fernando Haddad said the temporary measures will cost about 30 billion reais but will be funded by new export taxes on petroleum. The government also strengthened oversight powers to prevent abusive price increases at gas stations amid supply disruptions from the conflict affecting the Strait of Hormuz.
