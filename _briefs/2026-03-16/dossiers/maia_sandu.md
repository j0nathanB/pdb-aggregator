### Moldova's Sandu receives European Order of Merit award

STRASBOURG, France — Moldovan President Maia Sandu was named an honorary member of the European Order of Merit, a new distinction created by the European Parliament to honor contributions to European integration and values, European Parliament President Roberta Metsola announced Tuesday. The award recognizes individuals who have actively contributed to building Europe, Metsola said. Other recipients include former Lithuanian President Valdas Adamkus, former Polish Prime Minister Jerzy Buzek, and former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Mary Robinson. The official ceremony will take place during the European Parliament's plenary session May 18-21 in Strasbourg.

### Moldova's Sandu Awards Lithuania's President Highest State Honor

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Moldovan President Maia Sandu awarded Lithuania's President Gitanas Nausėda the "Order of the Republic," Moldova's highest state distinction, during an official visit Tuesday, according to a statement from Moldova's presidency. The honor recognizes Nausėda's "exceptional contribution to the development and consolidation of Moldovan-Lithuanian friendship and cooperation relations" and his support for Moldova's European path, the presidency said. Sandu's two-day visit coincides with the 36th anniversary of Lithuania's independence restoration from the Soviet Union on March 11, 1990 — an act Moldova was the first country to recognize, officials said. Sandu is scheduled to address Lithuania's parliament during a solemn session.
