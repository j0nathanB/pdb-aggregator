## Top Stories

### Zelenskyy signs drone production, energy agreements with Romania

BUCHAREST, Romania — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy signed bilateral agreements with Romanian President Klaus Iohannis on Thursday covering joint drone production and energy sector cooperation, Romanian officials said. The agreements were signed during Zelenskyy's visit to the NATO member country, where he also said Ukraine is awaiting White House approval for a major drone production deal proposed by Kyiv last year. Zelenskyy said Russia has fired more than 57,000 Shahed drones at Ukraine since its invasion began, while Ukraine has developed cut-price drone killers costing a few thousand dollars each. The Ukrainian leader was scheduled to visit French President Emmanuel Macron in Paris following his Romania trip.

### Zelenskyy criticizes US Russian oil sanctions waiver decision

PARIS, France — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Friday that the U.S. 30-day waiver on Russian oil sanctions is "not the right decision" and won't help stop Russia's invasion of Ukraine. The U.S. Treasury Department announced Thursday a temporary waiver aimed at freeing up Russian oil cargoes stranded at sea and easing supply shortages caused by the Iran war. Zelenskyy said lifting sanctions would only allow "more drones will later be flying at you," while German Chancellor Friedrich Merz also criticized the decision as "wrong." The Ukrainian president separately offered his country's drone expertise to help the U.S. and Gulf partners, though President Donald Trump rejected the assistance in comments to Fox News Radio.

### Slovak PM asks Zelenskyy to resume Druzhba oil transit

BRATISLAVA, Slovakia — Slovak Prime Minister Robert Fico has written to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy urging him to resume oil supplies via the Druzhba pipeline, according to a letter dated March 12 posted on Facebook. Fico said Ukraine's decision to stop oil transit has caused economic damage and logistical problems for Slovakia, comparing it to the unilateral termination of gas transit. The Slovak leader claimed he has information suggesting damage from Russia's January 27 attack on the pipeline was not severe enough to prevent resuming oil supplies. ### Ukraine Sanctions Russian Judges Over Prisoner Sentences

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy signed decrees imposing sanctions on 41 judges who issued unlawful prison sentences against Ukrainian prisoners of war, the Office of the President said Tuesday. The sanctions target 38 Russian citizens and three collaborators who "support and justify Russia's aggression against Ukraine," according to the announcement. Among those sanctioned is a Russian judge who sentenced nine Ukrainian prisoners of war in fabricated terrorism cases within a week, and former Ukrainians who sentenced foreign volunteers defending Ukraine to death in occupied Donetsk. Zelenskyy also extended sanctions against 11 Russian companies involved in aviation equipment repair, drone production, and illegal operations in occupied Crimea.

### Zelenskyy Awards Ukrainian Military Volunteers on National Day

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy presented state awards to Ukrainian military volunteers and families of fallen defenders during ceremonies marking Ukrainian Military Volunteer Day, the president's office said Tuesday. Zelenskyy awarded the Order of the Golden Star, Ukraine's highest honor, to families of five deceased soldiers, including Junior Sergeant Oleksandr Kovalenko, who was killed in August 2023 after leading operations across multiple regions. The president also presented Cross of Military Merit awards to active soldiers, including Lieutenant Colonel Yehor Nozdriakov, whose brigade eliminated about 1,300 enemy combatants from August to October 2023, according to the presidential office. "Ukrainians have repeatedly proven that they know how to fight for themselves and how to defend their home," Zelenskyy said during the ceremony.
