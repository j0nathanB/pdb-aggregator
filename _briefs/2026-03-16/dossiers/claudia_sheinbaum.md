## Top Stories

### Mexico's Sheinbaum proposes Plan B after electoral reform rejected

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum said she will send a "Plan B" electoral reform to Congress on Monday after lawmakers rejected her constitutional reform proposal Wednesday. The lower house fell short of the required supermajority with 259 votes in favor and 234 against, as Sheinbaum's usual allies from the Green Party and Labor Party withheld support. Sheinbaum said the new proposal would cap spending by local legislators and senators, potentially saving 4 billion pesos for public needs.

### Mexico's Sheinbaum renews gasoline price cap agreement

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum announced Wednesday the renewal of an agreement with fuel industry representatives to maintain a maximum price of 24 pesos per liter for regular gasoline, amid rising global fuel prices due to conflict in Iran.
