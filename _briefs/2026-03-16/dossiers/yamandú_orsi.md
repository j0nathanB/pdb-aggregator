### Uruguay's Orsi approval rating drops to 33%, poll shows

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi's approval rating fell to 33% while disapproval rose to 40%, marking the first negative net rating of his administration, according to a new poll released Thursday by Equipos Consultores. The survey shows a net rating of -7 percentage points, with 24% of respondents expressing neutral views, said Ignacio Zuasnabar, director of public opinion at Equipos. The decline represents a shift from November when approval and disapproval were tied at 36% each, making Orsi the first Frente Amplio president to register a negative rating in his first year, the polling firm said.

### Uruguay's Orsi meets with hate crime victim's sister

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi met Monday with Noemí Fremd, sister of David Fremd, a Jewish merchant killed in a 2016 hate crime that shocked the country. During the meeting at the Executive Tower, Fremd presented a proposal to combat antisemitism through educational and cultural interventions, according to presidential sources cited by El Observador. Orsi said it is necessary to work with various government levels to recover values that distinguish Uruguay as a nation, emphasizing the need to deepen "values of coexistence with Uruguayan Jews and tolerance." The meeting occurred as Uruguay approaches the 10th anniversary of David Fremd's murder on March 8, 2016, in Paysandú, which was investigated as a hate crime motivated by the victim's Jewish identity.

### Uruguay considers moving prosecutor to break judicial deadlock

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi's government is considering proposing that current Chief Prosecutor Mónica Ferrero move to the Administrative Court to break a political deadlock over judicial appointments, a government source said.
