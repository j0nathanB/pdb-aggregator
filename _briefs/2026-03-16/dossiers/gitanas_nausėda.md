### Lithuanian president says coalition party backs military training ground

### Lithuanian President Marks Independence Day, Supports Moldova EU Bid

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said Lithuania cannot be stopped as long as it remains united and maintains strong international alliances, speaking Monday during a flag-raising ceremony marking the 36th anniversary of Lithuania's independence restoration. ### Lithuania's President Discusses Energy Prices at EU Competitiveness Meeting

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda participated remotely Tuesday in a Competitiveness Friends Group meeting to prepare for the March 19-20 European Council summit in Brussels, his office said. The meeting, organized by Germany, Italy and Belgium, focused on energy prices, strengthening the EU single market and reducing bureaucracy, according to a statement from the presidential office. Nausėda said the unstable situation in the Middle East affects global energy markets and increases price volatility, requiring urgent action to protect European economic competitiveness. ### Lithuania's President Proposes Electricity Price Relief for Industry

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda will soon propose legislation to temporarily reduce electricity costs for major industrial consumers in exchange for repayment commitments starting in 2031. The plan would cut the regulated infrastructure portion of electricity prices by about 25% for energy-intensive businesses from 2027-2031, according to Ramūnas Dilba, the president's advisor and head of the environment and infrastructure group. Dilba said the measure aims to immediately strengthen Lithuania's industrial competitiveness, with businesses repaying the subsidized costs between 2031-2038 as domestic electricity generation and consumption grow.

### Lithuania's President Proposes Tax Breaks to Address Demographics Crisis

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said Monday he will propose legislation to address Lithuania's demographic problems, which he called a "ticking bomb." Nausėda said he plans to submit bills during the spring parliamentary session that would eliminate income tax for families with two or more children earning up to the average wage, limited to five years. The president also proposed tax incentives for employers who hire parents with multiple children, with their wages multiplied by at least 1.5 times when calculating taxable profits. State Data Agency figures show Lithuania's population declined 0.1 percent last year, with a record low 17,500 births in 2025.
