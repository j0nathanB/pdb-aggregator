### Macron meets Zelensky, vows to maintain Russia sanctions

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron met with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky on Friday and pledged that European Union sanctions against Russia would continue despite the Middle East conflict. ### Macron urges Iran to halt regional attacks, restore Hormuz navigation

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron said he told Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian it was "unacceptable" to target French interests after an Iranian-designed drone killed a French soldier in Iraq's Kurdistan region. ### French soldier killed in Iraq drone attack, Macron says

PARIS, France — One French soldier was killed and six others wounded in a drone attack in northern Iraq, President Emmanuel Macron said Friday. Macron called the attack in Iraq's Kurdistan region "unacceptable" and said France's position in the Middle East was "defensive," according to remarks made during a news conference with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy. The French military said the troops were conducting counterterrorism training with Iraqi partners when the attack occurred at a base in Mala Qara, about 40 kilometers from Erbil.

### Macron promotes nuclear energy as guarantee of independence

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron positioned nuclear energy as a guarantor of energy independence during the second Nuclear Energy Summit hosted at the Elysée Palace, according to French media reports. More than 100 guests, including French and foreign ministers, nuclear industry executives, and international representatives, attended Tuesday's closing dinner for the summit organized by France and the International Atomic Energy Agency. EDF CEO Bernard Fontana described the event as representing "a big momentum" for nuclear power's return to global energy policy. The gathering occurred amid rising oil prices and ongoing Middle East conflicts, with attendees citing the need for economic electrification to reduce dependence on volatile oil markets.

### French aid worker killed in drone strike in eastern Congo

GOMA, Democratic Republic of Congo — A French humanitarian worker with UNICEF was killed in drone strikes that hit the eastern Congolese city of Goma overnight Tuesday into Wednesday, French President Emmanuel Macron said on social media platform X. The woman, who was in charge of UNICEF programs in the region and was a mother, died when multiple sites were targeted in the city now controlled by the M23 rebel group, according to humanitarian sources. Corneille Nangaa, leader of the M23's political wing, said the strikes were ordered by the Congolese army and targeted former President Kabila's residence, with the humanitarian worker's house hit by mistake.
