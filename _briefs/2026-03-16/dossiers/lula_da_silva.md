## Top Stories

### Brazil announces diesel subsidies amid Middle East war

BRASILIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva signed decrees Thursday creating a package of measures to contain the impact of Middle East conflict on diesel prices, including eliminating federal taxes and providing subsidies totaling 0.64 reais per liter. "We are making a sacrifice to prevent the effects of the irresponsibility of war from reaching the Brazilian people," Lula said during a press conference. Finance Minister Fernando Haddad said the temporary measures will cost about 30 billion reais but will be funded by new export taxes on petroleum. The government also strengthened oversight powers to prevent abusive price increases at gas stations amid supply disruptions from the conflict affecting the Strait of Hormuz.

### Brazil revokes US diplomat visa after Bolsonaro prison visit denied

BRASILIA, Brazil — Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on Friday revoked the visa of U.S. State Department official Darren Beattie, who had sought to visit imprisoned former President Jair Bolsonaro. Lula said the decision was reciprocal after the U.S. revoked visas of Brazilian officials last year, including the health minister. Supreme Court Justice Alexandre de Moraes had denied Beattie's prison visit request Thursday, citing the Foreign Ministry's warning that such a visit during an election year could constitute "undue interference" in Brazil's internal affairs. Beattie, a Trump administration official, had initially received a visa to attend a minerals forum in São Paulo but failed to disclose his intention to visit Bolsonaro, who is serving a 27-year sentence for leading a coup attempt.

### Brazil's PT outlines Lula reelection platform with work reforms

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — Brazil's Workers' Party is preparing policy guidelines for President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's reelection campaign that include ending the six-day work week, implementing free public transportation and strengthening national sovereignty. The document will be voted on during the party's 8th Congress from April 23-26, 2026, said Cristiano Silveira, the congress sub-secretary responsible for drafting the guidelines. ### Lula releases $266 million for flood-hit Minas Gerais cities

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva announced the release of 266.5 million reais ($266 million) for emergency actions and reconstruction in Minas Gerais cities hit by heavy rains in recent weeks, according to a provisional measure published Monday in the official gazette. The flooding in the Zona da Mata region killed 72 people and left one missing, with Juiz de Fora recording the highest number of casualties, state authorities said. The federal funds will be administered by the Ministry of Integration and Regional Development to finance victim assistance, infrastructure repairs and direct support for families who lost their homes. Lula visited the affected region in late February and said the government would apply the same "assisted purchase" model used in Rio Grande do Sul, where families receive support to buy new homes in safe locations.
