### Polish President Vetoes EU Defense Loan Bill

WARSAW, Poland — President Karol Nawrocki said Thursday he would not sign legislation creating a mechanism to spend 43.7 billion euros in European Union loans to boost Poland's military, triggering condemnation from Prime Minister Donald Tusk's government. The clash over the 150 billion-euro Security Action for Europe initiative highlights deep divisions between the nationalist president and the pro-EU government. Tusk said the government would present its response at an extraordinary council of ministers Friday morning and called the president's decision shameful. The government has previously said it is preparing a "plan B" that would still allow Poland to access SAFE funds.

### Polish PM Tusk, Seven Leaders Urge EU Restrictions on Russian Soldiers

WARSAW, Poland — Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk and seven other European leaders called on EU officials to impose restrictions on Russian military personnel seeking entry to the bloc, according to a letter released Friday. The prime ministers wrote to European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and European Council President Antonio Costa, stating that Russia's invasion of Ukraine poses internal security threats to the EU, particularly from potential influx of former and active Russian soldiers into the Schengen zone. The letter said this group includes more than 18,000 previously convicted criminals recruited from Russian prisons for front-line service. The leaders requested visa denials, residence permit restrictions and long-term entry bans across the Schengen area for Russian military personnel.

### Polish poll shows mixed approval for Prime Minister Tusk

WARSAW, Poland — A new poll shows 38.2% of Poles approve of Prime Minister Donald Tusk's performance, while 60.3% disapprove, according to an IBRiS survey conducted for Onet. The poll found 94% of ruling coalition voters support Tusk, while only 3% of opposition voters approve of his work. Tusk has faced criticism from President Karol Nawrocki, who called him the worst prime minister in Poland's post-communist history. The survey of 1,000 adult Poles was conducted March 9 using computer-assisted telephone interviews.

### Polish PM Tusk warns of Polexit threat amid EU loan dispute

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said Poland faces a real threat of leaving the European Union, citing opposition from far-right parties and President Karol Nawrocki's veto of an EU defense loan program. ### Polish PM Tusk backs Hungarian opposition, draws minister's rebuke

WARSAW, Poland — Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk expressed support for Hungarian opposition demonstrations on the country's national holiday, prompting a sharp response from Hungary's foreign minister. ### Polish PM says no fuel shortage risk amid Iran conflict

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said Monday that Poland faces no immediate risk of fuel shortages despite the conflict in Iran, though he cautioned about longer-term uncertainties. Tusk said the government would not allow companies, including state oil giant Orlen, to profit from the crisis situation.
