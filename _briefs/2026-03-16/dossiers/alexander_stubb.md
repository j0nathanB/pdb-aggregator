### Finland's Stubb meets party leaders on nuclear weapons law

HELSINKI, Finland — President Alexander Stubb met with parliamentary party leaders Friday to discuss controversial changes to nuclear weapons restrictions that have divided Finland's political parties. The government and Stubb support removing legal restrictions on nuclear weapons while issuing a political declaration that no nuclear weapons would come to Finland during peacetime, according to Finnish media reports. Opposition Social Democratic Party leader Antti Lindtman proposed establishing a parliamentary working group to review nuclear weapons policy and demanded the government freeze current legislative preparations. Stubb said he understands criticism of the nuclear energy law preparations but urged parties to work toward finding the broadest possible agreement on the changes.

## International

### Finland won't host nuclear weapons in peacetime, president says

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb said Friday that NATO member Finland does not intend to host nuclear weapons on its soil during peacetime, aligning with policies in neighboring Nordic countries. Stubb made the comments after Finland's government last week unveiled plans to lift a longstanding ban on nuclear arms on its territory, drawing criticism from Russia and Finnish opposition parties. "Finland does not need nuclear weapons in peacetime. This is about nuclear deterrence — a deterrent to ensure they would never have to be used," Stubb told reporters in Helsinki. The Kremlin said it would respond if Finland placed nuclear weapons on its territory, with spokesman Dmitry Peskov calling such a move an escalation of tensions.

### Finnish President calls India key strategic partner during state visit
