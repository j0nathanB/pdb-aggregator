THE MIDDLE POWERS MONITOR
Week of March 9, 2026
THIS WEEK
This week European leaders confronted escalating tensions over Ukraine policy and Middle East conflicts, with Macron and Zelenskyy meeting to maintain Russian sanctions while criticizing U.S. plans to pause oil restrictions. Canadian Prime Minister Carney announced a $35 billion Arctic defense plan and rejected joining U.S. military operations in Iran, signaling independent foreign policy positioning. Latin American leaders faced domestic challenges as Mexico's Sheinbaum proposed alternative electoral reforms after congressional rejection while Brazil's Lula revoked a U.S. diplomat's visa following disputes over imprisoned former President Bolsonaro. Military casualties mounted across regions, with French forces losing personnel in Iraq and Congo drone attacks.

North America

Carney announced a $35 billion Arctic defense plan while opposing U.S. Russian oil sanctions and rejecting participation in Iran military operations, moving closer to parliamentary majority as an NDP member defected to his government. Sheinbaum announced 'Plan B' electoral reforms after Congress rejected her initial proposal, told Trump that Mexico's sovereignty on security matters is non-negotiable, and defended aid to Cuba while dismissing World Cup 2026 hosting concerns.

South America

Lula revoked a U.S. diplomat's visa after Washington denied access to imprisoned Bolsonaro, marking a significant diplomatic escalation, while announcing $2.08 billion infrastructure investment in Paraná state as polling showed him tied with Flávio Bolsonaro for 2026. Orsi's approval rating dropped to 33% as he addressed multiple high-profile crimes and expressed willingness to join Trump's proposed Americas Shield security coalition, signaling a potential shift in Uruguay's regional diplomatic approach.

Europe

Macron met with Zelenskyy pledging continued Russian sanctions while urging Iran to halt regional attacks, as France faced military losses with soldiers killed in Iraq and Congo drone strikes. Starmer acknowledged the Mandelson appointment as a mistake while announcing household energy support and signing energy cooperation frameworks with Ireland. Merz criticized U.S. easing of Russia sanctions and visited Norwegian space defense facilities, while Meloni faced domestic protests over judicial reforms and criticized U.S.-Israeli strikes on Iran. Zelenskyy signed drone agreements with Romania while offering Ukraine's defense expertise to U.S. and Middle East allies, increasingly challenging Western allies' policies including criticism of the U.S. oil sanctions waiver.

Baltic States

Nausėda set a 2030 target for Ukraine and Moldova's EU membership while securing coalition backing for military training grounds and proposing demographic crisis tax breaks. Siliņa ordered investigations into the troubled Rail Baltica project while directing officials to develop plans for cutting Russian economic ties. Michal faced mounting political pressure with party departures and parliamentary questioning over Middle East policy, defending his positions while announcing Estonia's defense spending reached a record 5.4% of GDP.
