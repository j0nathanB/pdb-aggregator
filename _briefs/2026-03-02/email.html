THE MIDDLE POWERS MONITOR
Week of February 23, 2026
THIS WEEK
This week marked a pivotal moment in global geopolitics as European and Western leaders committed $2 billion in new Ukraine aid on the war's fourth anniversary while simultaneously responding to escalating Middle East tensions following U.S.-Israeli strikes that killed Iran's Supreme Leader Khamenei. French President Emmanuel Macron called for an emergency UN Security Council meeting and updated France's nuclear doctrine, while Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney backed the Iran strikes and announced military support for Ukraine. Latin American leaders faced mounting domestic pressures, with Brazilian President Lula da Silva polling in a statistical tie with Flavio Bolsonaro and Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi's approval rating plummeting to 23% amid labor strikes and classified leak scandals.

North America

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney announced $2 billion in Ukraine military aid while backing U.S. strikes on Iran, simultaneously pursuing economic diversification through trade talks with India and defense agreements with South Korea. Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum secured a $6 billion Coca-Cola investment commitment and proposed electoral reforms cutting government spending by 25%, while managing cartel violence in Sinaloa state and coordinating directly with President-elect Trump following a major cartel leader's death.

South America

Brazilian President Lula da Silva confronted a political crisis as polls showed him tied with Flavio Bolsonaro in a potential runoff while facing majority disapproval ratings, forcing policy reversals on waterway decrees following Indigenous protests and deploying evangelical lawmakers to address religious community backlash. Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi's approval rating collapsed to 23% in his first year amid opposition calls for sanctions against a presidential aide over classified leaks and worker strikes over unpaid wages, even as he signed the EU trade agreement into law.

Europe

French President Emmanuel Macron called for emergency UN consultations following Iranian strikes while updating France's nuclear doctrine, accepting the Louvre director's resignation after a crown jewels heist and conducting government reshuffles amid coalition pressures. British Prime Minister Keir Starmer faced a stunning defeat as the Green Party captured Labour stronghold Manchester Gorton while allowing U.S. use of British bases for Iran strikes, as German Chancellor Friedrich Merz pursued China trade reset talks and Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni proposed electoral law changes while managing coalition tensions over Ukraine policy. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy conducted extensive diplomatic outreach from a wartime bunker, reporting 420 Russian drone and 39 missile strikes while stating peace talks timing depends on security conditions.

Baltic States

Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda was hospitalized following a household injury while signing defense agreements with Ukraine and marking the war anniversary with regional leaders, as a fence around his residence was demolished following complaints. Latvian Prime Minister Evika Siliņa joined the high-profile Kyiv visit while facing domestic criticism over cancer drug claims and Olympic attendance, and Estonian Prime Minister Kristen Michal announced 11 million euros in Ukraine military aid while warning against populism in his Independence Day speech.
