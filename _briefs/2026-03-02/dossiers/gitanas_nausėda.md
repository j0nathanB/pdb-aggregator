### Lithuanian President Nausėda hospitalized after household injury

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda was hospitalized Sunday after suffering a household injury at home and underwent surgery for a laceration to his left forearm, the presidential office said. The Lithuanian head of state was released from Vilnius University Hospital on Monday morning and will work from home in the coming days, according to his office. The injury forced Nausėda to cancel a planned visit to Kyiv this week for the fourth anniversary of Russia's war against Ukraine, with Defense Minister Robertas Kaunas representing Lithuania instead, the presidency said.

### Fence surrounding Lithuanian president's home demolished after complaint

VILNIUS, Lithuania — A fence surrounding Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda's home was demolished after a citizen complaint alleged it illegally occupied state land, officials said Wednesday. Adas Paulius Paražinskas filed the complaint with the State Territorial Planning and Construction Inspectorate, claiming the fence in Pavilniai Regional Park violated regulations. The concrete barrier was replaced with a metal security fence, according to local media reports. Construction inspectors said they planned to conduct an inspection despite the changes to the property.

### Lithuania's President marks Ukraine war anniversary with regional leaders

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda joined other Nordic and Baltic leaders in issuing a joint statement with Ukraine's president marking four years of Russia's war against Ukraine, according to official sources. In a video message posted on social media platform X, Nausėda told Ukrainians that Russia believed it could "erase your nation from history with rockets and terror," but praised Ukraine's resistance. "Your courage inspires millions. Your resilience strengthens Europe," Nausėda said in the message commemorating the war anniversary.

### Lithuanian President Condemns Iran Attacks on Regional Security

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda condemned Iran's attacks as violations of international law that further destabilize Middle East security, he said Saturday on social media platform X. Nausėda said Iran's continued support for Russia's war against Ukraine "is part of the same behavior pattern that threatens international peace and security." The statement came as Israeli and U.S. military forces attacked Iran, with Tehran launching retaliatory strikes, according to reports. Lithuania recommended its 17 citizens in Iran evacuate and advised 219 Lithuanians in Israel to seek shelter, National Crisis Management Center head Vilmantas Vitkauskas said at a press conference.

### Lithuanian President Affirms NATO Commitment as 'Plan A, B and C'

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda said NATO remains his country's primary security strategy, calling the alliance "our plan A, B and C" in an interview published Tuesday by Kyiv Post. Nausėda said he sees no reasons to believe NATO could face problems in the near future, citing the Munich Security Conference as evidence that the United States needs Europe and Europe needs the United States. The Lithuanian leader said he received a letter from U.S. President Donald Trump on Lithuania's Independence Day emphasizing that the United States values Lithuania's support for American troops stationed there.

### Lithuania, Ukraine sign defense equipment production agreement

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy signed an agreement Tuesday for the production of defense equipment for Ukraine, the Lithuanian presidency said. The signing coincided with the fourth anniversary of Russia's war in Ukraine. "This agreement is not only an industrial or technological project, but also proof of our political will and strategic partnership," Nausėda said in a statement. He said the agreement would strengthen Ukraine's ability to repel aggression and enhance European security overall.
