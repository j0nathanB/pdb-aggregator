### Moldova's Sandu Pledges Continued Support for Ukraine

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said Moldova will maintain "unwavering solidarity" with Ukraine as the country marked four years since Russia's invasion began. "We will always be grateful to Ukrainians for defending our peace and freedom and that of all Europe," Sandu said in a statement Tuesday. She said Ukraine deserves "a just peace" and thanked the country for "keeping Russia at a distance" from Moldova. The Moldovan parliament building was illuminated in Ukrainian flag colors Monday evening, with candles spelling "PEACE" placed in front of the structure.

### Moldova President Revokes Pardon for Drug Trafficking Convict

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu revoked a presidential pardon for Nicolae Șepeli, a man convicted of drug trafficking in Russia, after authorities informed her that he was suspected of being part of a criminal group planning assassinations of Ukrainian public figures, the presidency said. Sandu had originally pardoned Șepeli in April 2022 based on recommendations from prosecutors who described him as a victim of human trafficking. The case has sparked controversy over how a drug trafficking convict came to receive clemency and later allegedly became connected to the Șor network, according to investigative reports.
