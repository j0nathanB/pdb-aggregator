### Mexico's Sheinbaum proposes electoral reform cutting spending 25%

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum said Wednesday she will send a bill to Congress next week to reform Mexico's electoral law, proposing to slash public spending for electoral processes by 25% and redistribute funds to social programs.
