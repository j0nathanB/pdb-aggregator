WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk called opposition leader Jarosław Kaczyński "Jarosław Mercosur Kaczyński" during a parliamentary session Thursday, defending Agriculture Minister Stefan Krajewski against a no-confidence vote.
