### Macron calls for UN Security Council meeting after Iran strikes

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron called for an urgent United Nations Security Council meeting Saturday following U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran, saying the outbreak of war has "serious consequences" for international peace and security. Macron said France was not warned or involved in the attacks and called the escalation "dangerous for everyone," according to his office. The French leader spoke with leaders from Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Jordan and Iraq's Kurdistan region, and said France was ready to deploy resources to protect its closest partners. ### Macron denounces EU's provisional Mercosur trade agreement application

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron said Friday the European Commission's decision to provisionally apply a trade agreement with Mercosur countries was a "bad surprise" and "bad manners" toward the European Parliament. ### Macron accepts Louvre director's resignation after crown jewels heist

PARIS, France — President Emmanuel Macron accepted the resignation of Louvre Museum director Laurence des Cars on Tuesday following months of pressure after thieves stole French crown jewels valued at 88 million euros in October, his office said. Des Cars quit after what Macron's office called "an act of responsibility" at a time when the world's most visited museum needs "calm" and new momentum for security upgrades, according to a presidential statement. The resignation came amid widening scrutiny over security failures, a suspected decade-long ticket fraud scheme that may have cost 10 million euros, and ongoing labor disputes at the landmark museum. Macron plans to give des Cars a new mission focused on cooperation among major museums during France's presidency of the Group of Seven nations, the statement said.

### Macron appoints culture adviser as minister in reshuffle

PARIS, France — President Emmanuel Macron appointed his culture adviser Catherine Pégard as culture minister in a government reshuffle, the presidency said Thursday. Pégard, 71, a former political journalist who led the Château de Versailles for over 12 years, becomes the third culture minister during Macron's second term. ### French center-right parties fragment ahead of 2027 presidential race

PARIS, France — France's centrist and conservative parties that have governed together since 2024 snap elections have not formally considered a joint candidate for the 2027 presidential election, according to Le Monde. ### France reaches compromise on unemployment insurance reform

PARIS, France — French labor and business representatives reached a compromise on unemployment insurance reforms that will reduce maximum benefit duration, marking the fifth change to the system since President Emmanuel Macron took office in 2017. The CFDT and CFTC unions said their negotiators recommended signing the agreement with employer groups Medef, CPME and U2P, while the CGT and CFE-CGC unions rejected the text. Force Ouvrière negotiator Michel Beaugas said he refused to validate the agreement, criticizing it as a reduction in unemployed workers' rights. A final decision on union support is expected March 23.
