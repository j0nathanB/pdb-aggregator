# Week of February 16, 2026

> Global leaders this week confronted domestic institutional challenges while pursuing major international agreements, with Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum calling for calm after cartel violence and Brazilian President Lula da Silva vetoing congressional salary increases while criticizing Supreme Court actions. French President Emmanuel Macron sought a significant defense deal with India and requested sanctions relief from President-elect Trump, as Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney strengthened his parliamentary position through Conservative defections. The week highlighted tensions between executive authority and institutional oversight, with leaders from Mexico to Italy asserting federal power over courts and regional governments while managing complex diplomatic relationships.

### Sheinbaum calls for calm after cartel leader death sparks violence
{: #sheinbaum-calls-for-calm-after-cartel-leader-death-sparks-violence}

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum called for calm following the death of cartel leader Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes, known as "El Mencho," during a security operation in Tapalpa, Jalisco, that sparked road blockades and vehicle burnings across multiple states. Sheinbaum said there is "absolute coordination" with state governments and that activities continue with "full normalcy" in most of the territory. The president deferred detailed information about the operation to the Security Cabinet, saying they would provide updates through official social media channels. Federal sources confirmed the death of Oseguera, leader of the Jalisco New Generation Cartel, during the security operation.

### Estonian PM calls for aggressive future-oriented national strategy
{: #estonian-pm-calls-for-aggressive-future-oriented-national-strategy}

TARTU, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Estonia must become an "aggressively future-oriented country" during a Republic Day concert at the Estonian National Museum. Michal warned against those who advocate Estonia become a "gray mouse" accommodating to great power spheres of influence, stating such approaches would be "extremely dangerous" for small states. ### France seeks major Rafale jet deal during Macron India visit
{: #france-seeks-major-rafale-jet-deal-during-macron-india-visit}

NEW DELHI, India — France is preparing to negotiate a major fighter jet contract with India as President Emmanuel Macron prepares for a state visit from February 17-19, according to French officials. India approved the purchase of 114 Rafale fighter jets from Dassault Aviation for 3.25 trillion rupees ($30.2 billion), though the deal requires further technical and commercial negotiations, a presidential adviser said during a briefing. The Elysée called the approval a "very important milestone" toward a "historic" contract for the French aerospace manufacturer. India previously ordered 36 Rafale jets in 2015 and 26 more in 2025, as the world's largest arms importer increased military spending by 15% to $85 billion.

*Sources: (Le Monde [1](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/economy/article/2026/02/17/france-ready-to-offer-major-concessions-to-sell-rafale-jets-to-india_6750549_19.html))*

### Brazil's Lula defends carnival tribute after samba school relegated
{: #brazil-s-lula-defends-carnival-tribute-after-samba-school-relegated}

NEW DELHI, India — Brazilian President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva said Sunday that a carnival tribute to him by the Acadêmicos de Niterói samba school was "extraordinary," despite the school being relegated from Rio's top carnival division. Lula said he only accepted the school's invitation to be honored and did not participate in creative decisions for the parade, which featured his life story and criticized former President Jair Bolsonaro. The president, speaking during a visit to India, said the tribute also honored his late mother and called the parade's music "wonderful." The school will drop from the Special Group division in 2027 following its performance.

*Sources: (Reuters [1](https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/frances-macron-invites-brazils-lula-g7-summit-june-2026-02-19/); Folha de S.Paulo [1](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/cotidiano/2026/02/lula-diz-que-homenagem-da-academicos-de-niteroi-rebaixada-no-carnaval-foi-extraordinaria.shtml))*

### Meloni attacks Italian courts over migrant compensation rulings
{: #meloni-attacks-italian-courts-over-migrant-compensation-rulings}

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni accused Italian courts of undermining her government's efforts to curb illegal migration after a Palermo court ordered the government to pay 76,000 euros in compensation to charity Sea-Watch for unlawfully detaining its ship in 2019. "What message is being sent with this long series of objectively absurd decisions — that the government is not allowed to try to combat mass illegal immigration?" Meloni said in a video message Wednesday. The criticism comes ahead of a March 22-23 referendum on justice system reforms that polls show could go either way.

*Sources: (Reuters [1](https://www.reuters.com/world/tensions-mount-between-italys-government-courts-ahead-justice-referendum-2026-02-19/))*
