### Lithuania seeks balanced ties with China, Taiwan

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said Thursday that Lithuania could gradually restore diplomatic ties with China while maintaining relations with Taiwan, following a foreign policy meeting at the Presidential Palace. "If there is good will from the other side, we see an opportunity to gradually restore our diplomatic relations at the level of chargé d'affaires ad interim," Nausėda said after meeting with the prime minister, parliament speaker and other security officials. The president acknowledged that economic cooperation with Taiwan has fallen short of expectations since the Taiwanese Representative Office opened in Vilnius in 2021, prompting Chinese sanctions. Nausėda said Lithuania wants "fewer declarations and more concrete commitments from both sides" in future Taiwan cooperation.

## International

### Lithuania's Nausėda says Belarus sanctions to continue 12 months

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda said Thursday that European Union sanctions against Belarus will be extended for another 12 months, with EU ambassadors unanimously agreeing to the decision. Nausėda told reporters the sanctions extension should be implemented next week, citing Belarus's role as an accomplice to Russia in the war against Ukraine. The president said there are no signs that Belarus has changed its behavior, noting that while some political prisoners were freed, others were imprisoned.

### Lithuania president calls military mobility key to European security

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said military mobility is essential for strengthening European security, speaking at the LTG Forum as Ukraine continues defending against Russian aggression. Nausėda said Lithuania will maintain defense spending above 5 percent of GDP until 2030 and is preparing infrastructure in Rūdninkai for a German brigade deployment by 2027. The president said Europe needs to implement a common defense strategy and eliminate regulatory barriers for military movements across borders. Lithuania has introduced a green corridor for defense investments to position itself as a hub for Europe's defense industry, he stated.

### Lithuanian President Awards State Honors to Scientists, Athletes

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda awarded state honors to 33 individuals from science, medicine, sports, culture, diplomacy and national security fields during Lithuania's State Restoration Day ceremony Monday. The recipients included biochemist Virginijus Šikšnys, who received the Grand Cross of the Order of the Lithuanian Grand Duke Gediminas for his contributions to biotechnology, and tennis player Ričardas Berankis, who received the Medal of the Order "For Merits to Lithuania." Nausėda said each recipient contributed to strengthening the Lithuanian state and "left an indelible mark not only on the pages of history, but also in people's hearts." The ceremony marked the smallest number of recipients in the past five years, with previous years seeing more than 50 people receive medals and orders.
