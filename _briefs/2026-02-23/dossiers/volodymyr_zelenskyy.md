### Zelenskyy sanctions 225 Russian shadow fleet captains

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy signed decrees imposing sanctions on 225 captains of vessels in Russia's shadow fleet that export oil products, according to presidential decrees. Ukrainian authorities said monitoring in the Black, Red and Baltic Seas revealed these captains commanded vessels transporting oil in breach of EU, G7 and other international sanctions. The sanctioned captains are citizens of 11 countries including Russia, India and the Philippines. Zelenskyy also imposed separate sanctions on 46 Russian citizens, two Iranian citizens and 44 Russian companies servicing Russia's military industrial complex.
