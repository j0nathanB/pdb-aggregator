### Uruguay's Orsi attends concert marking fortress liberation bicentennial

ROCHA, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi attended a free concert by Uruguayan rock band No Te Va Gustar on Monday to commemorate the 200th anniversary of the liberation of the Santa Teresa and San Miguel fortresses from Brazilian rule. An estimated 20,000 people attended the event at the Santa Teresa Fortress, where the band presented their new album "Florece en el caos," according to the presidency's website. The celebration marked the bicentennial of the 1825 liberation of the fortresses in what was then the Brazilian-controlled Cisplatine Province, led by militia leader Leonardo Olivera. Defense Minister Sandra Lazo and Education and Culture Minister José Carlos Mahía accompanied Orsi at the event, the presidency said.

### Uruguay's Orsi faces contract disputes over naval, water projects

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi's government is confronting challenges over two major infrastructure contracts, with opposition lawmakers calling for investigations into his administration's handling of both deals. The Court of Accounts voted 4-3 to reject Orsi's renegotiation of a water treatment plant contract, citing substantial changes to the original terms signed by the previous government, Court President Francisco Gallinal said. Separately, National Party Senator Martín Lema proposed creating an investigative commission into Orsi's decision to terminate a contract with Spanish shipyard Cardama for two ocean patrol vessels, alleging the government sabotaged the deal. Presidential Deputy Secretary Jorge Díaz defended the contract termination, citing irregular guarantees provided by Cardama.

### Uruguayan President Orsi ranks 12th in Latin America poll

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi ranked 12th among 18 Latin American leaders in a February 2026 approval poll, according to Argentine consulting firm CB. The survey showed Orsi with 40.7% positive approval, with 40.7% of respondents rating him positively compared to 56.5% negatively. El Salvador's Nayib Bukele topped the ranking with 72.6% approval, followed by Mexico's Claudia Sheinbaum at 68.5%. The poll surveyed 2,050 Uruguayans between Feb. 10-15 with a margin of error of plus or minus 2.2 percentage points, CB said.

### Israel views Uruguay's Orsi as balanced amid regional tensions

JERUSALEM, Israel — Israeli officials said they view Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi as maintaining a more balanced approach to Middle East conflicts compared to other regional leaders, according to sources from Israel's Foreign Ministry. ### Uruguay signs agricultural research agreements with China during presidential visit

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Uruguay's National Agricultural Research Institute signed three cooperation agreements with Chinese institutions during President Yamandú Orsi's official visit to China, focusing on soybean research, grassland studies and scientific mobility. Institute President Miguel Sierra said one agreement with China's Academy of Agricultural Sciences targets soybean genetic improvements for higher productivity, protein content and climate resistance. The other agreements involve grassland digitalization research with Qingdao University and researcher exchange programs with China's Natural Sciences Foundation. Sierra said the partnerships aim to strengthen bilateral ties in agricultural science and innovation.

### Uruguay considers symbolic humanitarian aid to Cuba

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Uruguay's government is evaluating sending symbolic humanitarian aid consisting of powdered milk and rice to Cuba, Foreign Minister Mario Lubetkin said Thursday. "Always when we can, in some way, give a hand, we try to give a hand," Lubetker said during a press conference following a parliamentary hearing.
