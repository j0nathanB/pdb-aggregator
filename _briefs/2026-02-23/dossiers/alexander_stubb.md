## Top Stories

### Finnish President Stubb to meet Macron in Paris

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb will travel to Paris on Monday to meet with French President Emmanuel Macron at the Élysée Palace, the Office of the President of the Republic of Finland said. The presidents will discuss bilateral relations between Finland and France, Russia's war of aggression against Ukraine and ongoing peace negotiations, as well as the current security policy situation, according to the presidential office. The meeting comes ahead of Tuesday's fourth anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine. The latest round of talks between Russia, Ukraine and the United States ended without a breakthrough Wednesday in Geneva, though the main U.S. negotiator said talks could resume within three weeks.

### Finnish President Stubb reveals diplomatic tactics with Trump

HELSINKI, Finland — President Alexander Stubb has disclosed details about his behind-the-scenes diplomatic approach with former U.S. President Donald Trump, according to Finnish media reports. Stubb revealed in a 2017 book that he learned diplomacy requires understanding personal relationships rather than just political theory, stating that international politics needs "more of a psychotherapist than an international politics expert." The Finnish president has maintained regular contact with world leaders through direct phone calls and text messages, including with Trump during icebreaker negotiations, though a joint text message with Norway's prime minister requesting a call with Trump went unanswered and resulted in an angry letter from Trump to Norway.

HELSINKI, Finland — President Alexander Stubb met with the Finnish Parliament's Foreign Affairs Committee on Tuesday to discuss the current foreign and security policy situation, including Russia's war against Ukraine and ongoing peace negotiations, the Office of the President said.
