### Moldova President Warns Citizens Against Phone, Online Scams

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu warned Moldovan citizens about increasing phone and online fraud cases, saying criminals are using sophisticated methods including artificial intelligence to create fake videos. "Only thieves ask for money, passwords or propose quick gains by phone or internet," Sandu said in a public warning issued Thursday. The president said scammers pose as relatives, bank employees, investors or police officers to gain victims' trust. Moldova's National Financial Market Commission also warned that fraudsters are using images of public figures, including Sandu, to promote fake investment platforms.

### Moldova President Revokes Pardon for Man Linked to Assassination Plot

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu revoked a 2022 presidential pardon for Nicolae Andrei Șepeli after new information emerged linking him to a criminal group involved in an assassination plot targeting Ukrainian officials, the presidential office said Tuesday. Șepeli, who was convicted in Russia for drug trafficking and transferred to Moldova to serve his sentence, had been pardoned in April 2022 following recommendations from the presidential pardons commission. Moldovan and Ukrainian authorities detained 11 Moldovan citizens this week in connection with a Russian-directed scheme to assassinate high-profile Ukrainian figures, with Șepeli identified as the alleged organizer of the network.

### Moldova's Sandu launches national dialogue on children's online safety

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu launched a national dialogue Friday on protecting children in the digital space, bringing together teachers, psychologists, students, parents and mental health specialists. The first meeting addressed risks associated with social media and screen use, as well as solutions that can be applied in families, schools and public policies, Sandu said on Facebook. "The way we manage children's relationship with technology will shape tomorrow's generations," Sandu stated. Online safety ranks among the presidency's priorities for 2026, with authorities analyzing the possibility of limiting children's and adolescents' access to social networks.

### 37 Presidential Decrees Challenged in Courts During Sandu's Term

CHISINAU, Moldova — Thirty-seven presidential decrees issued by President Maia Sandu have been challenged in courts during her five-year term, according to a report. The lawsuits increased from three in 2021 to 12 in 2025, with most cases involving judicial appointments and dismissals, the report said. Fourteen cases concerned judiciary appointments or dismissals, while other challenges involved citizenship denials, political asylum decisions, and state award revocations, according to the data.
