### Sheinbaum calls for calm after cartel leader death sparks violence

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum called for calm following the death of cartel leader Nemesio Oseguera Cervantes, known as "El Mencho," during a security operation in Tapalpa, Jalisco, that sparked road blockades and vehicle burnings across multiple states. ### Sheinbaum proposes constitutional reform to cap executive pensions

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum announced she will send a constitutional reform proposal to the Senate on Monday to eliminate what she called "exorbitant" pensions for former high-level government officials, capping them at 50% of the president's salary. Anti-Corruption Secretary Raquel Buenrostro said the government identified pensioners receiving between 100,000 and one million pesos monthly from state entities including the defunct Luz y Fuerza del Centro, Pemex, and CFE. Sheinbaum said the reform would save approximately 5 billion pesos annually, which would be redirected to social welfare programs.

### Sheinbaum Proposes Constitutional Reform Capping High-Level Pensions

### Sheinbaum's electoral reform draft proposes party-initiated popular consultations

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — A draft of President Claudia Sheinbaum's electoral reform proposal would allow political parties to request popular consultations for the first time, according to documents reviewed by Mexican media. The proposal, set to be presented to Congress on Feb. 24, would also reduce the required participation threshold for binding results from 40% to 33% of voters, Deputy Ricardo Monreal said. Current law only permits the president, Congress chambers, or 2% of registered voters to request such consultations. Monreal confirmed the document's authenticity but said it does not represent the final version that will be submitted to lawmakers.
