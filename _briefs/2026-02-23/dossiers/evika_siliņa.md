# 🇱🇻 Latvia
## Evika Siliņa, Prime Minister
_Week of February 16, 2026_

> Latvian Prime Minister Evika Siliņa announced a 10 million euro allocation for Ukraine weapons purchases this week while adding a military cooperation advisor to her office, signaling an expansion of the country's defense coordination capabilities. Siliņa also proposed rebranding the international Ukraine support coalition from 'Coalition of the Willing' to 'coalition of strength' during discussions with Western allies. Domestically, her coalition government agreed to increase housing benefit coefficients and she proposed state support for heating bills as Latvia balances international military commitments with domestic economic pressures.

## International

### Latvia allocates 10 million euros for Ukraine weapons purchases

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa said Latvia's government allocated 10 million euros to support Ukraine through NATO's PURL fund framework. The money will be used to purchase weapons and equipment to help Ukraine defend itself, with procurement from the United States as a strategic partner, Siliņa stated. The contribution represents 0.25% of Latvia's gross domestic product, confirming Latvia's support for Ukraine, she said. The PURL initiative aims to facilitate the purchase and delivery of U.S. defense products and services to Ukraine using donations from allies and partners, the Defense Ministry said.

### Latvia Officials Skip Paralympics Attendance in Person

RIGA, Latvia — Latvian Prime Minister Evika Siliņa will not attend the Milan-Cortina Winter Paralympics in person, representatives of her office said. President Edgars Rinkēvičs and Education Minister Dace Melbārde also will not attend the games, which run March 6-15, their representatives confirmed. Parliamentary deputy Zane Skujiņa-Rubene will lead Latvia's Paralympic delegation, a role she has held since 2016. Seven Latvian athletes will compete in the games, including wheelchair curling teams and mixed doubles representatives who will serve as flag bearers.

### Latvia PM suggests renaming Ukraine support coalition

PARIS, France — Latvian Prime Minister Evika Siliņa suggested renaming the "Coalition of the Willing" to the "coalition of strength" during a meeting with leaders supporting Ukraine, organized by French President Emmanuel Macron on March 27. The proposal referenced the need to achieve peace in Ukraine through force, as pointed out by U.S. President Donald Trump, according to a Latvian government release. The suggestion was not immediately adopted by other participants, and the official release continued to refer to the original name. The meeting brought together leaders from nearly 30 countries, along with NATO and European Union representatives, to discuss increasing support for Ukraine and ensuring long-term peace in Europe.

## Domestic

### Latvia PM adds military cooperation advisor to office

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa added Colonel Raivis Melnis as an external advisor on military cooperation matters to her office starting Friday, Feb. 13, the Cabinet of Ministers said. Siliņa said Melnis brings extensive experience in defense capability planning and international military cooperation. The colonel currently serves as the Defense Ministry's representative in Ukraine, according to the announcement. External advisors to the Prime Minister's office work without compensation.

### Latvia coalition agrees to increase housing benefit coefficients

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa said Latvia's governing coalition agreed to increase calculation coefficients for housing benefits to help households cope with rising heating costs during the cold winter. The changes will expand the number of households eligible for benefits from January through April, with the government planning to compensate municipalities for 50% of increased expenses, Siliņa said. The Welfare Ministry prepared draft legislation to implement the changes, which the cabinet plans to review this week, she said. Siliņa said funding will come from emergency reserves and potentially from the ministry's budget, as officials typically expect higher demand for state support during winter months.

*Sources: (Delfi Latvija [1](https://www.delfi.lv/193/politics/120107055/koalicija-vienojas-atbalstit-majsaimniecibas-palielina-majokla-pabalsta-aprekina-koeficientu))*

### Latvia PM proposes state support for heating bills

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa said the government will decide on housing support measures following January's cold weather that caused heating bills to surge for many residents. Siliņa said the state has offered fair assistance to municipalities on heating bill issues, proposing to cover half of the increased costs municipalities must pay in housing allowances. The Cabinet is scheduled to decide Wednesday on amendments to increase housing allowance calculation coefficients for the winter period from January 1 to April 30, the State Chancellery said.
