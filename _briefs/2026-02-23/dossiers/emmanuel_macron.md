### France seeks major Rafale jet deal during Macron India visit

NEW DELHI, India — France is preparing to negotiate a major fighter jet contract with India as President Emmanuel Macron prepares for a state visit from February 17-19, according to French officials. India approved the purchase of 114 Rafale fighter jets from Dassault Aviation for 3.25 trillion rupees ($30.2 billion), though the deal requires further technical and commercial negotiations, a presidential adviser said during a briefing. The Elysée called the approval a "very important milestone" toward a "historic" contract for the French aerospace manufacturer. ### Macron asks Trump to lift sanctions on Breton, Guillou

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron wrote to U.S. President Donald Trump asking him to lift sanctions on former European Commissioner Thierry Breton and International Criminal Court judge Nicolas Guillou, according to letter excerpts seen by Agence France-Presse. ### Macron criticizes Meloni over comments on French activist death

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron on Thursday criticized Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni for commenting on the killing of a French far-right activist, telling reporters that nationalists "are always the first ones to comment on what's happening in other countries." Macron's remarks came after Meloni called the death of Quentin Deranque, 23, who was beaten by hard-left activists in Lyon, "a wound for all of Europe." ### ECB's Lagarde may quit early to help Macron pick successor

FRANKFURT, Germany — European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde plans to leave her position before her term ends in October 2027, ahead of France's 2027 presidential election, to allow outgoing President Emmanuel Macron a role in selecting her successor, the Financial Times reported Wednesday. The move would prevent France's far-right National Rally party from influencing the selection if it wins the presidency, according to the report citing a person familiar with the matter. An ECB spokesperson said Lagarde "is totally focused on her mission and has not taken any decision regarding the end of her term," departing from earlier guidance that she was "determined to complete her term." Potential successors include former Dutch central bank chief Klaas Knot, Bank for International Settlements General Manager Pablo Hernandez de Cos and German Bundesbank president Joachim Nagel, according to analysts.

### Macron Proposes European Bonds Despite France's Rising Debt

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron proposed the creation of European bonds to fund future investments, despite France's public debt reaching 3.482 trillion euros by September 2025, according to economic commentator Pierre-Antoine Delhommais writing in Le Point. Macron said in an interview with Le Monde that "it's time to launch a common debt capacity for future spending," Delhommais reported. The proposal comes as France's debt has increased by 1.201 trillion euros since Macron took office in June 2017, making it the eurozone's highest deficit country. Delhommais criticized the timing, noting that such bonds would benefit from Germany's implicit guarantee and lower borrowing costs.

### Macron advisor Legendre named first woman to head Arab World Institute

PARIS, France — Anne-Claire Legendre, President Emmanuel Macron's diplomatic advisor for North Africa and the Middle East, was unanimously selected Tuesday to lead the Arab World Institute, becoming the first woman to head the cultural institution. The 46-year-old career diplomat replaces Jacques Lang, who resigned amid scrutiny over his connections to Jeffrey Epstein, French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot said in a letter proposing her candidacy. Legendre said her role is to "bring back serenity" to the institute and "restore public confidence." The board of directors, composed of seven Arab country ambassadors and French Foreign Ministry appointees, approved her appointment unanimously.
