### Estonia PM announces wind farm incentives for municipalities

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal announced Thursday that the climate ministry is developing new incentive measures to encourage wind farm construction in local municipalities. ### Estonian President Allegedly Advised PM to Replace Foreign Minister

TALLINN, Estonia — Rumors circulated in Estonia's parliament that President Alar Karis advised Prime Minister Kristen Michal to replace Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna during a meeting last Wednesday, opposition leader Urmas Reinsalu said. Neither the president's office nor the government confirmed or denied the claim, with presidential spokesperson Epp-Mare Kukemelk stating the meeting focused on Ukraine assistance and "anything else would be an exaggeration." ### Poll shows majority disapprove of Estonian PM's performance

TALLINN, Estonia — A majority of Estonian citizens remain dissatisfied with Prime Minister Kristen Michal's performance, according to a monthly poll conducted by the Institute of Social Research and polling firm Norstat Estonia.
