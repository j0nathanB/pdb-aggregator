## Top Stories

### France accuses Russia of Epstein smear campaign against Macron

PARIS, France — French authorities said they detected a Russian disinformation operation that falsely linked President Emmanuel Macron to the Jeffrey Epstein scandal, a government source told Agence France-Presse on Friday. The campaign involved creating a fake website impersonating the news outlet France-Soir and publishing fabricated articles connecting Macron to the convicted sex offender, the source said. France's Viginum agency, which monitors foreign interference operations, attributed the effort to the Russian Storm-1516 network with "high degree of confidence," according to the source. The disinformation was then amplified on social media platform X through accounts monitored by French authorities.

## International

### France adopts 2026 budget boosting military spending

PARIS, France — France adopted its delayed 2026 budget Monday, clearing the way for higher military spending promised by President Emmanuel Macron to confront threats linked to Russia's war in Ukraine and Middle East conflicts. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu used special constitutional power to pass the bill without a parliamentary vote after lawmakers proved unable to reach a compromise. The Defense Ministry will receive an additional 6.7 billion euros ($7.9 billion) compared to 2025, funding a new nuclear-powered attack submarine, 362 armored vehicles and new surface-to-air missiles. The government is targeting a budget deficit of 5% of gross domestic product, down from 5.4% in 2025.

### France to send fertility letters to all 29-year-olds

PARIS, France — The French government will send letters to all 29-year-old citizens this summer as part of a new fertility plan aimed at boosting the country's declining birth rate, Health Minister Stéphanie Rist said Thursday. The initiative includes 16 measures to combat infertility, including creating a national portal on reproductive health and strengthening sex education in schools, Rist stated. France recorded more deaths than births for the first time since World War II in 2025, with only 645,000 babies born compared to 651,000 deaths, according to INSEE statistics. The plan supports President Emmanuel Macron's promised "demographic rearmament" announced in 2024, officials said.

### Macron meets farmers ahead of agriculture fair visit

PARIS, France — President Emmanuel Macron met with agricultural organizations at the Elysee Palace on Monday and visited a farm in Haute-Saone on Tuesday, seeking to reassure farmers ahead of the upcoming Agriculture Fair. The meetings come as agricultural anger persists over issues including the Mercosur trade deal and international competition, despite France's rejection of the trade agreement. The FNSEA union described Monday's exchange as "correct" but lacking ambition, while the Peasant Confederation called it "disappointing." Macron said France faces "an enormous agricultural challenge" and promised "very concrete responses" in coming weeks.
