### Moldova President Meets Baltic Parliament Leaders on EU Integration

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu met with the parliamentary speakers of Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania to discuss Moldova's European Union integration process, the presidency said. The meeting with Estonian Riigikogu President Lauri Hussar, Latvian Saeima President Daiga Mieriņa and Lithuanian Seimas President Juozas Olekas marked the first joint official visit by all three Baltic legislative leaders to Moldova. CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said most Moldovan citizens now earn higher incomes than in previous years, even accounting for inflation, though economic growth remains constrained by the war in Ukraine. ### Moldova's Sandu defends social media regulation against US report

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said freedom of expression applies to people, not fake accounts, responding to a U.S. House Judiciary Committee report suggesting the European Union pressured social media platforms to censor content during elections in several countries including Moldova. "Freedom of expression is for people, not for fake accounts, not for bots," Sandu said during a Feb. 5 television interview on TV8. The interim report published Feb. 3 alleged EU institutions created pressure mechanisms on social networks to moderate political discourse, potentially influencing elections. Sandu said Moldova has evidence of fake account operations and defended the need for platform regulation.

### Moldovan Socialist Deputy Explains Why He Didn't Applaud Sandu Speech

CHISINAU, Moldova — Socialist deputy Igor Dodon said he refused to applaud or stand during President Maia Sandu's speech to the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe because he disagreed with her message. ### Sandu says former health minister chose to leave government

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said former Health Minister Ala Nemerenco chose not to continue working in state institutions after her mandate expired, according to statements made during a Feb. 5 television interview.
