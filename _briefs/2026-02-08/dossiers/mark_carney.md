## Top Stories

### Canada drops EV sales quota amid auto industry pressure

VAUGHAN, Ontario — Prime Minister Mark Carney announced Thursday that Canada will scrap its electric vehicle sales mandate, replacing the controversial policy with stricter tailpipe emissions standards and new purchase incentives. The government will eliminate the Electric Vehicle Availability Standard that required 100% zero-emission vehicle sales by 2035, responding to pressure from auto manufacturers and provincial leaders who called the timeline unrealistic. Carney said the new approach "focuses on the results that matter to Canadians, while avoiding placing undue burdens on the Canadian auto industry." The revised strategy aims for 75% EV adoption by 2035 and includes $2.3 billion in purchase incentives for vehicles under $50,000.

### Carney praises Harper at portrait unveiling ceremony

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney praised former Conservative leader Stephen Harper's fiscal record and crisis management during Tuesday's unveiling of Harper's official portrait on Parliament Hill. Harper, who served as prime minister from 2006 to 2015, called for unity between political parties against "external forces that threaten our independence" and internal separatist movements in Alberta and Quebec. The ceremony featured remarks from both leaders, with Carney noting Harper's pragmatic approach during the 2008 financial crisis and his advice over the past year. The portrait was painted by Canadian artist Phil Richards and attended by about 300 guests including former prime minister Jean Chrétien and Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre.

### Carney announces Luxembourg visit, diplomatic appointments

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that Luxembourg Prime Minister Luc Frieden will visit Canada from Feb. 7-9, 2026, to discuss strengthening partnerships in manufacturing, aerospace and financial services, his office said Tuesday. ### Carney names Olszewski as Ministerial Lead for Jasper recovery

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that Eleanor Olszewski will serve as Ministerial Lead for Jasper, coordinating federal recovery efforts following devastating 2024 wildfires that destroyed nearly one-third of the Alberta town. Olszewski, who serves as Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience, will work with provincial, municipal and Indigenous partners to oversee reconstruction, the Prime Minister's Office said. The federal government has committed more than $383 million to recovery efforts, including interim housing for over 350 families and debris removal from all affected lots. The July 2024 wildfires were the largest to impact Jasper National Park in more than a century.
