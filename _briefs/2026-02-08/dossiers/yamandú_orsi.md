### Uruguay's Orsi says police warned him of tunnel robbery plot

BEIJING, China — Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi said police informed him more than a month ago about a possible tunnel robbery plot targeting banks in Montevideo's Old City, praising investigators' patience in the operation. Speaking from China during an official visit, Orsi said he received a call at 3 a.m. local time informing him that police had successfully thwarted the attempted heist. ### Opposition leader criticizes Orsi administration for dismantling predecessor's projects

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Opposition National Party leader Álvaro Delgado criticized President Yamandú Orsi's administration Saturday, saying it causes him "great sadness that a government dedicates itself to dismantling what the previous one did." Delgado said the current government has a "refoundational intention" to dismantle projects from former President Luis Lacalle Pou's administration, calling the approach "capricious" and "selfish." He cited the cancellation of the Neptune Project in Arazatí, a sanitation project for 190,000 Uruguayans in 61 interior localities, and the rescission of a contract with Cardama for two oceanic patrol boats.

### Uruguay's Orsi says 'things to resolve' before new port terminal

SHANGHAI, China — Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi said Thursday there are "many things to resolve before" considering installation of a new specialized terminal at Montevideo's port, following a court ruling that struck down restrictions on new terminals. Speaking during a state visit to China, Orsi said his administration will respect existing contracts while working to improve port operations through dialogue with operators. The Administrative Court partially upheld a challenge by port operator Montecon, striking down a clause that prevented new terminal permits until 2081.
