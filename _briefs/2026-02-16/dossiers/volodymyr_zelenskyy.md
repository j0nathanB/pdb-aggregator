## Top Stories

### Zelenskyy denies plans to announce elections on invasion anniversary

KYIV, Ukraine — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on Wednesday denied a Financial Times report that he planned to announce presidential elections and a peace referendum on Feb. 24, the fourth anniversary of Russia's invasion. "Even if there was a goal or appropriate steps were taken to bring certain elections closer, I believe that it would be an absolutely stupid idea to choose such a date to talk about politics," Zelenskyy said. The Financial Times had reported that Ukraine began planning presidential elections alongside a referendum on a prospective peace deal with Russia, citing Ukrainian and European officials. Zelenskyy said Ukraine would only hold elections after receiving appropriate security guarantees from the U.S. and other Western allies.
