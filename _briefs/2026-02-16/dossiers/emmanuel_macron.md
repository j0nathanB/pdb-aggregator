### Macron Names Amélie de Montchalin to Head Court of Accounts

PARIS, France — President Emmanuel Macron selected Public Accounts Minister Amélie de Montchalin to lead France's Court of Accounts, the country's financial oversight body, government spokesperson Maud Bregeon said Wednesday. ### Macron's approval rating rises while PM Lecornu hits low

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron's approval rating increased by two percentage points to 20%, while Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu's popularity dropped to its lowest level since taking office, according to a monthly Ipsos BVA-CESI poll published by La Tribune Dimanche.
