### Lula inaugurates emergency center at Rio federal hospital

RIO DE JANEIRO, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva inaugurated a new 24-hour emergency center for children and adults at the Federal Cardoso Fontes Hospital in Jacarepaguá on Sunday. The Brazilian government invested 100 million reais ($16.7 million) to modernize the facility as part of a restructuring partnership between the Health Ministry and Rio's municipal health department signed in December 2024, officials said. Lula criticized the historical political use of federal hospitals in Rio, saying they "were always used as bargaining chips in electoral campaigns," according to local media reports.

### Brazil's Lula to attend Rio carnival tribute amid legal challenges

### Brazil's Lula signs law providing free cooking gas to poor families

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva signed legislation Friday making the "Gás do Povo" (People's Gas) program a permanent public policy providing free liquefied petroleum gas to low-income Brazilian families. The law establishes vouchers that eligible families can use to refill gas canisters at no cost at authorized retailers nationwide, according to the Ministry of Social Development. The program targets families earning up to half a minimum wage per person and prioritizes women victims of domestic violence and traditional communities including indigenous and quilombola populations. Government data shows 94.14% of the 950,800 families currently benefiting from the program are headed by women.

### Brazil's Lula signs decree modernizing worker meal voucher program

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva signed Decree No. 12,712 modernizing Brazil's Worker Food Program, which limits transaction fees charged to establishments accepting meal vouchers and reduces payment processing times for merchants. The new rules cap transaction fees at 3.6% and require operators to transfer payments to businesses within 15 days, down from more than 30 days previously, Lula said Tuesday during the decree signing. The government estimates up to 22 million Brazilians could benefit from the changes, which aim to encourage small businesses to accept meal vouchers while expanding options for consumers.

### Lula announces $250 million investment in Butantan Institute expansion

SAO PAULO, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva announced Monday a 1.4 billion real ($250 million) investment to expand vaccine and serum production capacity at the Butantan Institute. The funding will build two new factories and modernize two existing units at Latin America's largest vaccine producer, Lula said during a ceremony attended by Vice President Geraldo Alckmin and Health Minister Alexandre Padilha.
