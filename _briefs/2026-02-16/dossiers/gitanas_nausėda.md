## Top Stories

### Lithuania President Says Taiwan Decides Own Representative Office Name

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said Thursday that Taiwan, not Lithuania, determines the name of its representative office in Vilnius, responding to Prime Minister Ingrida Šimonytė's suggestion the office could be renamed using Taipei instead of Taiwan. "This is Taiwan's representative office and primarily the Taiwan representative office itself establishes one name or another," Nausėda said before an informal European Council meeting in Belgium. Lithuania has faced diplomatic tensions with China since 2021 when it allowed Taiwan to open the office using the name Taiwan rather than Taipei, which Beijing considers a violation of its "One China" policy. Taiwan's foreign ministry said it has not discussed changing the office's name with Vilnius.

### Lithuania President Awards State Honors to Scientists, Athletes

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda awarded state honors to 35 individuals Monday on Lithuania's State Restoration Day, recognizing achievements in science, medicine, sports, culture, diplomacy and national security. Biochemist Virginijus Šikšnys received the Grand Cross of the Order of the Lithuanian Grand Duke Gediminas for contributions to biotechnology development, while tennis player Ričardas Berankis and children's television host Bernadeta Stankevičienė were among other recipients. Nausėda said each honoree contributed to strengthening the Lithuanian state and "left an indelible mark not only on the pages of history, but also in people's hearts." The ceremony marked the smallest number of recipients in five years, with previous ceremonies typically honoring more than 50 people.

### Lithuanian President Links EU Competitiveness to National Security

BRUSSELS, Belgium — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda said Thursday that European Union competitiveness is a matter of national security, calling for expanded free trade agreements during a European Council meeting. Nausėda said the EU should pursue trade deals with Australia, the Philippines and other regional countries, describing agreements with MERCOSUR nations and India as "very good decisions." The president said export market diversification helped Lithuania achieve 3% GDP growth, faster than other countries. EU leaders agreed on the need to boost Europe's economy but disagreed on specific measures, with France and Germany showing public unity despite previous disputes over procurement preferences and common debt.

### Lithuanian President Mourns Death of National Arts Prize Winner

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda expressed condolences following the death of flutist, conductor and pedagogue Algirdas Vizgirda, a recipient of Lithuania's National Culture and Arts Prize, according to a statement from the presidential office. Nausėda said the country lost "an outstanding personality of Lithuanian musical culture" whose life was inseparable from flute art, chamber music and training generations of performers. The president highlighted Vizgirda's contributions to baroque music tradition, his founding of the chamber ensemble "Musica humana" and the international chamber music festival "Kuršių nerija." Vizgirda was awarded the Knight's Cross of the Order of the Grand Duke Gediminas of Lithuania and the National Culture and Arts Prize.
