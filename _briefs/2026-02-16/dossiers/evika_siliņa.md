### Latvia PM Siliņa explains unique name origin in TV interview

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa explained the origin of her uncommon first name during a television interview, saying her grandmother created it to avoid duplicating a neighbor's name. ### Latvia Officials Criticize Olympic Committee Decision on Ukrainian Athlete

RIGA, Latvia — Latvian Prime Minister Evika Siliņa criticized the International Olympic Committee's decision to disqualify Ukrainian skeleton athlete Vladyslav Heraskevych for refusing to change his helmet displaying images of more than 20 Ukrainian athletes and coaches killed in Russia's war. "Showing remembrance is not political propaganda, but humanity," Siliņa said. President Edgars Rinkēvičs and Foreign Minister Baiba Braže also condemned the decision, with Education Minister Dace Melbārde sending an official letter to Olympic leadership expressing dissatisfaction with the ruling.

### Latvia PM considers agriculture minister's dismissal amid timber probe

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa said she has not ruled out requesting the resignation of Agriculture Minister Armands Krauze following a service inspection report on state support for timber companies. Krauze said Monday that if the prime minister demands his resignation, the entire government should fall. The government will review the inspection report Tuesday, with representatives from the Prosecutor General's Office and State Audit Office participating in the cabinet meeting, Siliņa said. The dispute centers on long-term timber contracts that the Competition Council said create unfair market advantages for certain companies.
