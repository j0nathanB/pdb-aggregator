### Estonian PM meets president, discusses foreign policy coordination

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal met with President Alar Karis on Thursday to discuss foreign policy coordination before traveling to Brussels for an informal European Council summit. Michal said the meeting was friendly and focused on ensuring Estonia's small team works together in one direction on foreign policy matters. "We have no illusions about Russia. Putin has not changed his goals," Michal told Delfi Estonia after the meeting. The prime minister emphasized that despite different backgrounds and approaches, he and the president are aligned on foreign policy, particularly regarding continued support for Ukraine.

### Estonian PM says MAGA approach would favor Orban over allies

MUNICH, Germany — Estonian Prime Minister Kristen Michal said a MAGA approach to foreign policy would favor leaders like Hungary's Viktor Orban over traditional allies, according to Estonian media reports from the Munich Security Conference. Michal stated that U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio emphasized Europe must provide greater contributions to its own defense rather than requiring care from America. The prime minister said the beginning of this year appears better for transatlantic relations than last year, following meetings with German Chancellor Friedrich Merz and discussions in the Berlin Group supporting Ukraine.
