### Tusk says Poland won't join US-backed Peace Council

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said Wednesday that Poland will not participate in a U.S.-backed Peace Council focused on Gaza reconstruction, citing concerns about the body's structure and goals. Tusk said Poland's ambassador to Washington received an invitation for the Feb. 19 inaugural meeting but added that "in current circumstances, Poland will not join the Peace Council's work." The prime minister also criticized President Karol Nawrocki for convening a National Security Council meeting to discuss allegations about parliament speaker Włodzimierz Czarzasty's business contacts with a Russian citizen, calling it an inappropriate politicization of security matters.

### Tusk calls opposition 'enemies of independence' over SAFE vote

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said opposition parties that voted against Poland's participation in the European Union's SAFE defense funding program are "enemies of Polish independence," following a parliamentary vote that approved the legislation. The Sejm passed the bill 236 votes in favor, with the ruling coalition supporting the measure while the opposition Law and Justice party and Confederation voted against it. The SAFE program would provide Poland with 43.7 billion euros in low-interest loans for defense spending, with officials saying 80 percent of funds would go to Polish defense companies. President Karol Nawrocki's office said it is preparing amendments to the legislation, leaving uncertain whether he will sign or veto the bill.

### Polish President Questions Tusk's Coalition Partner Over Russian Ties

WARSAW, Poland — Polish President Andrzej Duda's administration questioned Prime Minister Donald Tusk's decision to include Parliament Speaker Włodzimierz Czarzasty in his coalition government, citing concerns over Czarzasty's alleged business connections with Russia. Presidential Minister Karol Rabenda said Wednesday that Duda wants to know whether Tusk's government will take action regarding media reports about Czarzasty's ties. Rabenda noted that Czarzasty has avoided submitting required personal security questionnaires for years and automatically gained access to classified information through his parliamentary role. The National Security Council will discuss the matter during its Wednesday session.

### Polish PM Tusk Forms Team to Investigate Epstein Case

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk has established a special team to investigate Polish connections to the Jeffrey Epstein case, following the U.S. Justice Department's release of approximately 3 million pages of documents from the deceased financier's files in late January.
