## Top Stories

### Mexico offers to serve as air bridge for Cuba aid

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum said Mexico could serve as an air bridge for other nations to deliver humanitarian aid to Cuba if the island requests such assistance. "If Cuba asks for it, Mexico — due to its geographic location — could open an air bridge so other nations can deliver humanitarian aid to the island," Sheinbaum said during her morning press conference at the National Palace. The president said flights to Cuba remain open and aircraft needing fuel "can come to Mexico to refuel; they have to pay, of course." Mexico recently sent two naval vessels carrying more than 814 tons of humanitarian aid to Havana and is preparing a second shipment.

### Sheinbaum announces new cinema law, vaccine production agreement

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum announced two new legislative initiatives Friday, including a federal cinema law requiring 10 percent of theater screenings be Mexican films and protections for voice actors against artificial intelligence cloning. Sheinbaum said the cinema law, developed with industry stakeholders, guarantees state funding for Mexican film production and mandates streaming platforms create dedicated sections for national content. ### Sheinbaum announces tax incentives for Mexican film industry
