### Sandu warns Gagauz language faces extinction in Moldova

COMRAT, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said the Gagauz language is in danger of disappearing and called for strategic efforts to preserve it during a speech at Comrat State University's 35th anniversary celebration. Speaking in Russian and Gagauz, Sandu said the autonomous region has predominantly used a language that is not threatened with extinction while the native tongue has been heard less frequently. She said preserving the language depends primarily on the community using it daily in streets, shops, families and schools. The Education Ministry launched development of a national plan for teaching and promoting the Gagauz language in the autonomous region.

### Moldova Parliament overrides president's veto on lawyer law changes

CHISINAU, Moldova — Moldova's Parliament voted to override President Maia Sandu's refusal to sign amendments to the country's lawyer law, accepting her objections and removing controversial provisions about the Bar Association's governing bodies. The revised law now contains only provisions transposing European Union directives that would allow EU-qualified lawyers to practice in Moldova once the country joins the bloc, Parliament said in a statement. Sandu had refused to sign the original July 10 legislation, stating the amendments did not provide adequate solutions for structural problems affecting Moldova's legal profession. The president had called for comprehensive reform involving legal professionals, civil society and European partners.

### Moldova President Names New State Protection Service Chief

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu signed a decree appointing Alexandru Harea as director of the State Protection and Guard Service, according to official documents. Harea, who has over 20 years of experience in Moldova's Defense Ministry and participated in UN peacekeeping missions in Ivory Coast and Syria, will assume the position Feb. 18, 2026. The appointment comes three months after Sandu dismissed former director Vasile Popa and his deputy in November 2025 based on their resignation requests. The National Integrity Authority has initiated a wealth control investigation into Popa regarding potential violations in asset declarations from 2020-2024.

### Moldova considers social media age restrictions for minors

CHISINAU, Moldova — Moldova's Education Minister Dan Perciun said the country could adopt regulations restricting minors' access to social media by the end of this year, following models implemented in Australia and France. President Maia Sandu announced she will begin consultations with parents, teachers, doctors and youth representatives to find solutions for children's digital safety, according to statements made Monday. Perciun said social media companies would be responsible for verifying users' ages if restrictions are implemented. The discussions come amid growing concerns about digital addiction and harmful content exposure among children.

### Moldova Presidential Adviser Moves to Parliament Role

CHISINAU, Moldova — Igor Zaharov, former communications adviser to President Maia Sandu, has taken a new position as foreign policy adviser to Parliament Speaker Igor Grosu, according to NewsMaker.
