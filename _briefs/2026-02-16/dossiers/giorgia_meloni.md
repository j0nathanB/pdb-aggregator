# 🇮🇹 Italy
## Giorgia Meloni, Prime Minister
_Week of February 9, 2026_

> Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni backed European Union eurobonds and discussed enhanced cooperation with Germany at this week's EU summit, while her government approved controversial migration legislation allowing naval blockades of vessels carrying migrants. Meloni also promoted her Africa-focused Mattei Plan at an international summit and accepted Italy's observer role on a Gaza oversight board proposed by U.S. President-elect Donald Trump. The week highlighted Meloni's dual approach of deepening EU integration while maintaining hardline domestic policies on migration.

## Top Stories

### Meloni tells summit Mattei Plan opens new Africa chapter

ADDIS ABABA, Ethiopia — Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said her government has mobilized billions of euros in public and private resources for Africa through the Mattei Plan during the second Italy-Africa summit. Meloni told African leaders the plan represents a "paradigm shift" away from "predatory" and "paternalistic" approaches to the continent, according to government statements. The prime minister announced new debt suspension clauses for African nations facing climate crises and said the plan now covers 14 African countries, up from nine originally. Meloni addressed the African Union Assembly of Heads of State and Government, calling for cooperation based on equality rather than charity.

### Meloni backs eurobonds, discusses Italy-Germany cooperation at EU summit

BRUSSELS, Belgium — Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said she supports eurobonds despite acknowledging the issue remains divisive within the European Union, speaking at an informal EU summit on competitiveness. Meloni confirmed the existence of renewed Italy-Germany coordination but said it should not exclude other countries, stating that European alliances operate with "variable geometry." The summit at Alden Biesen castle brought together 20 countries in a pre-meeting organized by Italy, Germany and Belgium to discuss energy prices, regulatory simplification and strategic autonomy. ### Italy approves migration bill allowing naval blockades

ROME, Italy — Italy's cabinet approved legislation Wednesday authorizing naval blockades to halt migrant boats during periods of "exceptional pressure" at the country's borders, a government source said. The bill, which requires parliamentary approval, would allow the government to ban boats from entering territorial waters for up to six months in cases of serious threats to public order or national security, according to a draft seen by Reuters. Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said on social media the government had fulfilled another campaign promise with the "compatible" naval blockade measure. Those violating the blockades would face fines up to 50,000 euros and potential boat confiscation, measures aimed at charity rescue ships that Meloni's coalition argues incentivize dangerous sea crossings.

### Italy accepts observer role in Trump's Gaza board

ADDIS ABABA, Ethiopia — Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said Italy will accept an invitation to participate as an observer in President Donald Trump's Board for Gaza, citing the country's extensive work in the Middle East.
