## Top Stories

### Moldova President Blames Russia for Nistru River Oil Spill

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said Russia bears full responsibility for oil pollution in the Nistru River that has threatened Moldova's water supply. Sandu said in a social media post Sunday that Russia's attack on Ukraine's Novodnistrovsk hydropower plant caused the oil spill. The Moldovan government declared a 15-day environmental alert for the Nistru River basin after residents of Balti lost access to drinking water when petroleum products reached the river. Ukraine's Ministry of Economy, Environment and Agriculture confirmed the pollution could have resulted from Russian missile and drone attacks on energy infrastructure.

### Moldova's Sandu marks Down Syndrome Day with inclusion message

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu marked World Down Syndrome Day on Saturday with a message emphasizing inclusion and respect for people with disabilities. "The world becomes better when we learn to look at each other with more understanding and respect," Sandu said in a statement supporting a photography exhibition at the Government House. The exhibition was organized by the "My Friend" Association as part of a campaign called "Not all flowers grow the same way," which highlights that each child has their own development pace and talents. Prime Minister Alexandru Munteanu also participated in the campaign, receiving a flower pot planted by children with Down syndrome.
