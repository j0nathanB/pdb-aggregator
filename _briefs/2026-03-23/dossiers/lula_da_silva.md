# 🇧🇷 Brazil
## Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, President
_Week of March 16, 2026_

> Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva announced Finance Minister Fernando Haddad's candidacy for São Paulo governor this week, marking direct intervention in state-level electoral strategy ahead of the 2026 elections. ### Lula announces Haddad's candidacy for São Paulo governor

SÃO BERNARDO DO CAMPO, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva announced Friday that Finance Minister Fernando Haddad will run for governor of São Paulo, speaking at the ABC Metalworkers Union headquarters where Lula began his political career. ### Lula announces $1.6 billion Petrobras investments in Minas Gerais

BETIM, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva announced Friday that state oil company Petrobras will invest up to 9 billion reais ($1.6 billion) in Minas Gerais state over the next decade, potentially creating 36,000 jobs. Speaking at the Gabriel Passos refinery, Lula also defended oil exploration in Brazil's Equatorial Margin, a maritime area extending from Amapá to Rio Grande do Norte states. The president said the government will repurchase a Bahia refinery that Petrobras sold to UAE-based Mubadala Capital in 2021, though he provided no timeline or details.

### Opposition lawmakers seek Lula's proposal to dismiss prosecutor general

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — Two opposition lawmakers sent a letter Wednesday asking President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva to propose the dismissal of Brazil's prosecutor general, Paulo Gonet. Deputies Caroline de Toni and Gustavo Gayer said Gonet has failed to act on investigations involving Master bank and allegations against Supreme Court justices Dias Toffoli and Alexandre de Moraes. ### Lula pledges to maintain fishing subsidy for artisanal fishers

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva said Wednesday his government will not eliminate the fishing closure subsidy for artisanal fishers. The benefit, equivalent to one monthly minimum wage (currently 1,621 reais), is granted to artisanal fishers during closure periods when catching certain aquatic species is prohibited to protect their reproduction. "It would be incoherent to end something that gives people conditions to survive with dignity at a time when you cannot fish, when nature demands time for preservation," Lula said during a ceremony for the 3rd Women of the Waters Award, a Ministry of Fishing and Aquaculture initiative recognizing women's role in fishing, aquaculture and sustainability.
