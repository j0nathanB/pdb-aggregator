### Uruguay's Orsi announces competitiveness law, immediate business reforms

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi announced Tuesday a new competitiveness law to be introduced May 31 and four immediate measures to reduce bureaucratic barriers for businesses. Orsi said Uruguay must stop being "a machine that impedes" and transform into a partner for companies, acknowledging the country is viewed as "slow, expensive" with state agencies that complicate rather than simplify processes. Economy Minister Gabriel Oddone said the immediate measures will save businesses about $20 million annually by eliminating prior tax office intervention in foreign trade, removing paper documentation requirements, allowing deferred payments for imports, and reducing customs verifications.

### European far-right lawmakers demand Uruguay release dictatorship prisoners

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Thirty-one far-right members of the European Parliament wrote to Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi demanding the release of military and police personnel imprisoned for crimes committed during the country's military dictatorship, according to a letter obtained by El Observador. The lawmakers, led by German Alternative for Germany party member Markus Buchheit, threatened to promote measures against Uruguay in the European Parliament if the current situation continues. The letter calls the imprisoned individuals "political prisoners" and demands an end to all judicial proceedings that contradict Uruguay's amnesty law. The signatories represent four parliamentary groups from 11 countries and claim Uruguay's judicial processes risk the country's reconciliation climate.
