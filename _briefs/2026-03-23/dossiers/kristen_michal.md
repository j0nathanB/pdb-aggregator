## Top Stories

### Estonian PM Michal backs President Karis for second term

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Tuesday he supports President Alar Karis continuing for a second term. "I support President Karis. I will stick to that, I support President Karis, and I think President Karis can count on that," Michal said on ETV's "First Studio" program. Michal noted that polls show nearly 70 percent of Estonian citizens support Karis continuing as head of state. The prime minister said Karis must first decide whether to run for reelection and secure support from other political parties.

### Estonian PM backs modernizing criminal registry after sex offender database

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Estonia should consider modernizing its criminal registry after a controversial website listing convicted sex offenders sparked public debate. The website ettevaatust.ee, created by blogger Mallu Mariann Treimann-Legrant and her husband, contains names, court decisions and photos of more than 200 people convicted of sexual crimes and violence. Estonia's Data Protection Inspectorate said Thursday it launched oversight proceedings after receiving numerous complaints about the site. Michal said initiatives can be beneficial "if you don't take a negative stance from the beginning," according to Estonian media reports.

### Estonian PM calls for restricting Russian fighters' EU access

TALLINN, Estonia — Estonian Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Russian fighters who have killed and occupied territory in Ukraine should not be allowed to leave Russia, calling for continued restrictions on their entry to the European Union and Schengen area. Michal said the European Council agreed to limit Russian fighters' access to EU territory and tasked the European Commission with making concrete implementation proposals. "Today there are many in Russia who have occupied and killed. They should not escape from Russia," Michal stated, citing both pressure on Russia and European internal security concerns.

### Estonian PM Michal says Reform, Center parties tied in polls

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said his Reform Party leads the opposition Center Party by just two percentage points in a recent poll, calling the race "very even." Michal, who serves as Reform Party secretary-general, said the poll received Friday shows a tight contest similar to elections four years ago when 1.7 percent of voters decided the outcome. He warned that low voter turnout would benefit Center Party, whose core supporters are more active voters.
