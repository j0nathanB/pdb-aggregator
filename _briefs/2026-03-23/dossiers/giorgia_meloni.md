### Italians vote on judicial reform in test for Meloni

ROME, Italy — Italians began voting Sunday in a referendum on Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's contested judicial reform, a key test for her right-wing coalition ahead of next year's general election. The constitutional changes would separate career paths of judges and prosecutors and split Italy's judicial self-governing body into two entities, Reuters reported. Opposition parties led by the Democratic Party warned the reform would undermine judicial independence and allow political interference, while Meloni's government said the changes are needed to curb politicized appointments after scandals exposed backroom deals. Pre-vote polls showed the two camps in a tight race, with analysts saying a victory for either side could significantly impact the political landscape.

### Meloni calls deputy minister's restaurant investment 'light-hearted'

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said Deputy Justice Minister Andrea Delmastro showed poor judgment in investing in a Rome restaurant with the daughter of a man linked to organized crime investigations, but indicated he would remain in his position. ### Italy rules out military mission to unblock Hormuz Strait

BRUSSELS, Belgium — Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said Italy will not participate in a military mission to unblock the Strait of Hormuz, clarifying her country's position after six nations issued a joint statement on maritime security. ### Meloni announces 25-cent fuel tax cut amid Iran crisis

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni announced Wednesday that Italy's government approved a decree cutting fuel prices by 25 cents per liter through reduced excise taxes, responding to price spikes caused by the Iran conflict. The temporary measure will last 20 days while officials monitor developments in the Gulf region and potential closure of the Strait of Hormuz, through which one-fifth of global oil passes, Meloni said on state television. Economy Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti participated in government meetings on the fuel issue alongside Meloni and Environment Minister Gilberto Pichetto, according to officials. The decree also includes tax credits for truckers and anti-speculation measures with sanctions for fuel retailers who engage in price manipulation.
