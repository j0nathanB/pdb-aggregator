## Top Stories

### Ukraine helps 5 countries defend against Iranian drone attacks

KYIV, Ukraine — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said his country is providing drone defense expertise to five Gulf nations to counter Iranian attacks, with Ukrainian units already achieving results in the region. Zelenskyy said in his evening address that Ukraine is working daily with Gulf countries to provide expertise and support, adding that Russia is irritated by Ukraine's global assistance role. The president previously confirmed that three teams of dozens of Ukrainian drone experts had been deployed to Qatar, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia. Defense Secretary Rustem Umierov said the units are protecting civilian and critical infrastructure from Iranian drones.

### Zelenskyy Meets UK's Starmer, NATO Chief in London

LONDON, Britain — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy met with British Prime Minister Keir Starmer and NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte on Tuesday to discuss continued support for Ukraine amid concerns that the Iran conflict is diverting attention from the war with Russia. Zelenskyy called Russia and Iran "brothers in hatred" and said other countries could learn from Ukraine's adoption of defense technology including drones and AI, according to his remarks to British lawmakers. Starmer said "Putin can't be the one who benefits from the conflict in Iran, whether that's oil prices or the dropping of sanctions." The leaders signed a defense collaboration agreement combining Ukraine's expertise with Britain's industrial base to manufacture drones and defense capabilities.

### Zelenskyy signs defense, infrastructure agreements with Spain

MADRID, Spain — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy signed five bilateral agreements with Spain on Tuesday covering defense cooperation, financial assistance and railway modernization, according to the Ukrainian president's office. The agreements include a memorandum on joint defense production between the two countries' defense ministries and a technical cooperation framework that will provide Spanish financial support for various programs in Ukraine, Zelenskyy's office said. Spain allocated 200 million euros in November for Ukraine's infrastructure restoration, with the first project focusing on developing innovative freight wagon technology for faster delivery of Ukrainian exports across Europe, officials said.

### Zelenskyy says Ukraine counting on EU to unblock aid package

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Thursday that a 90 billion-euro aid package from the European Union currently blocked by Hungary is critical for Ukraine as it faces a "very difficult" situation while fighting Russia. In comments to the European Council, Zelenskyy said Ukraine was working productively with the EU on restoring the Druzhba oil pipeline damaged by Russia. He also said he has received signals from the U.S. that peace talks with Russia may resume soon.

### Trump accuses Zelenskyy of Middle East aid PR

### Zelenskyy says Ukrainian troops hold same positions as last year

LONDON, United Kingdom — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said his country's troops are holding positions that are almost the same as those they held last year, according to comments made to a Ukrainian blogger during a visit to the United Kingdom. Zelenskyy said Russians lose 30,000-35,000 troops monthly and have failed to achieve their initial goals of fully occupying the Donbas region or capturing Ukraine's capital. "We are almost at the same positions, only the losses are increasing," Zelenskyy told blogger Max Klymenko, adding that Russia cannot break Ukrainian defense lines and tries to create chaos through missile strikes on energy and civilian infrastructure.
