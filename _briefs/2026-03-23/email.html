THE MIDDLE POWERS MONITOR
Week of March 16, 2026
THIS WEEK
This week European leaders fractured over military responses to Iran's Strait of Hormuz blockade, with Starmer resisting Trump's pressure for British participation while Macron named France's new carrier 'Free France' amid Mediterranean operations against Russian vessels. Meloni faced a crucial judicial reform referendum as Zelenskyy revealed Ukraine is helping five nations defend against Iranian drone attacks. In the Americas, Lula criticized the UN Security Council as 'omissive' while announcing Haddad's São Paulo gubernatorial bid, and Uruguay's Orsi assumed the CELAC presidency calling for regional anti-drug cooperation.

North America

Carney expanded Canada's international engagement with a London meeting with Starmer, marking a shift from previous administrations' domestic focus while facing union criticism over automotive sector policies. Sheinbaum pursued economic diplomacy by seeking zero tariffs in USMCA negotiations and calling for banking sector growth, while managing domestic resistance to electoral reforms and maintaining active foreign policy including diplomatic outreach to Spain and continued Cuban humanitarian aid.

South America

Lula announced Finance Minister Haddad's São Paulo gubernatorial candidacy while using international forums to criticize the UN Security Council as 'omissive' on global conflicts and advocate for Global South unity, also announcing $1.6 billion in Petrobras investments and blaming fuel price increases on war profiteering. Orsi faced diplomatic pressures from Argentina over Uruguay's green hydrogen project and European Parliament demands for prisoner releases, responding by announcing business reforms through a new competitiveness law and assuming the CELAC presidency with calls for regional anti-drug cooperation.

Europe

Starmer resisted Trump's pressure to join Iran military action while calling emergency meetings on economic impacts and facing internal Labour challenges from Rayner's public criticism and over 100 MPs warning about assisted dying delays. Macron named France's new aircraft carrier 'Free France' while French forces boarded Russian tankers in Mediterranean operations, announced fighter jet project mediation, and intervened in Paris municipal politics. Meloni faced judicial reform voting while defending coalition members and ruling out Italian military participation in Hormuz operations. Zelenskyy conducted extensive diplomacy with Starmer and NATO leadership while revealing Ukraine's assistance to five countries against Iranian drone attacks. Merz rejected German military participation in Iran operations while threatening Hungary over Ukraine aid blocks, and Tusk accused Hungary of sharing EU intelligence with Russia.

Baltic States

Nausėda warned against easing Belarus sanctions while rejecting nationalist rhetoric and describing Lithuania's low birth rates as 'self-destruction,' also considering fuel price caps amid economic pressures. Siliņa defended her government's performance against criticism while assuring no fuel supply crisis despite Middle East tensions and promoting Latvia's digital initiatives at an AI forum. Michal endorsed President Karis for a second term while calling for criminal registry modernization and restrictions on Russian fighters' EU access, acknowledging his Reform Party's tied polling with the opposition Center Party.
