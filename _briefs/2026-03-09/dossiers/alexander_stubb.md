## Top Stories

### Finland to host JEF Leaders' Summit in Helsinki

HELSINKI, Finland — President Alexander Stubb will host a Joint Expeditionary Force Leaders' Summit in Helsinki on March 26, the Office of the President said Tuesday. The summit will focus on JEF nations' support for Ukraine and Europe's overall security situation, according to the presidential office. The JEF is a UK-led multilateral defense cooperation framework that includes Denmark, Estonia, Finland, Iceland, Latvia, Lithuania, the Netherlands, Norway and Sweden. The leaders last met in Norway in May 2025, the office said.

### Finland's Stubb completes state visit to India

NEW DELHI, India — Finnish President Alexander Stubb completed a four-day state visit to India on March 7, 2026, during which the two countries elevated their relationship to a Strategic Partnership in Digitalization and Sustainability. Stubb delivered the inaugural address at the Raisina Dialogue 2026 conference on March 5, calling for a "New Delhi moment" to reform international institutions and make them more representative of the Global South. ### Finland's Stubb backs removing nuclear weapon restrictions

NEW DELHI, India — Finnish President Alexander Stubb said Friday that Finland should remove current legislative restrictions on nuclear weapons to align with other Nordic countries and enable full participation in NATO nuclear planning. Speaking to Finnish media during a state visit to India, Stubb said Finland's security interests require eliminating legal barriers to the three pillars of NATO defense: conventional forces, missiles and nuclear weapons. The government announced Thursday it would propose changes to nuclear energy and criminal laws to allow nuclear weapons to be brought into Finland under certain conditions related to military defense and NATO cooperation. Stubb emphasized that Finland does not want nuclear weapons stationed on its territory and none are planned by NATO.

### Finnish President Discusses Citizens' Safety With UAE Leader

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb spoke by phone Wednesday with United Arab Emirates President Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed al-Nahyan about the situation of Finnish nationals in the UAE amid ongoing Middle East tensions. Stubb said he expressed condolences for victims of Iranian strikes in the UAE and condemned what he called Iran's "groundless and arbitrary attacks" on the Emirates and the region. The presidents discussed coordination between their countries' authorities regarding Finnish citizens in the UAE, Stubb said on social media platform X. Many Finns were stranded in Dubai after civilian flights were suspended due to Iranian strikes, though Finland's Foreign Ministry said some Finns have been able to leave the Middle East in recent days.

### Finnish lawmaker accuses President Stubb of misleading nuclear information

HELSINKI, Finland — European Parliament member Li Andersson accused Finnish President Alexander Stubb of providing misleading information about proposed changes to nuclear weapons legislation. Andersson said Friday on social media that Stubb incorrectly claimed the changes would align Finland with other Nordic countries' policies. The Left Alliance politician argued that while other Nordic nations don't categorically ban nuclear weapons transport and storage in law, they maintain national consensus against accepting nuclear weapons during peacetime, making Finland a regional exception under the proposed changes. Prime Minister Petteri Orpo's government announced Thursday it would propose legislation allowing nuclear weapons on Finnish territory for defense purposes or NATO cooperation.
