### Brazil's Lula signs law strengthening child rape protections

BRASILIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva signed legislation Sunday that reinforces protections for minors and disabled individuals in sexual assault cases by clarifying that victim vulnerability cannot be questioned based on case circumstances. The law amends Brazil's Penal Code to establish that vulnerability is absolute and cannot be reduced regardless of alleged victim consent, sexual experience or resulting pregnancy, the presidential palace said. Lula said the measure closes legal loopholes that allowed abusers to escape punishment by claiming relations were consensual. The legislation responds to judicial decisions that had relativized vulnerability based on factors like prior relationships, according to the government.
