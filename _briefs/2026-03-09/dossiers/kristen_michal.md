### Estonia authorizes evacuation assistance for citizens in Middle East

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Monday his government authorized the Foreign Ministry to take all necessary steps to help Estonian citizens safely return home from the Middle East conflict zone. Michal said Estonia is already in contact with its citizens in the region and providing round-the-clock communication through an emergency hotline. ### Estonia announces new wind farm tender worth 240 million euros

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said the government approved a new renewable energy tender to develop onshore wind farms capable of producing 1-2 TWh of electricity. Michal said the initiative will cost up to 240 million euros over 12 years, funded through the renewable energy fee currently set at 0.84 cents per kWh. The government authorized the energy and environment minister to organize the tender, which aims to bring up to 2 TWh of new electricity production to Estonia. Michal said supporting 1 TWh would cost 10 million euros annually.

### Estonian PM discusses defense spending, coalition stability

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Estonia will maintain defense spending at five percent of GDP while suggesting future tax increases could be avoided if economic conditions improve. Michal told Postimees newspaper that Estonia's defense costs have risen from a planned 800 million euros to 2.3 billion euros due to the war in Ukraine. The prime minister said he aims to keep the current coalition government together until parliamentary elections in March 2027, despite recent political tensions in Tallinn's municipal government.
