### Poland deploys military aircraft for Middle East evacuation

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk ordered the deployment of military aircraft to evacuate Polish citizens from the Middle East, saying more than 2,800 people have been evacuated since March 1 but warning there is "no reason for satisfaction" with the operation's progress. Foreign Minister Radoslaw Sikorski said evacuations from Jordan, Israel and Lebanon were "practically completed," while efforts continue to assist Poles in the United Arab Emirates and other Gulf states. Tusk said the European Union is ready to co-finance Poland's evacuation operation if other EU citizens are included on the flights. The evacuation follows recent attacks on Iran and retaliatory strikes that have escalated tensions across the region.

### Polish PM Questions Who Benefits From Rising Oil Prices

WARSAW, Poland — Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk questioned Saturday who benefits from rising oil prices amid potential U.S. sanctions relief on Russian oil. "War in the Middle East continues, and chaos spreads. Oil prices are rising. Washington may lift sanctions on Russian oil. Who is the real winner here?" Tusk said on social media platform X. U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Friday the administration may lift some sanctions on Russian oil to lower global prices, with Brent crude surpassing $90 per barrel Friday. Oil prices have risen nearly 40 percent since the start of the Iran war, primarily due to the closure of the Strait of Hormuz.

## Domestic

### Polish PM Tusk warns of three confederations opposing government

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said three confederations will oppose his government following the opposition Law and Justice party's selection of Przemysław Czarnek as its candidate for prime minister. "So three Confederations against us. There's nothing to fear, but they cannot be underestimated. One thing is certain: in 2027 Poland faces a game for everything," Tusk wrote on social media platform X on Saturday. Law and Justice party leader Jarosław Kaczyński announced Czarnek's candidacy during a party convention in Krakow, where Czarnek criticized Tusk's government and called the prime minister an "empty head."

### Polish Opposition Leader Calls Prime Minister Tusk 'Empty Head'

WARSAW, Poland — Former Education Minister Przemysław Czarnek called Prime Minister Donald Tusk an "empty head" during a parliamentary speech Saturday, escalating political tensions ahead of upcoming elections. Czarnek, who was announced as the Law and Justice party's candidate for prime minister, criticized Tusk's previous comments calling opponents "blockheads" regarding defense funding. ### Tusk seeks details on Polish defense funding proposal

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said he is waiting for concrete details about a Polish defense funding program proposed by President Karol Nawrocki and central bank chief Adam Glapiński. Tusk said Friday that if he receives specific information about additional funds from the National Bank of Poland for the defense industry, he could draft legislation within hours and present it to parliament by Monday. Presidential spokesperson Rafał Leśkiewicz responded Saturday that the government wants to use EU loan funds to cover budget deficits rather than genuinely address defense needs.
