### Moldova's Sandu defends lowering vetting commission vote threshold

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu defended her ruling party's decision to lower the parliamentary vote threshold for appointing international members to judicial vetting commissions from 61 to 51 votes. Speaking Thursday on Jurnal TV, Sandu said the change was necessary because "there are not 61 deputies in this Parliament who support justice reform." The modification allows Dutch candidate Herman von Hebel, who previously served on Moldova's pre-vetting commission, to be appointed with a simple majority after failing to secure the required supermajority last week. Opposition parties said they will challenge the law change at the Constitutional Court.

### Moldova's Sandu marks 1992 Transnistria war anniversary

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu participated Monday in commemorative events marking 34 years since the Transnistria war, calling for Russian troop withdrawal from Moldovan territory. ### Sandu says she wasn't informed Șepeli would be released in 7 months

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said she was not informed that Nicolae Șepeli would be released from prison within seven months when she granted him a pardon in 2022. Șepeli, who was later arrested as the alleged organizer of an assassination plot in Ukraine, was scheduled for early release due to good behavior, Sandu said during a March 5 television interview. The president said the Intelligence and Security Service began investigating Șepeli in summer 2025 after detecting him as a member of a Șor group during elections, later discovering he was planning crimes. Sandu revoked the pardon decree on February 19, 2026, after Moldovan and Ukrainian authorities arrested 11 Moldovan citizens in connection with the alleged Russian-directed assassination network.
