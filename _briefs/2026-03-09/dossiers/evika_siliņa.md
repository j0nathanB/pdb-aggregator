### Latvia PM arranges repatriation flights from UAE after safety talks

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa said she has coordinated with government ministers to arrange repatriation flights for Latvian citizens from the United Arab Emirates, prioritizing vulnerable people and families with children. Siliņa said the UAE president assured her in a phone call Monday that Latvian residents are safe, but acknowledged many want to return home. The Foreign Ministry said it is working with the Transport Ministry to prepare a Cabinet order for airBaltic flights, pending a safe window for air traffic in the region.

### Latvia PM Reports Progress on Government Priority Goals

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa said Monday that her government achieved many targets from its "4x4" priority plan launched in February 2025. Siliņa told cabinet members that 51.4% of national development goals were met or showed positive progress, according to her annual report to parliament. The government focused on four priorities: security, family support, economic growth and reducing bureaucracy by 25%. Defense spending reached 4.91% of GDP in 2025, approaching the target of 5% set for next year, Siliņa said.

## International

### Latvia PM says fuel prices rising due to Iran tensions

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa said fuel prices in Latvia have risen sharply due to military tensions in Iran, but stressed the country has adequate reserves and supply is not at risk. Siliņa said Latvia has purchased the required 15% fuel reserves and noted similar price spikes occurred last year during regional conflicts. She said fuel prices, while rising, remain below January 2023 levels, and the Economics Ministry will continue monitoring the situation.

*Sources: (Latvijas Avīze [1](https://www.la.lv/ta-bija-ari-pagajusaja-gada-evika-silina-komente-degvielas-cenu-kapumu))*

### Latvia PM denies state budget needed for Rail Baltica

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa denied that Latvia will need to contribute additional state budget funds beyond mandatory European Union co-financing for the Rail Baltica railway project, according to a January interview with Latvijas Radio. Siliņa's statement relies on uncertain hopes that the three Baltic states will receive approximately 10 billion euros from the EU's next multi-year budget for 2028-2034 to complete the first phase, officials said. The cross-border railway project connecting Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania to Poland faces significant funding shortfalls, with Latvia's portion potentially exceeding 4 billion euros.

### Latvia PM Opens Expanded STEAM Education Center

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa participated in the opening of expanded facilities for a STEAM education and technology center, the Cabinet of Ministers said. The center, which focuses on science, technology, engineering, arts and mathematics education, opened in early 2025 and has served approximately 20,000 children and educators from across Latvia, according to the government statement. The facility represents a social entrepreneurship project aimed at providing practical, creative and experience-based STEAM learning approaches.

### Latvia PM appeals court ruling on former official's disciplinary action

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa has appealed a court ruling regarding disciplinary action against former State Chancellery Director Jānis Citskovskis in connection with expensive private flights taken by former Prime Minister Krišjānis Kariņš, the LETA news agency reported. The Administrative District Court in January partially upheld Citskovskis' complaint, ruling that his suspension from office was unlawful and ordering compensation of 5,333 euros for unpaid wages. Siliņa appealed the portion of the ruling that found the disciplinary punishment unlawful, while Citskovskis appealed the court's rejection of his claim for severance pay. The case stems from Kariņs' use of private aircraft for 36 foreign trips costing over 1.2 million euros during his four-year tenure as prime minister.
