### Uruguay's Orsi faces 41% disapproval in first-year poll

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi faces a 41% disapproval rating after his first year in office, according to a Factum poll released Monday. The survey showed 37% of Uruguayans approve of Orsi's performance while 21% neither approve nor disapprove, Factum said. ### Uruguay government expects RCEP trade alliance membership this term

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Uruguay's government said it expects the country to join the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership trade alliance during President Yamandú Orsi's current term, according to executive branch sources cited by El País newspaper. Foreign Minister Mario Lubetkin sent a membership application letter to the Association of Southeast Asian Nations secretary general on Feb. 18, the report said. The RCEP represents 30% of global GDP and includes China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand and 10 ASEAN countries. Government sources said Uruguay's chances of acceptance are higher than with the Trans-Pacific Partnership agreement due to lower diplomatic complexity.

### Uruguay President Meets FAO Chief During Official Visit

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi met with FAO Director-General Qu Dongyu during the UN official's two-day visit to Uruguay, the Food and Agriculture Organization said. Orsi emphasized Uruguay's view of FAO as a strategic partner for promoting food security, which he called a fundamental condition for peace, according to the organization.
