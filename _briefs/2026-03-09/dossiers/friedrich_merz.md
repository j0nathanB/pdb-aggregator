### Merz meets Trump for third White House visit

The Iran conflict will dominate discussions more than originally planned following recent Israeli and American strikes on the Iranian regime, Merz said Sunday at the chancellery. ### Greens edge Merz's conservatives in German state election

STUTTGART, Germany — Germany's environmental Greens took a narrow lead over Chancellor Friedrich Merz's conservatives in a state election in Baden-Wuerttemberg on Sunday, early projections showed. Public broadcaster ARD projected the Greens winning 30.4% of the vote, followed by Merz's Christian Democrats with 29.7% and the far-right Alternative for Germany with 18.6%. The result represents the first electoral test for Merz's government and is likely to fuel internal discontent over the sluggish pace of promised reforms, according to political observers. Jens Spahn, head of the CDU parliamentary group, said the decisions made by Merz's government are "not yet sufficiently noticeable in everyday life."
