### Sheinbaum calls for US arms controls to help Mexico

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum said the best help the United States can provide Mexico is preventing weapons from entering the country illegally. Sheinbaum stated she does not know if Mexico's lawsuit in U.S. courts will result in formal charges, but said the goal is to highlight that manufacturers and sellers are responsible for selling to those who bring weapons to Mexico illegally. The Inter-American Court of Human Rights issued a non-binding advisory opinion supporting Mexico's position that states must exercise "due diligence" in regulating arms sales, including by private companies. Mexico's Foreign Ministry said the court's opinion reinforces the country's stance that companies play a significant role in armed violence through their commercial decisions and lack of controls in weapons sales and distribution.

### Sheinbaum submits electoral reform proposal to Mexican Congress

MEXICO CITY, Mexico — President Claudia Sheinbaum submitted her electoral reform proposal to the Chamber of Deputies on Wednesday, after a two-day delay, according to congressional officials. The initiative would modify 11 constitutional articles, including reducing the Senate from 128 to 96 members and changing how 200 proportional representation deputies are selected, said Chamber President Kenia López Rabadán. The proposal also seeks to cut political party funding by 25 percent and requires artificial intelligence-generated electoral content to be labeled, according to the submitted document. The reform faces opposition from the Labor Party, which said it would not support the measure.
