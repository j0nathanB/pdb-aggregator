### Zelenskyy says he wouldn't repair Russian oil pipeline

BUDAPEST, Hungary — Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Thursday he would prefer not to repair a damaged oil pipeline that delivers Russian crude to Central Europe, despite rising tensions with Hungary and Slovakia over oil flow interruptions. "To be honest, I wouldn't restore it. This is my position," Zelenskyy said during a news conference. Hungarian Prime Minister Viktor Orbán has blocked a 90-billion euro EU loan to Ukraine over the pipeline disruption and threatened to use "political and financial tools" to compel Ukraine to reopen the Druzhba pipeline. The pipeline was damaged in a Russian strike on Jan. 27 near Brody in western Ukraine.

### Ukraine Approves Regional Energy Resilience Plans for Winter

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy chaired a National Security and Defense Council meeting that approved comprehensive energy resilience plans for Ukraine's regions and cities, the president's office said. The council approved plans for every Ukrainian region and regional center except Kyiv, which was given additional time to prepare documents, according to Zelenskyy. "Kyiv was not ready today – just as it hadn't been this past winter," Zelenskyy said, emphasizing that every community must be prepared for any scenario next winter. Prime Minister Yuliia Svyrydenko said the government will provide protection from drones and missiles for major electricity transit, gas extraction and storage facilities.

### Zelenskyy says Ukraine needs €5 billion for heating season

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukraine needs approximately €5 billion to prepare its regions for the next heating season, speaking during a briefing with journalists. Zelenskyy said the country has six to seven months to complete protective and construction work before the heating season begins.
