### Meloni considers mobile fuel tax mechanism amid rising prices

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said her government is evaluating activating a mobile fuel tax mechanism to counter rising gasoline prices amid ongoing conflicts. "We are evaluating activating the mechanism of so-called mobile excise taxes," Meloni said in a video message, confirming earlier statements by Economy Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti. The mechanism would use increased VAT revenue from higher fuel prices to reduce excise taxes, Meloni said. Democratic Party leader Elly Schlein had proposed using extra VAT revenue to immediately reduce fuel costs through the mobile excise tax system.

### Meloni criticizes judges over family separation case

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni criticized Italian juvenile court judges after they ordered the separation of three children from their mother in a controversial custody case. ### Meloni says women's equality is right, not concession

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said women's equality is a right that should not depend on quotas or favor mechanisms, speaking at an event marking 80 years of women's voting rights in Italy. "I think that true freedom remains being able to earn one's position on the field," Meloni said, adding that the state should ensure "equality at the starting point" rather than using quotas.
