### Lithuania's president backs US military action in Iran

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda said Tuesday his country understands the United States' military operation in Iran, citing failed diplomatic efforts to halt Tehran's nuclear weapons development and human rights abuses. "All the diplomatic efforts to stop Iran's nuclear weapons development have unfortunately not yielded the expected results," Nausėda told reporters after a State Defence Council meeting at the Presidential Palace. Nausėda said Lithuania and Poland had advance knowledge of the US and Israeli plans to strike Iran.

### Lithuania president proposes two-year migrant work permits

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda proposed a circular migration system that would issue temporary work permits for a maximum of two years, after which migrants must leave the country for at least six months before reapplying. Under the plan announced Wednesday, foreign nationals would work for specific employers within set quotas and could not change jobs, according to the presidency. The measure would primarily target lower-skilled sectors such as construction, logistics and social services, said Deividas Matulionis, Nausėda's chief security adviser. Conservative party leader Laurynas Kasčiūnas said Friday his Homeland Union-Lithuanian Christian Democrats party supports the proposal.

### Lithuanian President Nausėda to Meet Parliament Leaders on Legislative Agenda

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda will meet with parliamentary leadership to discuss legislative initiatives for the spring session, his communications adviser Ridas Jasiulionis said. ### Lithuanian President Nausėda suffers nerve damage in home accident

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda suffered nerve damage to his elbow after falling at home last week, forcing him to cancel a planned visit to Kyiv for the fourth anniversary of Russia's war in Ukraine. Nausėda said he slipped while moving through his residence, hitting his head on furniture and injuring his arm on a sharp object, which "tore a piece of my elbow and damaged the nerve." Orthopedic traumatologist K.
