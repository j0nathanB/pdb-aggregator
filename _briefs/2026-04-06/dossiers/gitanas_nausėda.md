### Lithuanian president proposes daily fuel price caps

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda submitted draft legislation to parliament proposing daily fuel price caps to address rising costs driven by Middle East conflicts, his office said. Under the proposal, the energy minister would calculate and publish maximum fuel prices daily, with the system activated by government decree during significant price fluctuations or tax changes. Retailers violating the caps could face fines up to 50,000 euros or license suspension, according to the presidency. Nausėda said the mechanism would ensure financial benefits reach consumers when fuel taxes are reduced while remaining compatible with market economics.

### Lithuania's president sets conditions for Belarus dialogue

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda said Wednesday that dialogue with Belarus is possible only if Minsk demonstrates good will by stopping migrant flows, balloon attacks and releasing political prisoners. Nausėda made the comments to TV3 News in response to U.S. Special Envoy for Belarus John Coale's suggestion that Lithuania should hold bilateral political meetings with Belarus and restore fertilizer transit. "If in the next few weeks there are no balloons, no illegal migrants, political prisoners all safely return to Lithuania, and the regime demonstrates efforts to normalize relations, we will evaluate this as a sign of good will and possibly begin such dialogue," Nausėda stated. Lithuania recorded a daily record of 34 illegal border crossers this week, while contraband balloons from Belarus have forced temporary closures of Vilnius airport.

### Lithuanian President urges coalition decision on Nemuno Ausra party

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said Wednesday that the ruling Social Democrats should not delay deciding the fate of coalition partner Nemuno Ausra after most of its lawmakers opposed a military training ground project. Nausėda told TV3 that he sees no point in waiting much longer, citing the party's opposition to establishing the Kapčiamiesčio military training facility in Lazdijai district. The president said he struggles to understand a coalition guided by different values, particularly regarding national survival and state security, after Nemuno Ausra leader Remigijus Žemaitaitis and six other party members voted against the defense project last week.
