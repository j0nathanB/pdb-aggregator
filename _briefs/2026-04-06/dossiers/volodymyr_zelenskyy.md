### Ukraine, Syria agree to boost security cooperation

DAMASCUS, Syria — Ukraine and Syria pledged greater security cooperation during talks Sunday, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said, as Kyiv seeks to promote its military expertise across the Middle East. KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Ukraine must be prepared for a possible halt in U.S. weapons supplies as his government faces a severe funding crisis requiring parliamentary action next week. ### Ukraine Imposes Sanctions on Russian Military-Industrial Entities

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy signed decrees imposing sanctions on dozens of Russian entities supporting Moscow's military-industrial complex and facilitating sanctions evasion, the presidential office said Tuesday. The sanctions target 26 individuals and 31 legal entities involved in producing weapons systems, unmanned vehicles and components for missiles including Kh-101, Kh-59M2/M2A, and Iskander-K models. Additional sanctions hit 7 individuals and 11 companies engaged in illegal business activities in occupied territories and construction of the Kerch Bridge connecting Russia to Crimea. Ukraine will share information with international partners to coordinate sanctions across jurisdictions, said Vladyslav Vlasiuk, the president's advisor for sanctions policy.

### Egypt tells Zelenskyy it won't buy grain from occupied Ukrainian territories

KYIV, Ukraine — Egypt will no longer accept grain exported by Russia from temporarily occupied Ukrainian territories, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said following a conversation with Egyptian President Abdel Fattah el-Sisi. Zelenskyy said Egypt is also interested in increasing its imports of grain from Ukraine. Egypt is the world's largest wheat importer and a major buyer of Russian grain, having purchased around 7.6 million tonnes of Russian grain this season, according to Reuters. Russia stole more than two million tonnes of grain from temporarily occupied Ukrainian territories in 2025, according to Ukrainian officials.
