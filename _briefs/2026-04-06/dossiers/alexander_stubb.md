## Top Stories

### Finnish President Stubb cancels meeting due to virus infection

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb canceled a scheduled meeting with Moldovan President Maia Sandu due to a virus infection, the Finnish president's office said in a statement. Sandu's working visit to Finland, planned for Tuesday, has been postponed, according to the office. The leaders were expected to discuss bilateral relations between Finland and Moldova, Russia's war in Ukraine, and Moldova's EU accession process. A new date for the visit will be announced later, the office said.

### Finnish President Tells Trump 'More European NATO' Taking Shape

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb told U.S. President Donald Trump in a phone call Wednesday that "a more European NATO" was taking shape and that Europe was "shouldering responsibility," Stubb's office said. The call came as Trump told Reuters he was considering withdrawing the U.S. from the NATO military alliance. Stubb wrote on social media that the leaders had a "constructive discussion" on NATO, Ukraine and Iran. A more European NATO will be on the agenda at the military alliance's annual summit in Ankara on July 7-8, Stubb's office said.

## International

### Finnish President Stubb urges Iran to reopen Hormuz Strait

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb said Thursday he spoke with Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian about reopening the Strait of Hormuz and ending attacks on neighboring countries. Stubb said on social media that a ceasefire and diplomatic solution are needed quickly, emphasizing the need to restore free navigation through the strait. The presidential office said Finland does not have an official mediator role but considers it important to maintain dialogue with different parties. Iran has closed the Strait of Hormuz, through which about one-fifth of the world's oil passes.

*Sources: (Ilta-Sanomat [1](https://www.is.fi/politiikka/art-2000011922908.html))*

### Finnish President Discusses Ukrainian Drone Incident With Zelensky

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb discussed a recent drone incident with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, according to Zelensky's social media post. Two Ukrainian drones landed in southeastern Finland over the weekend, prompting Ukraine to apologize and cite probable Russian interference as the cause, according to Ukraine's foreign ministry. Zelensky said he and Stubb 'see the situation the same way' and are sharing all necessary information about the incident. The Ukrainian leader also briefed Stubb on his recent meetings in the Middle East and Persian Gulf region.

*Sources: (Ilta-Sanomat [1](https://www.is.fi/politiikka/art-2000011913328.html))*

### Finnish President Reflects on Military Service in British Interview

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb reflected on his military service during the Cold War in a recent interview with British newspaper The Times, contrasting his experience with that of his son. Stubb said he questioned why he was serving in the military during his 1988-1989 service, but noted his son, who completed military service two years ago, no longer needs to ask that question. The president warned that modern warfare has changed geographic vulnerabilities, stating that from Moscow it is equally easy to strike London as Helsinki. Stubb recently hosted a meeting of Nordic and Baltic leaders in Helsinki to discuss Ukraine, the Arctic region and hybrid warfare.
