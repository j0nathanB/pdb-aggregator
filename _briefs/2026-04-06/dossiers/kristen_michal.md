### Estonian PM denies airspace used for Russia attacks

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal denied claims that Estonia allows its airspace to be used for attacks against Russia, calling such assertions false information. Michal said drones are directed away from Russian airspace for defensive purposes while Russia attacks Ukraine, describing the incidents as "fragments of war." The prime minister said the misinformation may be part of Russian information operations using various channels including fake accounts and troll networks. Michal also acknowledged Estonia needs improvement in its alert systems following a March 31 drone incident.

### Estonian PM, Foreign Minister Say US Won't Leave NATO

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal and Foreign Minister Margus Tsahkna said the United States will not leave NATO, calling for calm amid President Donald Trump's critical statements about the alliance. Michal said the situation where the US as the largest security ally criticizes NATO inevitably creates uncertainty and shows that political relations between allies are not always good.
