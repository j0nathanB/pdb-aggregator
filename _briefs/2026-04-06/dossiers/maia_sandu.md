# 🇲🇩 Moldova
## Maia Sandu, President
_Week of March 30, 2026_

> Moldovan President Maia Sandu conducted a diplomatic tour of EU member states this week, making her first presidential visit to Slovakia and planning trips to Finland and Latvia to build support for Moldova's EU membership bid. Sandu met with Latvian officials to request backing for the membership application and held discussions with a U.S. senator on security issues. The visits represent Moldova's strategy to secure broader consensus among EU member states beyond major powers for its integration into the bloc.

## Top Stories

### Moldova's Sandu urges Latvia to support EU membership bid

RIGA, Latvia — Moldovan President Maia Sandu addressed Latvia's parliament Wednesday, urging lawmakers to support Moldova's European Union membership bid. Sandu said Moldova's EU accession "is not just about Moldova" but represents "strategic coherence" for European security, drawing parallels between the two countries' shared experience under Soviet occupation. She told Latvian deputies that supporting Moldova's membership and defending their own democracy "are essentially the same act of resistance." The visit came at the invitation of Latvian President Edgars Rinkēvičs.

## International

### Moldova President Sandu Plans Finland, Latvia Visits

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu will conduct official visits to Finland and Latvia from March 31 to April 1, the presidency announced. Sandu will meet with Finnish President Alexander Stubb in Helsinki on March 31, followed by discussions with Latvian President Edgars Rinkēvičs in Riga on April 1, according to the presidential office. The visits will focus on strengthening bilateral relations, regional security and Moldova's European integration path, officials said. The trips mark Sandu's sixth foreign travel in three months as she intensifies diplomatic efforts to promote Moldova's European Union membership bid.

### Moldova's Sandu makes first presidential visit to Slovakia

BRATISLAVA, Slovakia — Moldovan President Maia Sandu conducted the first official visit by a Moldovan head of state to Slovakia on March 26, meeting with Slovak President Peter Pellegrini and other officials, according to a statement from Moldova's presidency. The leaders signed a social security agreement and two memorandums of understanding aimed at boosting investment and trade cooperation between the countries. Pellegrini said Slovakia supports Moldova's European Union membership bid and wants to see Moldova "at the European table as an official member of the European Union as soon as possible." The visit marked 33 years of diplomatic relations between the two nations.

### Moldovan President Meets US Senator on Security Issues

CHISINAU, Moldova — Moldovan President Maia Sandu met with U.S. Senator Jack Reed on April 3 to discuss strengthening Moldova's resilience against security challenges, the presidential office said. Reed, a senior Democratic member of the Senate Armed Services Committee, also discussed Moldova's European integration process and bilateral cooperation during his visit to Chisinau, according to a presidential statement. The senator was accompanied by experts from the Senate Armed Services Committee. This marks Sandu's second meeting with U.S. senators this year, following a February session with four other senators.
