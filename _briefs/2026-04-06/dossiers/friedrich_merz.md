## Top Stories

### Merz says 80% of Syrian refugees expected to return

BERLIN, Germany — Chancellor Friedrich Merz said Monday that 80% of Syrian refugees in Germany are expected to return to their home country over the next three years, following a meeting with Syrian President Ahmed al-Sharaa. Merz said Germany would prioritize the return of those without valid residence rights, particularly criminal offenders, among the 700,000-800,000 Syrians who have sought asylum in the country. The two leaders announced plans for a joint reconstruction and return program, with al-Sharaa stating Syria would establish "a model of circular migration" allowing Syrian talents to contribute to rebuilding their country. However, German media reported confusion over the 80% figure, noting many Syrians are already naturalized or well-integrated.

### CDU Members Express Discontent With Merz Government Performance

BERLIN, Germany — Members of Chancellor Friedrich Merz's Christian Democratic Union are expressing dissatisfaction with how the government is handling major reforms, according to German media reports. Former Chancellor's Office chief Peter Altmaier said the administration creates new controversies every few days in the reform debate, causing citizens to take to the streets in protest. The CDU's worker wing has also expressed concerns about the government's approach, particularly after Merz made what critics called unprofessional remarks about Syrian refugees returning to their homeland.
