### Macron calls military action to reopen Hormuz Strait unrealistic

SEOUL, South Korea — French President Emmanuel Macron said Thursday it would be unrealistic to launch a military operation to force open the Strait of Hormuz, rejecting calls from U.S. President Donald Trump for allies to help reopen the waterway. Macron said the strait, which carries about a fifth of global oil and liquefied natural gas supplies, can only be reopened through dialogue with Iran. The waterway has been effectively closed since Iranian attacks following U.S. and Israeli strikes on Iran in February. Macron said France is not part of the U.S.-Israel operation and wants peace as soon as possible.

### Macron redefines French nuclear deterrence strategy

BREST, France — President Emmanuel Macron delivered a major speech on nuclear deterrence at the Île Longue naval base, redefining France's nuclear strategy in response to Russian and Chinese threats, according to Le Point. ### Macron's approval rating drops amid fuel price crisis

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron's approval rating fell to 23% in April, down two points from the previous month, according to an Elabe poll for Les Echos newspaper. The decline follows a temporary boost Macron received last month during Middle East tensions, which pollster Bernard Sananès called an "ephemeral flag effect." Sananès said Macron is now facing domestic concerns, particularly criticism over France's debt levels amid rising fuel prices. The poll showed 73% of French citizens said they do not trust Macron, a four-point increase from the previous month.

### Macron calls payment systems strategic for economic sovereignty

PARIS, France — President Emmanuel Macron said payment systems represent the "last kilometer of economic sovereignty" during a video address to France's annual banking cards summit Tuesday. "Payment is not a technical detail of economic life, it's the last kilometer of economic sovereignty," Macron said in remarks to the Cartes Bancaires network gathering at the Carrousel du Louvre. He said controlling payment systems ensures "the security of our exchanges, the continuity of our economy, our capacity to decide for ourselves, and therefore our independence." Macron called the payment sector "strategic" and said it should be treated as such.

### Senate President Pressures Macron on Agricultural Pesticide Law

PARIS, France — Senate President Gérard Larcher sent French President Emmanuel Macron a favorable Council of State opinion on the "Duplomb 2" law, which would reintroduce limited exemptions to France's ban on two insecticides including acetamiprid, according to Le Point. Larcher transmitted the legal opinion before its official publication, with the emergency agricultural bill expected in the Council of Ministers on April 8. Macron had previously promised to defer to the high court's judgment on the controversial legislation that would allow restricted use of pesticides banned in France but authorized in Europe until 2033.
