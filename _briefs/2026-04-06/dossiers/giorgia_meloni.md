### Meloni extends fuel tax cuts amid economic pressures

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said Friday her government extended a 25-cent per liter fuel excise tax reduction until May 1, citing a "complex situation" caused by international tensions affecting energy costs. The announcement came as Italy's central bank cut growth forecasts to 0.6% this year and warned the country could enter recession under adverse scenarios. "The government will continue to monitor the evolution of the international scenario with the utmost attention and to work on every necessary intervention to protect families, businesses, and jobs," Meloni said on social media. The Bank of Italy attributed the economic challenges to weakening domestic demand and rising energy prices linked to Middle East conflicts.

### Meloni visits Gulf states amid energy security concerns

ROME, Italy — Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni visited Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the United Arab Emirates this week in an unannounced trip aimed at strengthening energy security amid regional tensions, a government official said. ### Italy's Meloni pushes electoral reform amid opposition warnings

ROME, Italy — Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni's ruling coalition began parliamentary discussions on electoral law changes that opposition parties said could boost her re-election chances, despite warnings against forcing the reform through without consultation. The proposal would install full proportional representation and guarantee a majority for any coalition winning more than 40% of the vote, according to lawmakers. Democratic Party lawmaker Simona Bonafe said Meloni had not learned from a recent referendum defeat and warned that "coercion nor top-down measures are acceptable" on electoral law. ### Italy signs teacher contract raising salaries for 850,000 educators

ROME, Italy — Italy signed a new contract that will increase salaries for 850,000 teachers by an average of 143 euros per month, Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said Tuesday.
