### Uruguay's Orsi to sign press freedom declarations

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi will meet Monday with a high-level delegation from the Inter-American Press Association to sign two declarations supporting press freedom, the organization said. The meeting will focus on signing the Chapultepec and Salta II declarations, which defend freedom of expression and press as pillars of democratic systems. The international mission aims to analyze Uruguay's media ecosystem firsthand and strengthen editorial principles in the region, according to the press association. The delegation includes representatives from Argentina, the United States and Ecuador.

### Frente Amplio leader questions low support for Orsi government

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Frente Amplio President Fernando Pereira said he struggles to understand why what he called "successful" administrations led by President Yamandú Orsi and Montevideo Mayor Mario Bergara lack broader support among party voters. Recent polls show 24% of Frente Amplio voters disapprove of Orsi's government performance, while 40% disapprove of Bergara's municipal administration, according to Cifra polling. ### Uruguay's Orsi invited to progressive platform launch in Spain

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi received an invitation from Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez to participate in the launch of a "Global Progressive Mobilization" platform in Barcelona on April 17, according to Uruguayan presidency sources cited by El País. The event will bring together Brazilian President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and Colombian President Gustavo Petro, among other center-left leaders. ### Cardinal says Pope Leon XIV likely to visit Uruguay

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Cardinal Daniel Sturla said Pope Leon XIV will "very probably" visit Uruguay between late November and early December as part of a tour that would also include Argentina and Peru. Sturla said the visit depends on various factors, including Peru's elections this year and the need to wait for a new government to be established there. ### Uruguay approves Solymar housing megaproject despite height concerns

CANELONES, Uruguay — The Broad Front coalition in Canelones will accelerate approval of a massive housing complex in Médanos de Solymar after Easter, reviving a project coordinated under former President Yamandú Orsi when he served as mayor, officials said. The National Housing Agency signed a framework agreement in February 2025 to sell the seven-hectare site for 21.3 million indexed units, equivalent to about $3.4 million, according to agency documents obtained by El Observador. The project calls for 21 buildings with 1,152 housing units, including towers up to 13 stories along Giannattasio Avenue, though some council members have raised concerns about the increased height exceptions. Architect Carlos Ponce de León said Orsi as mayor 'understood at that time that it could be viable' to allow greater height, though it would require later study by the Departmental Board.
