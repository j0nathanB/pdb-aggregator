### Brazil's Lula names Alckmin as 2026 running mate

BRASILIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva said Tuesday that Vice President Geraldo Alckmin will again serve as his running mate in Brazil's 2026 presidential election. Lula announced the decision at a cabinet meeting, ending months of speculation about whether Alckmin would leave the ticket to run for office in Sao Paulo state. The announcement came alongside a broader cabinet reshuffle as Brazilian law requires candidates holding certain executive posts to step down six months before elections. Alckmin, 73, previously served as Lula's centrist rival in 2006 before joining the leftist leader's successful 2022 ticket against then-President Jair Bolsonaro.

### Lula announces new education minister amid cabinet reshuffle

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva announced Janine Mello dos Santos as Brazil's new education minister as part of a sweeping cabinet reshuffle ahead of the 2026 elections, according to government sources. Santos, who previously served as executive secretary of the Education Ministry, replaces Macaé Evaristo, who stepped down to run for a seat in the Minas Gerais state legislature. At least 17 ministers left their posts by Saturday's deadline to comply with electoral law requiring officials to resign six months before elections if they plan to run for office. ### Lula vows to cancel Petrobras gas auction over pricing

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva said Thursday he will cancel a Petrobras liquefied petroleum gas auction that sold cooking gas to distributors at prices up to 100% higher than the state oil company's official rates. In an interview with TV Record Bahia, Lula called the auction "banditry" and said it was conducted against Petrobras management's wishes. "We will review this auction, we will cancel this auction, because poor people will not pay, under any circumstances, the price of this war," Lula said, referring to Middle East conflicts affecting international oil prices. The president also announced plans to publish a provisional measure creating diesel import subsidies with a discount of 1.20 reais per liter.

### Brazil's Lula signs law expanding paternity leave to 20 days

BRASILIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva signed legislation Tuesday expanding paternity leave in Brazil from the current five days to 20 days by 2029, according to government officials. The law implements a gradual increase, reaching 10 days in 2027, 15 days in 2028, and 20 days in 2029, with social security covering the costs instead of employers. The measure extends coverage to autonomous workers, domestic employees and individual microentrepreneurs beyond traditional employees, labor relations manager Rodrigo Marques of PG Advogados said.

### Brazil pays less than 1% of required congressional amendments

BRASÍLIA, Brazil — President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva's government has paid only 102.3 million reais ($17 million) of the 17.3 billion reais in congressional amendments required to be disbursed in the first half of 2024, according to data from the Ministry of Planning and Budget's Integrated Planning and Budget System. The payments represent less than 1% of mandatory amendments that must be paid by June under Brazil's budget law calendar, the report said. Congress approved a total of 49.9 billion reais in amendments for 2024, with 65% of individual and state delegation amendments designated for health and social assistance funds. The Liberal Party received the largest share of disbursed funds at 16.9 million reais, followed by Republicans and Social Democratic Party with 16.6 million reais each, the data showed.
