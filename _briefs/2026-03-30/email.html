THE MIDDLE POWERS MONITOR
Week of March 23, 2026
THIS WEEK
This week European leaders confronted escalating Middle Eastern tensions as Macron called on Iran to negotiate regional disputes while joining Meloni in condemning Israel's blocking of Palm Sunday mass in Jerusalem. Starmer convened emergency meetings on Iran's war economy as British borrowing costs surged, while Merz publicly disagreed with Washington over military strategy regarding Iran. In the Americas, Sheinbaum delivered 30,000 land titles to women farmers and proposed federal feminicide legislation, while Lula signed anti-crime measures targeting criminal factions. Canadian Prime Minister Carney achieved the NATO 2% defense spending target as his first budget received royal assent.

North America

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney achieved the NATO 2% defense spending target while his first budget received royal assent, marking early legislative success as he criticized Air Canada's CEO for English-only crash condolences and announced housing partnerships with the Aga Khan. Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum delivered 30,000 land titles to women farmers while proposing federal legislation establishing 40-70 year sentences for feminicide cases, seeking to ease Gulf naming tensions with the United States while pressuring the Supreme Court on transparency and ordering criminal investigations into oil spills.

South America

Brazilian President Lula da Silva signed anti-crime legislation targeting criminal factions and announced Chinese train factory investments while leading first-round polling scenarios but trailing in potential 2026 runoff matchups, as congressional panels rejected reports seeking his son's arrest and he signed land redistribution decrees for 478 families. Uruguayan President Yamandú Orsi accepted alternative transit plans without tunnel projects while presenting a delayed National Security Plan, facing criticism from farm groups over water restrictions as his approval rating dropped to 13th among Latin American presidents amid accusations over Spanish shipyard contract manipulation.

Europe

French President Emmanuel Macron intervened in Middle Eastern disputes by backing Jerusalem's Latin Patriarch blocked from Palm Sunday mass and calling on Iran to negotiate in good faith, while threatening to cancel joint tank development with Germany over fighter jet disagreements. British Prime Minister Keir Starmer convened emergency meetings on Iran's war economy while authorizing military forces to board Russian shadow fleet vessels, facing parliamentary pressure over transparency as he advanced cryptocurrency donation bans. German Chancellor Friedrich Merz publicly disagreed with the United States over Iran military strategy, refusing to join potential action and questioning US-Israel coordination effectiveness. Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni accepted Tourism Minister Santanche's resignation following legal pressure while suffering justice reform referendum defeat, traveling to Algeria for gas supply agreements amid Qatar disruptions. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy conducted diplomatic visits to Saudi Arabia and UAE offering drone defense expertise while reporting 3,000 Russian attack drones launched against Ukraine, positioning his nation as a regional security provider despite ongoing conflict.

Baltic States

Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda called for urgent air defense upgrades while warning against dialogue with Belarus's government, congratulating Belarusian opposition supporters on Freedom Day as he nominated Dainius Žalimas as Constitutional Court chief justice. Latvian Prime Minister Evika Siliņa faced intensified opposition pressure over alleged corruption bureau interference while focusing on energy independence and border security priorities, as politicians publicly questioned her political survival. Estonian Prime Minister Kristen Michal told parliament that Ukraine aid has evolved into a two-way exchange, with Estonia receiving technology and knowledge in return for military support, advocating for reframed understanding of Western assistance among NATO allies.
