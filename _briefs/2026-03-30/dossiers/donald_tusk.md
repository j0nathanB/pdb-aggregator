### Poland's Tusk announces fuel price cuts, maximum pricing controls

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk announced Thursday a package of measures to reduce fuel prices, including cutting VAT from 23% to 8% and reducing excise taxes by 29 groszy per liter of gasoline and 28 groszy per liter of diesel. The government said the changes, dubbed the "Fuel Prices Lower" package, would reduce prices by approximately 1.20 zloty per liter and include maximum retail price controls enforced by fines up to 1 million zloty. Opposition politicians from the Law and Justice party criticized Tusk for taking three weeks to implement similar proposals they had submitted in early March, with candidate Przemyslaw Czarnek saying the delay cost Polish drivers about 2 billion zloty.

### Polish PM warns of Middle East escalation amid fuel price measures

WARSAW, Poland — Prime Minister Donald Tusk said Wednesday that intelligence from allies indicates potential escalation in the Middle East conflict in coming days and weeks, prompting his government to implement emergency fuel price controls. Tusk told parliament that Poland received information suggesting the situation will worsen rather than stabilize, contradicting earlier expectations of a quick resolution to the Iran-Israel-U.S. conflict. The government approved a package reducing VAT and excise taxes on fuel and introducing maximum price caps at gas stations, with Tusk saying fuel prices could drop by about 1.2 zloty per liter.
