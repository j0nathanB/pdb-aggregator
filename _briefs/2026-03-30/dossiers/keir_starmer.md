### UK bans crypto donations to political parties

LONDON, Britain — Prime Minister Keir Starmer announced Wednesday that British political parties will be banned from accepting cryptocurrency donations, saying illicit finance poses a "stark" danger to the country's democracy. The government also said it will cap donations from British voters living abroad at 100,000 pounds ($134,000) annually. The measures follow a review by former senior civil servant Philip Rycroft, who expressed concern that untraceable digital currency donations could channel foreign money into Britain's political system. The changes must be approved by Parliament but will be backdated to take effect Wednesday, the government said.
