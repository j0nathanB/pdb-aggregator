## Top Stories

### Finland's Stubb says no military threat after drones crash

HELSINKI, Finland — President Alexander Stubb said Finland faces no military threat after two drones crashed in southeastern Finland on Sunday, with one identified as Ukrainian. The Finnish Air Force said one drone was confirmed as a Ukrainian AN196 model that crashed near Kouvola, while the second drone's origin remained unidentified, according to Air Force Commander Maj. Gen. Timo Herranen. Stubb said authorities responded immediately to the incident and continue monitoring the situation. The crashes occurred amid increased Ukrainian drone attacks on Russian oil facilities in the Baltic Sea region.

### Finland's Stubb hosts Nordic-Baltic security summit in Helsinki

HELSINKI, Finland — President Alexander Stubb hosted a Joint Expeditionary Force summit Thursday bringing together Nordic and Baltic leaders to discuss Ukraine support, Arctic security and hybrid threats. The meeting included British Prime Minister Keir Starmer, Lithuanian President Gitanas Nauseda, and prime ministers from Norway, Sweden, Latvia, Estonia, Iceland and the Netherlands, according to Finnish media reports. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney participated remotely. Stubb said leaders would also discuss Iran's situation during a dinner following the formal sessions.

### Finland's Stubb warns Iran war could trigger global recession

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb warned that Iran's war threatens to cause a "self-inflicted global recession" with economic consequences potentially worse than the coronavirus pandemic, according to an interview with Politico. Stubb said the conflict demonstrates how interconnected global supply chains and energy markets have become, stating "everything is connected to everything: oil prices to gas prices, food prices, fertilizer prices, medicine prices." The president criticized what he called the "transactional" approach to foreign policy, saying European nations should not get involved in Iran's war as long as fighting continues, though they could discuss participating in peacekeeping efforts.

## International

### Finland's Stubb warns Russia nearing general mobilization

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb said Thursday that Russia may be approaching a situation requiring general mobilization as Ukrainian forces kill more than 30,000 Russian soldiers monthly. Speaking at a press conference with Nordic leaders, Stubb said Russia has lost 500,000 soldiers for each percentage point of territory captured and cannot recruit enough troops to replace losses. Norwegian Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Støre said no negotiations between Russia and Ukraine have been scheduled despite discussions between Ukraine and the United States.

*Sources: (Ilta-Sanomat [1](https://www.is.fi/politiikka/art-2000011906868.html))*

### Stubb says Ukraine is winning war against Russia

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb said Ukraine is winning its war against Russia, marking a shift toward more positive messaging about the conflict. Lithuanian President Gitanas Nauseda agreed with Stubb's assessment during a Joint Expeditionary Force meeting Thursday, saying Ukraine's resistance has not broken despite Russia's limited territorial gains. British Prime Minister Keir Starmer echoed the sentiment, stating "Russia is not winning, they will not win" after nearly four years of conflict.

*Sources: (Ilta-Sanomat [1](https://www.is.fi/politiikka/art-2000011905091.html))*

### Finnish President Stubb conducts diplomatic talks during London jog

LONDON, Britain — Finnish President Alexander Stubb met with Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney during a joint run in Hyde Park last week, according to Finland's presidential office. The presidential office published a video of the Hyde Park run on social media.
