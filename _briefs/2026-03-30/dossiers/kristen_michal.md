## Top Stories

### Estonian PM says Ukraine aid becoming two-way exchange

HELSINKI, Finland — Estonian Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Ukraine is now providing technology and knowledge back to the world in exchange for military and financial aid. Speaking after a Joint Expeditionary Force meeting, Michal said Ukraine is sharing expertise on modern electronic warfare methods, making assistance "no longer just a one-way street." The meeting also included Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney via video link, with discussions on Arctic security and NATO presence. Michal said cooperation with like-minded countries strengthens the JEF alliance.

### Estonian PM Michal addresses parliament on security, economy

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal participated in a parliamentary question session alongside Energy and Environment Minister Andres Sutt and Justice and Digital Minister Liisa Pakosta, according to parliamentary records. Michal answered lawmakers' questions on security, fuel and food prices, farmers' concerns, cost of living, Estonia's economy, independent economic studies and electricity prices. The session was part of the Riigikogu's regular information hour format.
