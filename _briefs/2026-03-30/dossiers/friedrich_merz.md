### German Chancellor Merz doubts US-Israel Iran war strategy

BERLIN, Germany — Chancellor Friedrich Merz said Friday he has doubts about whether the United States and Israel have a clear strategy to end the war in Iran, though Germany would be ready to help stabilize the region after hostilities end. Since the U.S. and Israel attacked Iran on February 28, Iran has launched strikes against Israel, U.S. bases and Gulf states while effectively blocking Middle East fuel exports via the Strait of Hormuz. Merz said at a conference hosted by the FAZ newspaper that he is not convinced current actions by Israel and America will lead to success, adding that Germany is trying to influence Israel with limited success.
