### Italian Tourism Minister Santanche Resigns After Meloni Pressure

ROME, Italy — Italian Tourism Minister Daniela Santanche resigned Wednesday after Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni publicly demanded her departure following a bruising referendum defeat. Santanche, who faces trial on false accounting charges and is under investigation for alleged benefit fraud, initially resisted Meloni's call to quit before relenting in a letter stating she was leaving "only in response to a request from the leader of my party." The resignation came as Meloni seeks to reset her government after voters rejected her judicial reforms in a March 22-23 referendum, with the prime minister reportedly saying she was "no longer prepared to cover for discredited allies." Santanche was the third government figure to leave following the referendum defeat.

### Meloni travels to Algeria seeking gas supplies amid Qatar disruptions

ALGIERS, Algeria — Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni traveled to Algeria on Wednesday as Rome seeks to secure alternative gas supplies amid prolonged disruptions in deliveries from Qatar, her office said. Energy Minister Gilberto Pichetto Fratin said Friday that Iranian strikes on Qatar appeared to have halted its exports for an extended period, prompting Italy to seek suppliers from Algeria, the United States and Azerbaijan. Algeria now covers around 30% of Italy's annual gas consumption after Rome moved to replace Russian gas following Moscow's invasion of Ukraine in 2022. Qatar previously covered around 10% of Italy's consumption but told Italian customer Edison it would not be able to fulfill contractual obligations for April after Iranian attacks on gas facilities.
