### Moldova's Sandu makes first official visit to Slovakia

BRATISLAVA, Slovakia — Moldovan President Maia Sandu arrived in Slovakia on Tuesday for the first official visit by a Moldovan head of state to the country, the Moldovan presidency said. Sandu met with Slovak President Peter Pellegrini to discuss strengthening bilateral relations and Slovakia's support for Moldova's European Union membership bid, according to the presidency. The leaders witnessed the signing of a bilateral social protection agreement and held a joint press conference. ### Moldova's Sandu tells judges justice reform serves citizens first

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu told judges at their general assembly that Moldova's justice system reforms are primarily intended to serve citizens rather than meet European Union requirements. "We are not doing justice reform because the EU or the commissioner asks us to, but for the people," Sandu said, according to the presidential office. She met separately with European Commissioner for Justice Michael McGrath to discuss accelerating reforms needed for EU accession chapters on judicial systems and fundamental rights. Sandu said justice reform remains the most important reform for Moldova's European integration process.

### Moldova's Sandu backs Ukraine volunteer coalition, Transnistria plan

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said Saturday that Moldova has expressed interest in joining the Coalition of Volunteers supporting Ukraine and endorsed government proposals for international administration of the breakaway Transnistria region. ### Moldova's Sandu marks 108th anniversary of Bessarabia-Romania union

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said Moldova's 1918 union with Romania was a "vital return to family" as the country marked the 108th anniversary of Bessarabia's incorporation into Romania on Thursday. "Who knows what our fate would be today if that historic act had not been accomplished," Sandu said in a statement. Parliament Speaker Igor Grosu called the union "an act of national will," while Prime Minister Alexandru Munteanu said Romania remains Moldova's "most steadfast and reliable partner." A recent poll showed 42.3% of Moldovans support reunification with Romania, up from 32.4% in September.

### Sandu calls Vartic death failure of ruling party team

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said the death of Ludmila Vartic represents "a failure of the PAS team from Hincesti," marking her first public comments on the case that has gripped the country. Sandu said during a March 23 press conference that she finds it hard to believe no one from the ruling party knew about Vartic's situation before her March 3 death. Vartic, a kindergarten teacher and mother of two, died after falling from an 11th-floor building in the capital, with police treating the case as suicide amid allegations her husband, former Hincesti vice-president Dumitru Vartic, subjected her to domestic violence. Sandu urged women facing domestic violence to seek help and said there is no interest at the national level to cover up the case, despite the suspect's former membership in the governing party.

### Moldova's Sandu blames Russia for power line disconnection

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu said Tuesday that Russia bears full responsibility for the disconnection of the Isaccea-Vulcanesti power line following overnight attacks on Ukraine's energy infrastructure. Sandu called the Russian strikes on civilian energy infrastructure "war crimes" and said the attacks disconnected Moldova's main electrical interconnection line with Europe, according to her statement on X. Prime Minister Alexandru Munteanu confirmed the Isaccea-Vulcanesti line remains disconnected due to Russian attacks on southern Ukraine's energy infrastructure, though he said alternative routes exist. Moldova's Energy Ministry said it activated four 110kV interconnection lines with Romania to maintain the country's electricity supply.
