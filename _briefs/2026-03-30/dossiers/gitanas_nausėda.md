### Lithuanian president calls for urgent air defense upgrades

HELSINKI, Finland — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda called for urgent completion of air defense upgrades Thursday, citing hybrid and drone attacks as integral elements of Russia's war against Ukraine. Nausėda made the remarks at the Joint Expeditionary Force summit in Helsinki, days after a drone came down near Lake Lavysas in Lithuania's Varėna district. "We must transition from the somewhat pacifist air policing model, which was acceptable in peacetime, to air defense under current circumstances," he said. The drones that entered Baltic airspace Wednesday night were Ukrainian and intended for targets in Russia but believed to have veered off course, officials said.

### Lithuanian president warns against dialogue with Belarus regime

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said dialogue with Belarus is only possible if Minsk demonstrates goodwill, but Lithuania sees no such signs currently. Nausėda told U.S. Special Envoy to Belarus John Coale that Americans should treat Belarus actions carefully because the Belarusian government cannot be separated from Russia due to its alignment with Moscow, according to the president's office. The Lithuanian leader said Belarus might attempt to divide NATO unity and create differences between U.S. and European Union policies toward Belarus. Nausėda welcomed the recent release of 15-20 Belarusian political prisoners but noted that other people continue to be imprisoned.

## International

### Lithuanian President Congratulates Belarusians on Freedom Day

VILNIUS, Lithuania — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda congratulated Belarusians observing Freedom Day on Wednesday, expressing support for their pursuit of democracy. "This day symbolizes your perseverance and determination to create a free, democratic and pluralistic Belarus," Nausėda said in a statement released by his office. The Lithuanian leader said his country will continue to firmly support Belarusians' efforts to return to rule of law, end repressions and release all political prisoners. Nausėda expressed hope that despite current challenges, a democratic Belarus will find its deserved place in the European family of nations.

### Lithuanian President calls for critical thinking amid geopolitical changes

TARTU, Estonia — Lithuanian President Gitanas Nausėda said Friday that current global changes require critical thinking, intellectual flexibility and the ability to operate under unpredictable conditions. Speaking to faculty and students at the Baltic Defence College, Nausėda said the rules-based world order is in danger and power politics is accelerating. He cited Russia's war against Ukraine, China's challenge to the existing world order, malicious hybrid actions against states and societies, and growing terrorism as examples requiring innovative approaches beyond traditional models.

### Lithuania President Nominates Davulis for Constitutional Court Chief

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda signed a decree Wednesday nominating Tomas Davulis to serve as chief justice of Lithuania's Constitutional Court, the presidential office said. The nomination follows constitutional requirements that the president propose a chief justice candidate from among sitting court justices after parliament appoints all members. Davulis, appointed as a Constitutional Court justice in March 2023, previously served as dean of Vilnius University's Law Faculty and has 28 years of legal and academic experience, according to the presidential office.
