## International

### Macron backs Jerusalem patriarch blocked from Palm Sunday mass

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron said Sunday he gives his "full support" to the Latin Patriarch of Jerusalem after Israeli police prevented the cardinal from celebrating Palm Sunday mass at the Church of the Holy Sepulchre. Macron condemned the Israeli police decision and called it part of a "worrying multiplication of violations of the status of Jerusalem's Holy Sites," according to a statement from the French presidency. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said the restriction was imposed for "security" reasons with "absolutely no malicious intent," his office stated on social media. The Latin Patriarchate said Cardinal Pierbattista Pizzaballa and the custodian of the Holy Sepulchre were stopped while traveling privately and forced to turn back, marking "the first time in centuries" church leaders were prevented from celebrating Palm Sunday mass at the site.

### Macron Calls on Iran to Negotiate in Good Faith

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron called on Iran to engage in good faith negotiations aimed at de-escalating the Middle East conflict, following a phone conversation with Iranian President Massoud Pezechkian. Macron said he urged Iran to provide a framework addressing international concerns about Iran's nuclear and ballistic programs as well as regional destabilization activities, according to a statement posted on social media. The French leader also spoke with Israeli President Isaac Herzog, expressing France's solidarity while urging direct talks between Israel and Lebanon. Macron convened a defense and security council meeting Tuesday evening to address the ongoing Middle East crisis.

### Macron threatens to cancel tank project over fighter jet dispute

PARIS, France — French President Emmanuel Macron said he would consider canceling a joint Franco-German tank development project if Germany abandons a separate fighter aircraft program, according to statements reported by Les Échos. Macron said in February that if the German partner questioned the joint aircraft project, France would be forced to question the joint tank project. The president announced Thursday he would attempt a final mediation between Airbus and Dassault with Chancellor Friedrich Merz regarding the fighter jet dispute.

### French Magazine Reports Fake Russian Spy Cables Target Macron

PARIS, France — French magazine Le Point said it obtained fabricated intelligence reports designed to destabilize France and President Emmanuel Macron, allegedly produced by fake Russian agents. The documents, signed with code names like "Felix" and "Frank" and addressed to a handler in Moscow, contain false claims that Macron is planning coups in Africa and France will deliver nuclear weapons to Ukraine, according to Le Point. The magazine said the fake cables were written in recent weeks and attributed to supposed Russian Foreign Intelligence Service operatives stationed abroad.
