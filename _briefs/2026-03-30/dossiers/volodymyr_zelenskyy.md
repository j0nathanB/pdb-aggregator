### Zelenskyy says Russia launched 3,000 attack drones this week

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia launched more than 3,000 attack drones against Ukraine over the past week, many of them Shahed-type UAVs, according to his weekly report posted on social media. KYIV, Ukraine — Ukraine is using long-range strikes on Russian energy infrastructure to maintain pressure on Moscow after international oil sanctions were eased, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Wednesday. ### Zelenskyy leads Ukraine presidential poll, Zaluzhnyi second

KYIV, Ukraine — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy would receive 22.6% support if presidential elections were held next Sunday, followed by Ukraine's ambassador to the UK Valerii Zaluzhnyi at 19.3%, according to a poll conducted by SOCIS from March 12-18.
