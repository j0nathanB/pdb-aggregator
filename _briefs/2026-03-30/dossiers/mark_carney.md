## Top Stories

### Carney criticizes Air Canada CEO over English-only crash condolences

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney on Wednesday criticized Air Canada CEO Michael Rousseau for delivering condolences after a fatal crash only in English, calling it a lack of judgment and compassion. Carney said Rousseau showed poor judgment by not speaking in French, Canada's second official language, when expressing condolences after Sunday's crash at New York's LaGuardia Airport that killed both pilots. Parliament's language committee voted Tuesday to summon Rousseau to Ottawa to discuss the matter. Air Canada said Rousseau used English because his French skills were insufficient to convey such a sensitive message effectively.

### Canada reaches NATO 2% defense spending target, Carney announces

HALIFAX, Nova Scotia — Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that Canada has achieved NATO's 2% defense spending target, half a decade ahead of the previous government's schedule. Carney said the government spent more than $63 billion over 10 months, representing the largest year-over-year increase to Canada's defense spending in generations. The prime minister also announced more than $3 billion in infrastructure and defense-related investments across Atlantic Canada, including $1.2 billion to modernize critical infrastructure at CFB Halifax Dockyard and Stadacona. Canada's defense spending had fallen to just 1% of GDP by 2014, according to the Prime Minister's Office.

### Carney announces housing partnership with Aga Khan during Canada visit

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney met with His Highness Prince Rahim Aga Khan V during the Ismaili leader's first official visit to Canada from March 25 to 31, 2026, the Prime Minister's Office said. The leaders announced new partnerships including Ismaili Imamat investments in multi-generational not-for-profit housing projects across Canada to address the affordable housing crisis, according to a joint declaration. Carney and the Aga Khan also created an Economic Partnership Platform linking FinDev Canada with the Aga Khan Fund for Economic Development to collaborate on infrastructure, renewable energy and agriculture projects in sub-Saharan Africa and Asia Pacific regions. The Prime Minister named Ambassador David Lametti as the new Representative of Canada to the Ismaili Imamat to advance the collaboration.

## Domestic

### Carney's first budget bill receives royal assent

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney's first budget implementation bill received royal assent Thursday evening after the Senate gave final approval to the legislation. Bill C-15 implements measures from the Liberal government's 2025 budget, which projected a deficit of 78.3 billion Canadian dollars for the current fiscal year. The budget establishes a new fiscal framework that would limit federal borrowing to new capital investments while reducing current program spending and public service staffing levels.
