# Global allowlist — cross-country trusted sources.

# When MPM_DOMAIN_ALLOWLIST=1 is set, every country's effective allowlist =
# union of (this file) + (that country's `$boost` entries in {code}.goggle).

# brave.search_news, before they reach story_map.

# subdomain (after stripping `www.`) — e.g., `bbc.com` also matches
# `news.bbc.com`.
