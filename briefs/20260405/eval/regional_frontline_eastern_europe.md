Ukrainian drones are entering allied airspace. Finland increased surveillance after Ukrainian drones crossed over, Estonia has handled crossings during Russian attacks, and Latvia triggered emergency alerts as Russian aircraft approached. The war is spreading beyond these crossings: Western allies are now blocking Ukraine's refinery attacks as the Iran war drives up global energy prices, making Kyiv more dependent on Western fuel.

Poland's effort to restore democracy has hit a crisis. Andrzej Nawrocki, the president, refuses to swear in four of six judges elected by parliament. The standoff creates deadlock between the presidency and the restored democratic majority, with Mr Nawrocki's defiance threatening to derail the entire repair effort. This crisis mirrors tensions across the region, where Petr Pavel, the Czech president, has been excluded from his country's NATO summit delegation and Lithuania's prime minister and president are clashing over European Council representation.

The Middle East conflict is hurting regional economies. Central banks from Estonia to Romania are cutting growth forecasts and warning of higher inflation as energy price swings from the Iran war spread. Governments are stepping into fuel markets directly — the Czech Republic has imposed emergency measures, Lithuania is subsidising fuel prices, and Romania's central bank has warned of economic risks. The speed of the impact shows how quickly global energy shocks can reach the region despite years of EU efforts to diversify supply.

## Gap Paragraphs

Coordination failed despite shared pressures across the alliance. The EU produced no joint response to drone spillover affecting multiple member states, while NATO offered no coordination on burden-sharing despite identical pressure from the Trump administration.
