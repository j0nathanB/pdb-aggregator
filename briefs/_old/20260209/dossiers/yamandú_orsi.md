### Uruguay moves toward canceling Spanish shipyard contract

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi's government is moving toward finalizing the cancellation of a contract with Spanish shipyard Cardama for two oceanic patrol vessels worth 82.5 million euros, according to El País newspaper. The government has until Feb. 15 to open a second letter of credit or risk breaching the contract, the report said. A recent audit by Belgian certification company Bureau Veritas found the construction quality of the ships "satisfactory," despite the government's efforts to document alleged breaches by the Spanish company. Uruguay has paid 28.8 million euros of the total contract value since December 2024.
