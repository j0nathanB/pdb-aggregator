### Finland Spent 264,000 Euros on Brazil Climate Summit

HELSINKI, Finland — Finnish President Alexander Stubb and 30 other officials spent approximately 264,000 euros in taxpayer funds attending the UN climate summit in Belém, Brazil, in November, according to travel invoices obtained by Finnish tabloid Ilta-Sanomat. Stubb's flights alone cost 7,304 euros for his November 4-8 trip, while Climate and Environment Minister Sari Multala's travel expenses totaled 6,533 euros.
