### Moldova President Meets Baltic Parliament Leaders on EU Integration

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu met with the parliamentary speakers of Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania to discuss Moldova's European Union accession process, the presidency said. The meeting with Estonian Riigikogu President Lauri Hussar, Latvian Saeima President Daiga Mieriņa and Lithuanian Seimas President Juozas Olekas marked the first joint official visit by all three Baltic legislative leaders to Moldova. Sandu cited economic data showing the Baltic states' average wages increased 4-7 times and GDP per capita grew 2.5-4 times following EU membership. The leaders also discussed regional security and hybrid warfare threats, including cyberattacks and disinformation campaigns.

### Moldova Foreign Minister Open to Creative EU Accession Ideas

CHISINAU, Moldova — Foreign Minister Mihai Popșoi said Moldova is open to "creative ideas" that could accelerate the country's European Union accession process, even if it initially means joining without full membership rights. Popșoi told Euroactiv that achieving tangible progress before the 2028 presidential elections is essential for maintaining public support. His comments contrast with President Maia Sandu's previous statements that Moldova advocates for EU accession with full rights and "does not ask for shortcuts" in the process. Moldova became an EU candidate country in June 2022 and began accession negotiations in 2024.
