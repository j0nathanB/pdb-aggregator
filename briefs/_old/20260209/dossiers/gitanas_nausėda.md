### Lithuania President Says China Relations Need Mutual Will

VILNIUS, Lithuania — President Gitanas Nausėda said restoring relations with China requires the will of both states, but Lithuania sees threats in too close cooperation with the communist regime, according to a BNS report Friday. His comments came as China urged Lithuania to correct what Beijing calls mistakes in developing relations with Taiwan. Prime Minister Ingrida Ruginienė said this week that Lithuania "jumped in front of a train and lost" by opening a Taiwanese Representative Office in Vilnius without coordinating with the EU and US. Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Lin Jian said China's doors for communication remain open but Lithuania must "turn its desire to improve bilateral relations into real actions."
