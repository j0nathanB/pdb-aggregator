## International

### Estonia closes nighttime traffic at Russian border crossings

TALLINN, Estonia — Estonia will close nighttime traffic at two major road border crossings with Russia for three months starting February 24, Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Tuesday. The government cited ongoing border incidents originating from Russia as the reason for closing the Luhamaa and Koidula crossings during nighttime hours while keeping them open for 12 hours during the day. "Since Russia behaves somewhat irrationally at the border, we need to free up resources for more effective border surveillance," Michal said. Interior Minister Igor Taro said the measure will allow officials to be deployed more effectively where needed most.
