### Latvia opposition seeks no-confidence vote against PM Siliņa

RIGA, Latvia — Opposition lawmakers in Latvia's parliament initiated a motion of no confidence against Prime Minister Evika Siliņa, with a vote expected next week after failing to secure enough support Thursday. The opposition "Latvia First" party cited concerns over migrant reception requirements, oversight of the "Rail Baltica" project, and economic indicators as reasons for the dismissal motion, according to parliamentary proceedings. Forty-six deputies opposed Siliņa's dismissal while 34 did not vote, with one abstention. "New Unity" faction deputy Gatis Liepiņš dismissed the motion as attention-seeking, citing Latvia's stable credit rating and wage growth statistics.
