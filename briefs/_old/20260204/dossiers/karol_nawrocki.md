### Polish President Calls Security Council Meeting on Parliament Speaker

WARSAW, Poland — President Karol Nawrocki called a National Security Council meeting for Feb. 11 to examine alleged business contacts between Parliament Speaker Włodzimierz Czarzasty's wife and a Russian citizen, presidential spokesman Rafał Leśkiewicz said Tuesday. Deputy Parliament Speaker Piotr Zgorzelski criticized the agenda as "embarrassing" and urged Nawrocki to remove the item, calling it "disproportionate" to media reports about Czarzasty's wife's art market dealings with a Russian national allegedly linked to Russian state-controlled financial institutions. The Parliament Chancellery said Czarzasty receives full counterintelligence protection and access to classified information, adding that security services would have acted if any threat existed. Czarzasty has rejected the allegations, saying no formal charges or investigations exist and the matter is purely political.

### Polish President Nawrocki pardons three convicted individuals

WARSAW, Poland — President Karol Nawrocki pardoned three convicted individuals while rejecting five other clemency requests, the Presidential Chancellery said in a statement. Nawrocki reviewed eight clemency applications submitted by Prosecutor General Waldemar Żurek, according to the chancellery. The pardoned individuals included a person convicted of causing a fatal traffic accident, another convicted of economic crimes, and a third convicted of criminal threats, the statement said. The president cited humanitarian reasons, including poor health conditions and advanced age of the convicted persons, as primary factors in his decisions, according to the chancellery.

### Polish President Nawrocki leads trust poll, survey shows

WARSAW, Poland — Polish President Karol Nawrocki topped a new trust ranking with 52% approval, followed by Deputy Prime Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz at 46% and Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski at 45%, according to a CBOS poll released this week. The survey, conducted Jan. 8-20 among 938 adult Polish residents, showed Warsaw Mayor Rafał Trzaskowski at 42% trust and Prime Minister Donald Tusk at 41%. Former Prime Minister Jarosław Kaczyński, leader of the opposition Law and Justice party, ranked 15th with 26% trust and 56% distrust, the polling center said.
