### Uruguay delays universal school bonus amid budget constraints

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — President Yamandú Orsi's administration will delay implementing a universal $2,500 school bonus for all primary students, citing budget management problems, education officials said. The National Public Education Administration said the bonus will be distributed gradually, covering students in income quintiles 1-3 in 2026, with remaining quintiles added in 2027 and 2028. Orsi had promised during presidential debates to provide the bonus to "all" primary students starting classes, but interim education council president Elbia Pereira attributed the scaled rollout to "budget management problems." The bonus payments begin March 3, after the school year starts rather than before as originally planned.

MONTEVIDEO, Uruguay — Opposition National Party leader Álvaro Delgado criticized President Yamandú Orsi's administration Saturday, saying it causes him "great sadness that a government dedicates itself to dismantling what the previous one did."
