### Carney tells Trump Canada paid for Gordie Howe bridge

### Canada scraps EV sales mandate, boosts purchase incentives

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney said Wednesday his government was scrapping Canada's electric vehicle sales mandate while launching a C$2.3 billion program offering up to C$5,000 incentives for EV purchases. Carney said the new approach replaces mandatory sales targets with stronger emissions standards for 2027-2032 model years, aiming for 75% EV sales by 2035. The government also announced C$1.5 billion for charging infrastructure and C$3.1 billion to help automakers transition to electric vehicles. Environmental groups criticized the move as a setback, while industry associations praised the shift from mandates to incentives.

### Canada eliminates Islamophobia envoy post, creates advisory committee

OTTAWA, Canada — Prime Minister Mark Carney's government eliminated the position of Canada's special representative for combating Islamophobia, held by Amira Elghawaby, and the vacant post of special envoy for Holocaust remembrance and combating antisemitism, replacing both with an advisory committee. "This is not a question of anyone being too controversial or not vocal enough on an issue, but I think both representatives played their role," Canadian Identity and Culture Minister Marc Miller said at a press conference Tuesday. The new advisory committee will focus on "promoting social cohesion, bringing people together around a common identity, and fighting racism and hatred in all its forms," according to a ministry statement. Elghawaby's 2023 appointment had sparked controversy over past statements suggesting most Quebecers seemed "influenced by anti-Muslim sentiment" regarding Quebec's secularism law.
