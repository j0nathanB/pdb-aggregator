### Estonian PM says president's remarks contradict national values

TALLINN, Estonia — Prime Minister Kristen Michal said Thursday that President Alar Karis's recent statements were "unsuccessful" and contradicted Estonia's fundamental principles regarding Ukraine policy. Michal told a government press conference that Estonia's foreign policy toward Ukraine and Russia remains unchanged despite the president's comments. "Estonia's foreign policy has not changed," Michal said, adding that the country continues to support Ukraine and does not make concessions to Russia. The prime minister said he maintains good relations with Karis but expressed sadness at having to comment on the president's statements.
