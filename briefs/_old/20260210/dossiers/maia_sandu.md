### Moldova's Sandu announces consultations on children's online safety

CHISINAU, Moldova — President Maia Sandu announced consultations with parents, teachers, doctors, psychologists and youth representatives to develop solutions for protecting children in the digital environment, marking International Internet Safety Day.
