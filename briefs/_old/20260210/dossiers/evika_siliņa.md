### Latvia opposition disrupts parliament vote on PM confidence

RIGA, Latvia — Opposition lawmakers disrupted a parliamentary vote of no confidence against Prime Minister Evika Siliņa by deliberately breaking quorum, forcing postponement of the proceedings until next week. The opposition party "Latvia First" had called for Siliņa's dismissal over migration policies, the Rail Baltica project, and economic performance, marking the sixth such attempt since she took office in 2023. While 46 lawmakers voted against the no-confidence motion and one abstained, 34 opposition deputies refused to vote, leaving the session without the required 50-member quorum. Parliament Speaker Daiga Mieriņa suspended the session indefinitely, saying the timing for resuming deliberations would be decided by the parliamentary presidium.

### Fact-checkers question Latvia PM's foreign investment claims

RIGA, Latvia — Fact-checkers questioned Prime Minister Evika Siliņa's claim that foreign investment attraction doubled in 2025, finding the actual increase was 1.54 times rather than twofold. Re:Baltica's "Re:Check" project said Latvia's Investment Development Agency reported attracting 1.01 billion euros in 2025 compared to 655 million euros in 2024. The agency clarified that "attracted investments" represent potential projects rather than completed investments, with only about one-fourth of previously announced attractions materializing into actual investments between 2021-2024.
