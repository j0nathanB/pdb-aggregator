### Latvia Opposition Breaks Quorum on Own No-Confidence Vote

RIGA, Latvia — Opposition lawmakers in Latvia's parliament deliberately broke the voting quorum Thursday on their own motion to dismiss Prime Minister Evika Silina, preventing the sixth no-confidence vote against her from proceeding. The opposition coalition "Latvia First" had requested Silina's removal over migration policy, the Rail Baltica project oversight, and economic indicators, but 34 opposition deputies abstained from voting after heated debates. ### Latvia PM to attend Milan Winter Olympics opening ceremonies

RIGA, Latvia — Prime Minister Evika Siliņa will travel to Italy Feb. 6-8 to attend the opening ceremonies of the XXV Winter Olympic Games in Milan and meet with Latvian Olympic athletes, the Cabinet of Ministers said.
