# Week of January 27, 2026

## Top Stories

### Kremlin says Russia has invited Ukraine's Zelenskiy to come to Moscow for peace talks

On January 29, the Kremlin reiterated its invitation for Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy to come to Moscow for peace talks. On January 30, Zelenskyy rejected this invitation and counter-invited Putin to come to Kyiv instead. Zelenskyy also indicated that the next round of U.S.-mediated peace talks scheduled for February 1 in Abu Dhabi may be postponed due to U.S.-Iran tensions.

### Zelenskiy says Russia largely observing energy ceasefire ahead of peace talks

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy initially stated on February 2 that Russia had largely observed a ceasefire on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, with no targeted strikes in the previous 24 hours. However, the following day Russia launched a major attack with hundreds of drones and 32 ballistic missiles targeting the power grid, which Zelenskyy said violated the commitment to halt energy infrastructure strikes.

*Sources: (Reuters [1](https://www.reuters.com/world/europe/no-new-targeted-russian-strikes-ukrainian-energy-infrastructure-zelenskiy-says-2026-02-02/); AP News [1](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-missile-drone-attack-electricity-c10dbc6b621e196606fc79caab0eaad5))*

### Russian drones kill 3 and wound children in Ukraine as Zelenskyy urges speedier diplomacy

Russian drone strikes killed at least 15 people across Ukraine, including attacks on a bus carrying mineworkers in Dnipro that killed 12, and strikes in Odesa that killed 3 and wounded 23 including children. Zelenskyy announced peace talks with Russia and the U.S. scheduled for February 4-5 in Abu Dhabi, later stating talks were penciled in for February 1 and calling for acceleration of diplomatic efforts.

### Zelensky believes return of Ukrainians can help army and entire country

President Volodymyr Zelensky gave an interview to Czech Radio where he stated that the return of mobilization-age Ukrainians from abroad would help Ukraine's army and the country. He specifically mentioned young Ukrainians in the Czech Republic who could return to Ukraine to help the army.

*Sources: (Ukrinform [1](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/4086476-zelensky-believes-return-of-ukrainians-can-help-army-and-entire-country.html))*

### Zelensky names areas most affected by energy system accident

On January 31, a technological accident occurred in the energy systems of Romania, Moldova, and Ukraine, causing simultaneous power grid shutdowns. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy stated that Kyiv and central Ukraine, along with Chernihiv, Kharkiv, Vinnytsia, and Sumy regions, faced the most severe consequences. He reported that 3,419 multi-story residential buildings in Kyiv were left without heating, and that weather conditions causing icing of power lines and automatic shutdowns were the likely cause rather than external interference or cyberattacks.

*Sources: (Ukrinform [1](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-economy/4086469-zelensky-names-areas-most-affected-by-energy-system-accident.html))*

### Zelensky: We are not ready for compromises that lead to violation of territorial integrity

President Volodymyr Zelensky stated during a conversation with journalists that Ukraine is not ready for compromises that violate territorial integrity, specifically regarding the Donetsk region. He said no compromise has been reached on territorial issues and that such matters must be resolved at the leadership level. Zelensky referenced trilateral negotiations in Abu Dhabi where the American side proposed a free economic zone solution.

*Sources: (Ukrinform [1](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-polytics/4085937-zelensky-we-are-not-ready-for-compromises-that-lead-to-violation-of-territorial-integrity.html))*
